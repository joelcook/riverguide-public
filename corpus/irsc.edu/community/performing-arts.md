# **MCALPIN THEATER**

### 2026–2027 Season: Live & Local

Live music. Stand-up comedy. Community collaborations. Unforgettable performances.

The McAlpin Theater at Indian River State College brings artists and audiences together for live entertainment on the Treasure Coast. Our new Live & Local season celebrates the performers, partnerships, and experiences that make our community extraordinary.

Tickets for the 2026–2027 season go on sale September 29.

## Live & Local: 2026–2027 Season

From laugh-out-loud comedy to live symphonic music, the new season brings an exciting mix of entertainment to the McAlpin Theater stage.

###

**Stand-Up Comedy**
**November 7 | 7 p.m.**

Get ready for an unforgettable night of stand-up, takedowns, and wry relatability that proves no matter where we are in life, we’re all in on the same joke.

The McAlpin Theater, in partnership with Casey n Spaz Comedy, presents **Dad Jokes**, featuring Tom Briscoe, seen on *Dry Bar Comedy*, and Richie Minervini, whose film and television credits include *The King of Queens, Paul Blart: Mall Cop, Grown Ups,* and *The Rosie Show*.

Roll with the punches—and the laughter—in this hilarious night of comedy.

###

**Instrumental Concert**
**November 13 | 7 p.m.**
**November 14 | 2 p.m.**

The McAlpin Theater and the Port St. Lucie Community Band come together for a stirring salute to our nation’s veterans.

Celebrate the service of our nation’s heroes through the power of live symphonic music. Experience an uplifting concert honoring their courage and legacy, enhanced by stunning visual effects.

###

Instrumental Concert
December 11 | 7 p.m.
December 12 | 2 p.m.

Start the season with music, memories, and holiday cheer.

This festive co-production from the McAlpin Theater and the Port St. Lucie Community Band features an instrumental concert of seasonal favorites along with a newly expanded audience sing-along.

Bring family and friends and make **Sing In the Holidays** part of your holiday tradition.

###

Concert
**January 30 | 7 p.m.**

The Queen of Retro Pop has arrived.

Florida-born singer-songwriter **Tabitha Meeks** brings a feel-good throwback sound that blends vintage charm with modern flair. Described as “Norah Jones meets Nancy Sinatra,” Meeks delivers catchy, lighthearted songs designed to have you bopping your head, tapping your toes, and smiling long after the final note.

###

**Stand-Up Comedy**
**February 6 | 7 p.m.**

Get your giggles on with the ladies of laughter.

Casey n Spaz Comedy returns to the McAlpin Theater for an evening featuring some of the sharpest and funniest female voices in stand-up.

Whether it’s girls’ night, date night, family fun, or you simply need a good laugh, **Ladies Night** brings bold comedy and brilliant storytelling to the McAlpin stage.

###

Instrumental Concert
**February 19 | 7 p.m.**
**February 20 | 2 p.m.**

Fall in love with live music.

The McAlpin Theater and Port St. Lucie Community Band come together for a romantic symphonic concert blending enchanting melodies with breathtaking visuals.

Whether you’re hearing a longtime favorite or discovering something captivatingly new, celebrate the timeless love of music at the McAlpin Theater.

###

Concert
**February 27 | 7 p.m.**

Experience the violin like you’ve never heard it before.

Award-winning South Florida duo **Sons of Mystro** blend classical technique with reggae, R&B, hip-hop, and pop for an electrifying musical experience.

Mentored by Black Violin and performing with a DJ and drummer, the duo has captivated audiences across the country. Now they’re bringing their high-energy sound to the McAlpin Theater.
