# Hallstrom Planetarium

---

Step into the universe at Hallstrom Planetarium. Housed at Indian River State College, this 40-foot dome theatre uses immersive digital projection and a state-of-the-art Spitz 512 system to bring the cosmos to life.

Travel through space, witness celestial events, and see the stars as they appear from Earth in the past, present, or future.

## More ways to explore

### Planetarium Gift Shop

Bring home something out of this world from the Hallstrom Planetarium Gift Shop! Your one-stop-shop for cool stuff – located just outside the planetarium entrance and open before and after most public events.

### Field trips & special group presentations

Curriculum-based planetarium shows are available to K-12 public, private and homeschool classes on weekday mornings from October through April. Community groups can also book the planetarium theatre for any of our topical programs on astronomy and science. Teachers and other group leaders can book field trips and visits by dialing [772-462-7503](tel:+17724627503)

### Telescopes & backyard astronomy

The Treasure Coast Astronomical Society (TCAS) provides telescopic views of the heavens after shows and hosts meetings at the planetarium on the last Tuesday of each month. For details, email [arsenal1957@att.net](mailto:arsenal1957@att.net)

### Ad Astra (To The Stars)

The Hallstrom Planetarium is part of Indian River State College and, as such, provides instruction for our students. If you’re interested in learning about astronomy, check out our public programs and our college classes by going to [irsc.edu](https://irsc.edu/). Indian River State College is an EA/EO educational institution.
