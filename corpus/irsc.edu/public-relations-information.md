# PUBLIC RELATIONS & PUBLIC INFORMATION

River News & Views serves as your gateway to compelling stories that showcase Indian River State College’s impact from local to global levels. We spotlight the innovative research, dedicated faculty, accomplished students, and transformative programs that define our institution.

Our mission centers on connecting audiences — from students and faculty to local, national, and international media — with newsworthy developments and the remarkable individuals behind them. We share stories of discovery, achievement, and service that demonstrate how The River’s influence extends from our community to the world stage.

Through strategic partnerships with communicators and content creators both on campus and across Florida, we work to elevate The River’s standing as a premier state college committed to student success, workforce development, and creating opportunity for our community.
