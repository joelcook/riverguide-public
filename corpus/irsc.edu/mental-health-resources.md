# **MENTAL HEALTH AND SUICIDE PREVENTION RESOURCES**

Indian River State College is committed to supporting the mental health, well-being and safety of our students and employees. If you or someone you know is having a mental health crisis, help is available.

## ****If There is Immediate Danger****

If you believe someone is an immediate danger to themselves or others, call **911.**

## ******Call, text, or chat 988******

The **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** offers 24/7 support for people experiencing emotional distress or having a mental health crisis.

- Call or text **988** or chat online at **[988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org).**

## ****Student Mental Health Resources****

### ****Student Wellness Center****

Indian River State College’s Student Wellness Center offers free counseling, crisis intervention, referrals to mental health providers, wellness workshops and more.

- Call the Student Wellness Center at **772-462-7825** or send an email to **[studentwellness@irsc.edu](mailto:studentwellness@irsc.edu).**

### ****Free Counseling****

The College partners with **UWill** to provide students with free counseling and crisis intervention.

- Schedule an appointment with a counselor at [app.uwill.com](https://app.uwill.com/).
- Call the **UWill** crisis line at **833-646-1526.**

## ****Employee Assistance Program****

Indian River State College’s Employee Assistance Program offers free counseling, financial coaching, referrals to childcare providers and more. Services are available to employees and their family members.

- Call the Employee Assistance Program at **855-775-4357** or schedule a counseling session online at [**https://reliance-matrix.allonehealth.com**](https://reliance-matrix.allonehealth.com)**.**

## ******Campus Safety and Security******

If an emergency happens on an Indian River State College campus, call **911 first** and then call Campus Safety at **772-462-4755.**

## ****After-Hours Help****

Mental health emergencies can occur at any time. If you need support outside of normal college hours:

- Call the **UWill** crisis line at **833-646-1526.**
- Call the **New Horizons Mobile Response Team** at **211** or **772-468-3909.**
- Call or text the national suicide prevention line at **988.**
- Call **911** if there is an emergency.
