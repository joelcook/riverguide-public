# EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

Our staff and faculty members help transform lives and communities—and they’re our biggest asset. We offer a range of employee benefits and perks to help ensure their well-being.

## Overview of Employee Benefits at The River

Indian River State College offers full-time, benefit-eligible employees and their qualifying dependents a competitive and comprehensive benefits package that includes:

- Medical, dental, vision and supplemental insurance plans
- Retirement plans
- Paid time off
- Wellness resources
- Onsite services
- Discounts and other perks

This page outlines these offerings in more detail.

[Download Benefits Brochure (PDF)](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/benefits-flyer-2025.pdf)

### Floating Holiday

A holiday for Support Employees that can be used at the discretion of the employee. The date selected must be requested at least two weeks in advance and approved by the immediate supervisor.
