# JOBS AT THE RIVER

Inspire, educate and support students during their academic and professional journeys. Explore open staff and faculty positions at Indian River State College.

[View Job Openings](https://irsc.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/External/jobs)

## ****Where Passion Meets Purpose****

Indian River State College transforms lives. Join us as we create lasting, positive change in Florida’s Treasure Coast and beyond.

Our employees, whether faculty or staff members, help shape the future of our students and the communities we serve.

When you work at The River, you’ll be part of a forward-thinking institution that values innovation, inclusivity and the pursuit of knowledge. Here, you’ll grow professionally and be supported.
