# WELCOME TO THE RIVER

Your Impact Starts Now. Whether you’re teaching in the classroom or supporting student success behind the scenes, you’re now part of a community dedicated to excellence, student accessibility, and innovation.

Need help with your classes? Come to Indian River State College/s Academic Support center. Our professional tutors can help you with whatever subject matter you need assistance with.

At Indian River State College, our people make the difference. Whether you’re teaching in the classroom, guiding operations, or supporting students across our campuses, your role matters—and your work helps create the culture of connection and excellence we’re known for.

This internal site is built to get you started with clarity and confidence. Inside, you’ll find the tools, resources, and support you need to navigate your first days and beyond. Because here at The River, every role contributes to something bigger—and we’re glad you’re here.

## HAVE YOU ATTENDED RIVER READY, YOUR NEW EMPLOYEE ORIENTATION?

River Ready is your first step into the culture, community, and mission of Indian River State College. In this engaging session, you’ll meet colleagues from across the College, learn about the support and benefits available to you, and gain insight into what makes The River a place where everyone can thrive.

[Sign Up for River Ready](https://outlook.office.com/book/RiverReadyNewEmployeeOrientation@mailirsc.onmicrosoft.com/)

## RESOURCES FOR ALL

No matter your role, we want you to feel supported every step of the way. From technology tools and wellness programs to brand guidelines and campus maps, The River provides a range of resources to help you succeed in your position and stay connected across departments. Please note: benefit information is only applicable to **full-time employees**. If you are a part-time employee, you are still encouraged to explore the other resources provided here to support your success at The River. All other resources can be found via Okta.

## NEED HELP? HR IS HERE FOR YOU.

Whether you have questions about benefits, payroll, leave, or workplace policies, our Human Resources team is ready to support you. You can schedule a one-on-one meeting with an HR representative or submit your inquiry using the online HR Request Form. We’re here to ensure you have the information and guidance you need to feel confident in your new role—and throughout your time at The River.
