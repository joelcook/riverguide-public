# SENDING TRANSCRIPTS TO INDIAN RIVER STATE COLLEGE

**Important:** A delay in receiving some official high school transcripts is temporarily affecting the application of **Financial Aid and Promise funding** for some students. If you’ve completed your application, requested your transcript, and finished your enrollment steps, **no further action is needed at this time.** You can still register for classes. Students impacted by this issue will not be dropped for nonpayment or charged a late registration fee. Visit **irsc.edu/support** for updates and resources.

Among the supporting documents required for admission to Indian River State College are official, sealed transcripts from the high school you graduated from, and any colleges or universities you have attended.

Official transcripts may be sent directly from the high school and/or college or university to Indian River State College Student Records. We prefer electronic delivery via FASTER or a transcript service like Parchment or National Student Clearinghouse.  If the envelope is sealed, you may also deliver transcripts to one of our campuses in person. Your application for admission to Indian River State College is not considered complete until all required official transcripts are received.

Non-degree-seeking students are not required to submit transcripts and are not eligible for federal financial aid.

## Home Schooled Students

Home schooled students must provide Indian River State College Student Records with a signed [affidavit](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/FORM-homeschool-affidavit-FL-20252.png), completed by your parent or legal guardian, attesting that you have completed a Florida home education program.  In addition, you must provide a Verification Letter from the School District in which you reside.  More information can be found [here](https://new.express.adobe.com/webpage/rWQ14JEbt3KxG).

## Students with General Educational Development (GED)

Students with a Florida or non-Florida GED® must request that official GED® transcripts be sent to Indian River State College Student Records. GED® transcripts can be requested from <https://ged.com/>.

GED® is a registered trademark of the American Council on Education (ACE) and administered exclusively by GED® Testing Service LLC under license.

## International Students

Foreign High School: Successful completion of a high school program is required for admission. Students who have graduated from a foreign high school must have their foreign credentials evaluated by a current [NACES Member](https://naces.org/members/) and provide Indian River State College Student Records with an evaluation. The evaluation must state that the student has completed a secondary education equivalent to a standard US High School Diploma. \* If the student has completed two years of a foreign (non-native) language in High School, they should request a course-by-course evaluation.

Foreign Colleges and Universities: Submission of foreign post-secondary transcripts is optional. Students who have attended a college or university outside the United States and wish to transfer their coursework must have their foreign academic credentials evaluated by a current [NACES member](https://www.naces.org/members.php) and provide Indian River State College Student Records with a course-by-course evaluation from each institution attended. The student is responsible for evaluation fees, and there is no guarantee that any coursework will transfer to Indian River State College.

All international transcript evaluations should be sent to [records@irsc.edu](mailto:records@irsc.edu).

International students transferring from a school within the United States must complete the top portion of the International Student Transfer Release Form in the [International Student Admission Packet](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/international-student-admission-booklet.pdf). The international student advisor at your school must complete the bottom portion and forward it to Indian River State College.

## Veterans

United States military veterans are encouraged to submit their military transcripts. Transcripts are available through the [Joint Services Transcript website.](https://jst.doded.mil/jst/)

Air Force personnel should request transcripts through the [Air University Office of the Registrar](https://www.airuniversity.af.edu/Registrar/Transcript-Requests/).
