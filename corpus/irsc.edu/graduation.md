# **Steps to Graduate**

You’re almost there! Whether you’re completing a certificate, associate degree or bachelor’s degree, graduating from Indian River State College (The River) is an important milestone.

Before you finish, there are a few steps you need to take to make sure your graduation is official.

## Your Graduation Checklist

### 1. Apply for Graduation

All students must submit a graduation application to officially complete their degree or certificate, even if they do not plan to attend the Commencement ceremony.

Log in to the River Portal and access the graduation application through Banner.

Apply for Graduation

Important: Applying for graduation does not automatically register you for the Commencement ceremony. If you plan to participate, you’ll need to complete a separate registration.

### 2. Review Your Degree Progress

Before applying, make sure you’re on track to complete your program requirements.

Use River Guide Degree Audit (DegreeWorks) to review your academic progress, including completed courses, remaining requirements and courses currently in progress.

Review Your Degree Progress

If you have questions about your degree audit or graduation eligibility, connect with Academic Advising.

### 3. Complete Your Program Requirements

To graduate, you must successfully complete all requirements for your degree or certificate, including any applicable coursework, credit hours, GPA requirements and other program-specific requirements.

Submitting a graduation application does not guarantee graduation. Your degree or certificate will be awarded once the College confirms that all requirements have been met.

### 4. Meet with Academic Advising, If Needed

Not sure whether you’re ready to graduate? Your academic advisor can help you review your degree progress, identify outstanding requirements and plan your remaining courses.

[Connect with Academic Progress](https://irsc.edu/programs/academic-resources-support/)

Important: Applying for graduation does not automatically register you for the Commencement ceremony. If you plan to participate, you’ll need to complete a separate registration.

### 5. Verify Your Contact Information

Make sure your personal information is accurate so you don’t miss important updates or experience delays receiving your diploma or certificate.

Review and update the following information in the River Portal:

- Your name as it should appear on your diploma or certificate
- Your personal email address
- Your current mailing address

[Log In to The River Portal](https://go.irsc.edu/)

If your legal name needs to be updated, contact Student Records for instructions and required documentation.

### 6. Receive Your Diploma or Certificate

Once your final grades are posted and your graduation requirements have been verified, the College will process your degree or certificate.

Indian River State College uses **Parchment** for diploma and credential services.

Diplomas and Certificates

Additional information about diploma availability, delivery and how to request a replacement will be provided here.

## **Graduation vs. Commencement:**

## **What’s the Difference?**

Graduation and Commencement are two different milestones.

Graduation is the official completion of your academic program. Once you meet all requirements and your degree or certificate is awarded, you are a graduate of Indian River State College.

Commencement is the ceremony where we celebrate your accomplishments with fellow graduates, family and friends.

You can graduate without attending Commencement, and participating does not confirm your degree or certificate has been awarded.

If you’d like to walk across the stage, be sure to review the eligibility requirements and register separately for the ceremony.

[Explore Commencement Ceremonies](https://irsc.edu/commencement/)
