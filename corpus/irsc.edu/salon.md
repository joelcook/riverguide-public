# SALON & BARBERING SERVICES AT THE RIVER

Experience professional **hair, beauty, and barbering services at affordable prices** at the **Indian River State College Salon in Fort Pierce**.

Our services are performed by talented **cosmetology and barbering students** as part of their training, under the supervision of licensed instructors. Every appointment supports student learning and workforce development on Florida’s Treasure Coast.

[Book Now](https://waitwhile.com/locations/cosmetology?registration=booking)

## Why Choose Our Salon?

- Affordable services starting as low as **$10**
- Hair, skin, and nail treatments for all ages
- Personalized attention from students in training
- Peace of mind with supervision from licensed instructors
- The chance to make a difference—every appointment directly supports student education and workforce readiness right here on the Treasure Coast.

*Because services are performed by students, **appointment times and results may vary slightly**, but every visit supports real-world learning.*
