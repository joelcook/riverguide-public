# PROFESSIONAL TRAINING FOR PUBLIC SAFETY PROFESSIONALS

Advance your skills, maintain certifications, and complete specialized public safety training through Indian River State College’s Treasure Coast Public Safety Training Complex.

[**Register for Training**](https://tcpublicsafetytraining.gosignmeup.com/Public/Course/Browse)

## **At the River, You…**

- Can complete advanced, specialized, and in-service training designed for current public safety professionals.
- Will learn from experienced instructors using industry-standard equipment and training facilities.
- Can access professional development opportunities for law enforcement, corrections, fire service, emergency medical services, and other public safety disciplines.

## **Available Training**

The Treasure Coast Public Safety Training Complex offers professional development opportunities for current and certified public safety professionals. Training offerings vary throughout the year and may include:

- Law Enforcement
- Corrections
- Fire Service
- Emergency Medical Services (EMS)
- Instructor Certification
- Leadership Development
- Specialized Public Safety Training
- Physical Ability Testing
- FLUSAR

[**View Available Courses & Register**](https://tcpublicsafetytraining.gosignmeup.com/Public/Course/Browse)

## **Why Train at The River?**

Whether you’re maintaining required certifications, preparing for instructor credentials, or expanding your professional expertise, The River provides high-quality training that helps public safety professionals stay current with industry standards and best practices.

Courses are offered throughout the year, with registration and course availability managed through our online training portal.

## **We’re Here to Help**

Have questions about professional training or finding the right course? Our team is happy to help you identify available training opportunities and guide you through the registration process.
