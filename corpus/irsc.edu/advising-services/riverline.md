# THE RIVER LINE

### The support you need. The next step made simple.

Have a question about admissions, financial aid, classes, or your academic goals? The River Line makes it easy to connect with the people who can help.

At Indian River State College, you don’t have to navigate your college journey alone. Whether you’re just getting started, planning your next semester, or preparing for graduation, The River Line connects you with student support services across all five campuses.

Use The River Line to schedule an appointment or join a virtual line for assistance.

[Join The River Line](https://waitwhile.com/accounts/irsc)

## Connect with Student Support Services

The River Line helps you connect with staff from:

- **Admissions:** Get guidance on applying to The River and taking your next steps toward enrollment.
- **Academic Advising:** Get help choosing classes, planning your academic path, understanding degree requirements, and preparing for graduation or transfer.
- **Financial Aid:** Find answers to questions about financial aid and the steps needed to fund your education.
- **Veterans Services:** Connect with staff for guidance on veterans’ education benefits and available resources.
- **Career and Transfer Services:** Explore career goals, transfer opportunities, and your next steps after The River.
- **Student Records:** Get assistance with student records and related academic processes.

## How The River Line Works

Getting connected is simple.

### **1. Choose Your Campus and Service**

Select your campus and the student support service you need. You can access The River Line online or visit a self-service kiosk at a campus Student Success Center (The Hub).

### **2. Join the Line or Request an Appointment**

Join the virtual line for an available drop-in service, or select **Schedule an Appointment** for assistance that requires dedicated time with a staff member.

### **3. Stay Connected**

If you’ve joined the virtual line, you can wait in a comfortable location while monitoring your notifications.

If you’ve requested an academic advising appointment, your advisor will review your request and communicate whether it is confirmed or needs to be rescheduled.

### ****4. Meet with Our Team****

When it’s your turn in the virtual line, follow your notification instructions to connect with the appropriate student support office.

For scheduled appointments, follow the details provided in your appointment confirmation.

## Academic Advising at The River

### Your goals. Your plan. Your next step.

Whether you’re choosing classes, exploring a new program, or preparing to graduate, your academic advisor can help you make informed decisions and stay on track.

The River offers two ways to connect with academic advising: scheduled appointments and in-person drop-in advising.

###

Personalized guidance for your academic journey.

A scheduled appointment gives you dedicated time with your assigned academic advisor to discuss your goals and develop a plan for moving forward.

What to expect:

- Appointments are 45 minutes long.
- Appointments may be available in person, by video, or by phone.
- Appointments must be requested with your assigned academic advisor at least 24 hours in advance.
- Availability varies by advisor.
- Students arriving more than 15 minutes late will be asked to reschedule.

Scheduled appointments are required for:

- New student advising
- Creating or updating your academic plan
- Changing your program of study or major
- Academic standing and reinstatement
- Reviewing final degree requirements or completing a graduation audit
- Reviewing transfer prerequisites for your intended program

*New student advising, creating or updating an academic plan, and final degree requirements or graduation audits are available in person or by video only.*

###

Your assigned academic advisor is your guide to making informed academic decisions. Advisors are assigned based on your selected program of study.

To find your advisor:

1. Log in to your **Student Portal**.
2. Select the **New River Portal**.
3. On the home page, find the card labeled **My Assigned Advisor**.
4. Review your advisor’s name and campus location.

You’ll need this information to schedule your advising appointment through The River Line.

###

Follow these five steps to request an appointment with your assigned academic advisor.

**Step 1: Select Your Campus**

Visit [The River Line](https://irsc.edu/riverline) and select the campus where your assigned academic advisor is located. Then choose **Schedule an Appointment**.

**Step 2: Choose Academic Advising**

From the available services, select **Academic Advising**.

**Step 3: Select Your Advisor and Appointment Time**

Choose your assigned academic advisor from the list, then select an available date and time.

Don’t see your advisor’s name? Make sure you’ve selected the correct campus.

**Step 4: Enter Your Information**

Complete the appointment request form, including:

- Your name
- Phone number
- Student ID
- How you would like to meet with your advisor

Select **Send appointment request** to submit your information.

**Step 5: Watch for Your Appointment Confirmation**

Your appointment request will be sent to your academic advisor for review.

Within 24 hours of submitting your request, you’ll receive communication confirming your appointment or providing instructions to reschedule.

**Important:** Submitting an appointment request does not mean your appointment has been confirmed. Be sure to review the follow-up communication from your advisor.

[Schedule an Advising Appointment](https://irsc.edu/riverline)

###

**Quick questions? We’re here to help.**

Drop-in advising is a convenient option for students who need assistance with brief academic questions and don’t require a full advising appointment.

Drop-in advising is available **in person only**, during designated drop-in hours.

**What to expect:**

- No scheduled appointment is required.
- Meetings typically last 15–20 minutes.
- Students meet with the first available academic advisor.
- Requests for a specific advisor cannot be accommodated.

**Drop-in advising can help with:**

- Reviewing holds on your student account
- Course recommendations for one semester
- Learning how to register for classes
- Referrals to other student services
- Questions about college academic policies

*Some student account holds require a scheduled appointment to be removed.*
