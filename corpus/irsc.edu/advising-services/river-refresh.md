# RIVER REFRESH

River Refresh provides personalized support, resources, and guidance to help students navigate academic challenges and move forward successfully.

## **Get Back on Track with River Refresh**

River Refresh is a program that helps students on Academic Warning, Probation, Suspension, Reinstatement, and Leave learn about resources that are available to them at The River. The River Refresh Mentors are available to assist those students who may need extra help.

River Refresh Mentors assist students whose current Academic Standing is:

- Academic Warning
- Academic Probation
- Academic Suspension
- Academic Reinstatement
- Academic Leave

How to Complete the Academic Reinstatement Process

[See the Checklist](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Reinstatement-Process-Checklist-Spring-2027.pdf)

## **Are You on Academic Suspension and Would Like to Return?**

If a student wants to return to Indian River State College after Academic Suspension, they must complete the Academic Reinstatement Process. If approved, the student would return under Academic Reinstatement Standing and will be required to participate in the River Refresh Program.

Those who are returning to Indian River State College after Academic Suspension receive additional support through a required meeting with a River Refresh Mentor, Tutoring, and a Canvas organization.

Students approved for Academic Reinstatement are required to participate in River Refresh. River Refresh requirements:

- Earn a minimum 2.0 term GPA to be able to continue to take courses in the next semester and to avoid being placed on Academic Leave.
- Meet with a River Refresh Mentor (in person at the Massey Campus or on Zoom)
  - <https://irsc.edu/advising-services/riverline/>
- Meet with your assigned advisor each semester while on Academic Reinstatement for academic planning and support.  Schedule your appointment here: <https://irsc.edu/advising-services/riverline/>
- Participate in River Refresh on Canvas.
- Complete three tutoring sessions and self-report them in Module 5 of the River Refresh Canvas.
  - Tutoring can be in-person or online. No appointment necessary!
    - In-person: <https://irsc.libguides.com/TutoringCenters>
    - 24/7 Online Tutoring: Brainfuse can be found on your River Portal

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Academic Warning?

- A student is placed on **Academic Warning** if their cumulative **GPA is below 2.0** for one semester.
- A student on Academic Warning should schedule an appointment with their assigned academic advisor to discuss strategies to increase their GPA.
- A student can return to **Good Standing** by raising their cumulative GPA to **2.0 or higher** in the next semester.
 What is Academic Probation?

- A student is placed on Academic Probation if their cumulative GPA is below 2.0 for a second consecutive semester.
- An Academic Probation Hold will be placed on the student’s account. The hold is a notification to the student that they are required to schedule an appointment with their assigned academic advisor to discuss strategies for increasing their GPA.
- A student will not be able to register for more classes until they meet with their assigned academic advisor.
- A student can return to **Good Standing** by raising their cumulative GPA to **2.0 or higher** in the next semester.
 What is Academic Suspension?

- A student is placed on Academic Suspension if their cumulative GPA is below 2.0 for a third consecutive semester.
- An Academic Suspension Hold will be placed on the student’s account.
- A student will not be allowed to take courses in the semester following the Academic Suspension. If the student is registered, the courses will be dropped.
- To return to Indian River State College, a student must request and be approved for Academic Reinstatement.
 How does a suspended student request Academic Reinstatement?

View the checklist [here](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Reinstatement-Process-Checklist-Summer-2026.pdf).

 What is Academic Reinstatement?

- A student is placed on Academic Reinstatement if their request for Academic Reinstatement is approved.
- An Academic Reinstatement Hold will be placed on the student’s account.
- A student will not be able to register on their own while on Academic Reinstatement. A student will need to schedule an appointment with their assigned academic advisor to discuss appropriate courses for increasing GPA.
- A student will be allowed to take 6 credits per semester.
- A student will be required to participate in the River Refresh Program.A student must earn a term GPA of 2.0 or higher to be allowed to continue to take classes and avoid being placed on Academic Leave.
- A student can return to Good Standing by increasing their cumulative GPA to 2.0 or higher.
 What is a cumulative GPA?

A cumulative GPA is the average of all the grades a student has earned in all of the classes they have taken, including schools they have attended prior to Indian River State College.

Learn how to calculate GPA: <https://youtu.be/qYHsThZWydY?si=ZoYGM0DjVcsONDRz>

 What is a term GPA?

A term GPA is the average of the student’s grades for one semester.

 Who is required to participate in the River Refresh program?

A student who is approved for Academic Reinstatement and is registered in a degree-seeking program is required to participate in the River Refresh Program during their first semester returning to Indian River State College.

 What is Academic Leave?

- A student can be placed on Academic Leave if they earn a semester GPA of less than 2.0 while on Academic Reinstatement.
- An Academic Leave Hold will be placed on the student’s account.
- A student will not be allowed to take courses in the three consecutive semesters following the Academic Leave. If the student is registered, the courses will be dropped.
- To return to Indian River State College, a student must request and be approved for Academic Reinstatement.
 How does withdrawing impact my GPA?

Withdrawing from a course does not affect the GPA. However, this is considered an attempt and will show as a “W” on an official transcript. If a student is receiving Financial Aid, it is recommended that they speak with the Financial Aid Department to learn how withdrawing could affect financial aid eligibility.

NOTE: You cannot withdraw from a course if you are taking it for the third time.

 How do I return to Good Standing?

To return to Good Standing, a student must raise their cumulative GPA to a 2.0 or higher. They can do this by retaking classes they did not pass and earning a higher grade, or by doing well in new classes. Every student’s situation is different, so it is recommended that students schedule an appointment with their assigned academic advisor to discuss strategies for improving their GPA.
