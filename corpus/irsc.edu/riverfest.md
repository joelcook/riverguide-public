# RIVER FEST

Join us for an all-day free community festival celebrating the energy and excellence of Indian River State College. Held alongside the 2026 NJCAA Swimming & Diving National Championships, River Fest is packed with **live entertainment, food trucks, games, and family fun**—open to all!

## A Full Day of Music, Food & Fun

### Saturday, March 7 | 11:00 AM–9:00 PM

- **Free entry for all**
- DJs all day
- Food & drink trucks
- Games & inflatables
- Fireworks
- Swimming & Diving finals @ 5:30 PM

📍 **Fort Pierce Campus | [3209 Virginia Ave](https://www.google.com/maps/place/3209+Virginia+Ave,+Fort+Pierce,+FL+34981/@27.4243817,-80.3599739,17z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x88def1f25f31e6ff:0x51f83db6266e5de!8m2!3d27.4230061!4d-80.3564133!16s%2Fg%2F11c0vy3yjp?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MDYxNS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)**

## About the Championship

The River is home to the most dominant teams in NJCAA history.

- 51-time consecutive national champions – Men’s Swimming & Diving
- 47-time consecutive national champions – Women’s Swimming & Diving

## Alumni, We’ve Got a Spot Just for You

The Indian River State College Alumni Association is hosting a special gathering during River Fest — and you’re invited!

Stop by our exclusive alumni tent to reconnect, relax, and enjoy:

- A casual bar with beer, wine, seltzers, soda, and water
- Snacks and bites (food menu coming soon!)
- Giveaways for alumni
- A peek into our IRSC archives
- A photo zone to capture the memories

Whether you’re coming back for the championships, the fireworks, or just to see what’s new at The River — we can’t wait to welcome you home.

Open from 4:00–9:00 PM on the Massey Campus — just look for the big Alumni flags!
