# **What Do You Need Help With?**

### Scheduled System Maintenance – October 15

[View Spring Classes](https://reg-prod.irscsaas.elluciancloud.com:8103/StudentRegistrationSsb/ssb/term/termSelection?mode=search)

The new River Portal may be temporarily unavailable on **Thursday, October 15, from 12:30 a.m. to 5:00 a.m.** due to scheduled system maintenance. During this time, some services in the new River Portal, including registration and student records, may be unavailable.

Please plan accordingly and complete any time-sensitive tasks outside the maintenance window. Thank you for your patience.
