## How to Claim Your Free Coffee or Boba

As a thank-you for all you do for our students and the College, we invite you to set up your Mobile River Card and enjoy a complimentary coffee or boba through October.

Your Mobile River Card gives you a convenient digital version of your College ID right on your phone. Once your card is active, show it at a participating café to redeem your free drink.

## **Before You Begin: Photo Required**

An approved photo in the IDX card system is required before you can add your Mobile River Card to your digital wallet.

You may submit a photo through the Transact eAccounts app or have your photo taken at Campus Safety.

## **How to Set Up Your Mobile River Card**

### **1. Download Transact eAccounts**

Download the **Transact eAccounts** app from the App Store or Google Play. Open the app and sign in using your College credentials.

### **2. Add Your Card to Your Mobile Wallet**

Once your photo has been approved, select your Mobile River Card in the Transact eAccounts app and follow the instructions for your device.

###

1. Select your Mobile River Card in the Transact eAccounts app.
2. Choose **Add to Apple Wallet**.
3. Follow the prompts to complete setup.
4. Open Apple Wallet to confirm that your Mobile River Card appears.

###

1. Select your Mobile River Card in the Transact eAccounts app.
2. Choose **Add to Google Wallet**.
3. Follow the prompts to complete setup.
4. Open Google Wallet to confirm that your Mobile River Card appears.

###

1. Select your Mobile River Card in the Transact eAccounts app.
2. Choose **Add to Samsung Wallet**.
3. Follow the prompts to complete setup.
4. Open Samsung Wallet to confirm that your Mobile River Card appears.

## Redeem Your Free Drink

Once your Mobile River Card is active, show it at one of the participating locations below to receive your complimentary drink.

- The Landing Café at Massey — KSU
  - Coffee and boba
- The Perch Café at Pruitt — Building J
  - Coffee; boba coming soon
- The Perch Café at Okeechobee — Student Center parking lot
  - Coffee

Offer available to faculty and staff through October.

## **Need Help?**

For help with your Mobile River Card, contact [auxservices@irsc.edu](mailto:auxservices@irsc.edu).
