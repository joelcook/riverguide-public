# BACHELOR’S DEGREE ADMISSIONS PROCESS

Advance your career with a high-quality, affordable bachelor’s degree from Indian River State College. Whether you’re a recent associate degree graduate or an adult learner returning to school, we provide the flexible pathways you need to lead in your field.

## A Bachelor’s Degree designed for your life.

At The River, we believe a degree should work for you, not against your schedule. Our Baccalaureate programs are crafted to meet the needs of the Florida workforce, offering a blend of academic rigor and real-world application.

- **Format Your Way:** Choose from 100% online, hybrid, or traditional in-person classes at our local campuses.
- **Affordability First:** Graduate without the burden of heavy debt. 91% of our students graduate debt-free, with tuition rates significantly lower than state universities.
- **Adult-Ready Support:** We specialize in helping students ages 30+ navigate the transition back to school with dedicated advisors and technical support.

## 4 Steps to Starting Your Degree

Ready to take the leap? Our streamlined process is designed to get you into the classroom and on your way to graduation.

1. **Submit Your Application:** Complete the online Baccalaureate application and pay the $30 non-refundable fee.
2. **Send Your Transcripts:** Provide official transcripts from all prior colleges. We’ll help you evaluate your credits to see how close you already are to your degree.
3. **Prove Residency:** Submit your Florida Residency Declaration to ensure you receive our lowest tuition rates.
4. **Connect with an Advisor:** Meet with a dedicated Bachelor’s program advisor to map out your personalized degree plan and discuss financial aid.
