### Academic Breakout Sessions

All students will attend **three required breakout sessions** designed to give you clarity and direction:

- **Navigate Your First Year: Promise, Scholarships & Success at The River**
  Understand financial aid, scholarships, the Promise Program, and how to start strong.
- **Student Services & Academic Resources**
  Explore Learning Resources, the Help Desk, Student Support Services, the Bookstore, Student Life, and River Refresh.
- **Exploring Academic Programs**
  Discover pathways aligned with your goals—whether you plan to transfer, earn a degree, or train for a career.

Plus:

- Food trucks
- Live DJ
- Giveaways and River gear
- Opportunities to meet classmates and campus leaders

This is more than orientation. It’s your first step forward.
