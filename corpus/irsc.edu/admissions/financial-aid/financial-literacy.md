# CENTER FOR FINANCIAL LITERACY

Learn to make informed financial decisions.

The Indian River State College Center for Financial Literacy’s mission is to provide financial literacy educational programs, activities and materials to assist students and the community in making informed financial decisions.

We provide activities, essential tools and resources to prepare students to improve their financial decision-making, enabling them to achieve long-term financial stability while promoting critical thinking in applied contexts to assist them in succeeding in their academic pathways. This purpose parallels Indian River State College’s commitment to developing a highly skilled workforce and cultivating student success.

The Center for Financial Literacy was the recipient of the Marilyn C. Link Endowed Teaching Chair in Financial Management from 2017 to 2020.

## **Essential Tools and Resources**

The Center for Financial Literacy provides essential materials to help students improve their financial decision-making, allowing them to achieve long-term financial stability.

The [Kapoor, Dlabay, Hughes, and Hart (2017) white paper](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/steps-to-create-a-successful-budget.pdf) will guide you through the personal budgeting process, help you better understand credit, explain savings options and financial goals, and provide tips on how to protect your identity.
