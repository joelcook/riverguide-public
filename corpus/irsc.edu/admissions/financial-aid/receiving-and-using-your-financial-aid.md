# RECEIVING AND USING YOUR FINANCIAL AID

Learn how aid is paid and how to keep it.

## How Financial Aid Disbursement Works

Disbursement is when aid is applied to eligible charges. Check RiverMail and New River Portal for updates.

Aid pays tuition, fees, and authorized bookstore charges first. Remaining funds are refunded after attendance is verified.

Most aid is paid 4-6 weeks after classes begin. Later-starting classes and first-time federal loans may take longer.

### Set Up Direct Deposit

In The River Portal, go to General Self-Service, then Direct Deposit, and add your bank account.

### Paying for Tuition Fees and Books

Your award may change if your eligible enrollment changes. You are responsible for any balance your aid does not cover.

Keep your address current. Contact the Financial Aid Office before withdrawing or changing your enrollment.

### If Your Family Situation Changes

Contact Financial Aid if your family has a major financial change. You may need to provide documentation.

## Satisfactory Academic Progress Policy

To keep federal aid, meet SAP standards. SAP is reviewed every semester.

### SAP Standards and Evaluation

| Standard | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Cumulative GPA** | At least 2.0 cumulative GPA. |
| **Completion rate** | Successfully complete at least 67% of all attempted credits. |

SAP includes applicable College and accepted transfer credits, including withdrawals, failed courses, repeats, and developmental coursework.

### SAP Status

SAP includes applicable College and accepted transfer credits, including withdrawals, failed courses, repeats, and developmental coursework.

### SAP Appeals

If circumstances beyond your control affected your progress, you may appeal with supporting documents. If approved, follow your assigned aid plan.

## Promise Program Students

Promise students must meet SAP standards. Promise covers two years only and does not automatically cover a full associate degree.

## Withdrawal and Return of Aid

Withdrawing before completing 60% of the term may require repayment of federal aid. Contact the Financial Aid Office before withdrawing to understand how it may affect your aid.

## Program Objective Enrollment Compliance

Federal aid applies only to coursework required for an eligible program. Stopping attendance in eligible courses may affect your aid.
