# Apply to Indian River State College

Applying is simple and can be completed entirely online. Whether you’re earning your first degree, returning to college, or preparing for a new career, we’re here to help every step of the way.

[Apply Now](https://apply.irsc.edu/)

## Before You Apply

Have these items ready before starting your application:

- Your full legal name and date of birth
- A personal email address (not a school email)
- Your current mailing address
- Your cell phone number
- Your Social Security Number (if available)
- Your high school information
- Any previous college information and transcripts
- A payment method for the application fee

## Start Your Application

Use your **personal email address** to create your account by following these steps:

1. Go to [**apply.irsc.edu**](https://apply.irsc.edu)
2. Click **Apply Now**
3. Select **River Application**
4. Enter your personal email address
5. Create a password
6. Save your email and password—you’ll use them to return to your application.

## Finish Your Application

If you’ve already started an application, you can return at any time to:

- Finish your application
- Upload required documents
- Check your application status

If you’ve forgotten your password, select **Forgot Password** on the sign-in page.

## Application Fees

Most programs require a **$20 application fee**, including:

- Associate in Arts (A.A.)
- Associate in Arts (A.A.)
- Associate in Science (A.S.)
- Career Training
- Associate Level Professional Development / Non-Degree
- Technical Certificates
- Adult Education (ESL & GED)

Select programs require a different application fee:

- **$30** — Bachelor’s Degree programs
- **$30** — Selective Admission programs
- **$60** — A.S. Nursing

*Some programs may have additional application fees.*

## After You Apply

### 1. Watch Your Email

We’ll send updates to the personal email address you used on your application.

### 2. Submit Any Requested Documents

If additional information is needed, we’ll let you know.

### 3. Complete Your Enrollment Checklist

Once your application is processed, you’ll receive instructions for your remaining enrollment steps.

### 4. Activate RiverMail

You’ll receive a RiverMail activation email **after your application has been processed.** Activate it as soon as you receive it to avoid missing important updates.

*If you don’t receive your RiverMail activation email after your application has been processed, check your spam folder or contact Student Services.*
