# MILITARY AND VETERANS SERVICES

Indian River State College is proud to welcome veterans and active-duty service members of the U.S. Armed Forces and their dependents into our educational programs. Our dedicated staff are in place to serve and ensure that our heroes are given elite-level customer support.

The College is recognized as a top college by *Military Advanced Education* and offers military-friendly classes, convenient course scheduling, and support services to help you reach your educational goals*.*

The Department of Military and Veterans Services provides dedicated services to all active-duty personnel, reservists, members of the National Guard, veterans and qualified dependents. We understand the intricate and unique issues our military and veteran students face. Our motto is “Servientes Nostros Heroas,” which translates to “Serving Our Heroes.”

## Readmission After Interruption of Education Due to Military Service

Service members who have their attendance interrupted due to military service are eligible to be promptly re-admitted with the same academic status as when the service member last attended.  The cumulative length of the absence and of all previous absences from the school for military service may not exceed five years. Only the time the student spends actually performing service is counted.

In order to qualify, the student must notify the College of his/her military service and intention to return to school. Students must contact the Military and Veterans Service Department to provide notification of service and notification of intent to return.

Board Policy 4135 – Withdrawal From Classes can be viewed [**here**](https://go.boarddocs.com/fl/irsc/Board.nsf/vpublic?open).  The policy regarding Service member readmission aligns with Chapter 3 of Volume 2 of the Federal Student Aid Handbook.
