# FINANCIAL AID

Get help paying for college.

## Learn the Steps

### 1. Complete the FAFSA

Complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) as early as possible and list Indian River State College school code: 001493.

[Start Your FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa)

### 2. Check your eligibility and submit required records

You may be eligible if you are a U.S. citizen or eligible noncitizen, have a high school diploma or equivalent, enroll in an eligible program, meet SAP standards, and complete your financial aid file.

Federal aid is available for degree programs, certificates of 24 or more credits, and vocational programs of 600 or more clock hours. Apprenticeships are not eligible.

Send your official high school transcript to the Records Department.  If selected for verification, those documents must be submitted before aid eligibility can be determined.
