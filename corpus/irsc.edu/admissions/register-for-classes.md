# REGISTER FOR CLASSES

You’re just a few steps away from building your class schedule. Before you register, make sure you’ve completed the items on this page.

Early registration opens October 7 at 8:00 a.m. for student-athletes, students in B.S. or B.A.S. programs, students with approved SAS accommodations, veterans, and students who are at least 75% complete with their program.

Open registration for all students begins October 14 at 8:00 a.m.

[View Spring Classes](https://reg-prod.irscsaas.elluciancloud.com:8103/StudentRegistrationSsb/ssb/term/termSelection?mode=search)

View Full Academic Calendar

## Step 1. Have You Been Admitted?

You must be admitted before you can register for classes. If you haven’t applied yet, start here.

[Apply Now](https://apply.irsc.edu)

## Step 2. Complete Your Enrollment Checklist

Before registering, make sure you’ve completed:

Residency/Citizenship Requirements

Financial Responsibility Form

Any required admissions documents

RiverMail activation

[Finish Application](https://apply.irsc.edu)

[Get Application Support](https://irsc.edu/admissions/qless/)

## Step 3. Meet with an Advisor (If Needed)

Meet with your assigned academic advisor to discuss:

- First semester courses
- Degree planning
- Transfer pathways
- Program changes

[Meet an Advisor](https://irsc.edu/admissions/qless/)

[View Steps for Advising](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/How-to-schedule-appt-find-advisor.pdf)

***Tip:** Many students can register without an advising appointment. If you’re unsure, check with your assigned advisor first.*

## Step 4. Register for Classes

Once you’re eligible, register online through the River Portal.

***Remember:** Adding classes to your cart does **not** reserve your seat. You must click **Submit** to complete your registration.*

[Access River Portal](https://go.irsc.edu/)

[View Steps to Register](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/How-to-Register-Swap-Drop.pdf)

## Step 5. Check Your Balance

Registering for classes creates a financial obligation.

After registering:

- Review your account balance
- Review Financial Aid
- Make payment arrangements if necessary

Even if you receive Financial Aid or The Promise Program, you may still owe fees or other charges.

[Financial Aid](https://irsc.edu/admissions/financial-aid/)

[View Steps to Check Balance](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Steps-to-Check-Balance.pdf)

***Tip:** Many students can register without an advising appointment. If you’re unsure, check with your assigned advisor first.*

## Need Help?

Our Student Services teams are assisting a high volume of students.
Before visiting campus:

1. Bring a physical photo ID
2. Complete your Financial Responsibility Form
3. Resolve account holds
4. Log in to your River Portal

Join The RiverLine before arriving and monitor your place in line remotely.

[Join The River Line](https://irsc.edu/advising-services/riverline/)
