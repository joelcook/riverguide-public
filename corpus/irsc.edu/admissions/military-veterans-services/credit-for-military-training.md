# CREDIT FOR MILITARY TRAINING

At Indian River State College, your military experience could help you earn credit toward your degree or certificate—which means you take fewer classes and graduate faster.

## Opportunities for Earned Credit

Learn more about how you may be awarded credits thanks to your training and education in the military.

### Transfer Credits

If you attended an accredited college while you were in the military, such as Community College of the Air Force or Central Texas College, you could receive credit for classes you completed there.

To qualify, you will need to submit your official school transcripts, which will be evaluated for credit opportunities.

### Credit for Military Experiences

To receive credit for your military experiences, you should request an official transcript from the Joint Services Transcript.

[Request form for Army, Navy, Marines and Coast Guard](https://www.military.com/education/timesaving-programs/the-joint-services-transcript.html)

[Request form for Air Force](https://www.airuniversity.af.mil/Barnes/CCAF/)

We will evaluate your transcript to determine what experiences will apply directly to your program at The River.

Typically, credits can be awarded toward Associate of Science degree programs.

### Competency Credit

Competency credit can be awarded in certain areas, such as criminal justice or health science. The Department of Military and Veteran Services can work with you and your chosen program to determine if you are eligible for competency credit toward your degree or certificate.
