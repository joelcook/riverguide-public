# SCHOOL OF PUBLIC SERVICE EDUCATION

Preparing public service professionals to protect, serve, and lead our communities.

The School of Public Service Education at Indian River State College, under the direction of Executive Dean Louis Caprino, offers academic degree programs and real-world training pathways for careers in criminal justice, fire science, emergency medical services, and public service leadership. Explore your path to certification, degree completion, or advanced professional development — all from one central hub.

## Academic Degree Programs

These programs provide foundational knowledge and career pathways supported by hands-on experiences and real training opportunities at the College and the Training Complex.

- [Criminal Justice (A.A. / A.S. / B.S.)](https://irsc.edu/program/criminal-justice/)
- [Emergency Medical Services (A.S. / EMT Certification / Paramedic Certification)](https://irsc.edu/program/emergency-medical-services/ "Emergency Medical Services")
- [Fire Science (A.S. / Basic Fire Recruit Academy)](https://irsc.edu/program/fire-science/ "Fire Science")
- [Paralegal / Legal Assistant (A.S.)](https://irsc.edu/program/paralegal-studies/ "Paralegal Studies")
- [Public Administration (A.A. / B.S.)](https://irsc.edu/program/public-administration/ "Public Administration") — *100% Online Option Available*

## Treasure Coast Public Safety Training Complex

Located at 4600 Kirby Loop Road in Fort Pierce, Florida, the Treasure Coast Public Safety Training Complex is a 50-acre, state-of-the-art facility for criminal justice, fire, EMS, and public safety training.
