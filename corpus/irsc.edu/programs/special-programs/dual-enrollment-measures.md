# DUAL ENROLLMENT PLACEMENT MEASURES

When you join Indian River State College’s dual enrollment program, we’ll help you determine which classes fit in your academic journey.

Indian River State College uses standard placement measures to determine which English, reading and math courses you should take when you enroll.

These placement measures can include standardized test scores, high school coursework or other official test score reports. You must have your high school transcripts and official score reports sent directly from the organization to The River. We cannot accept the documentation otherwise.

## Performance in High School Coursework

### Full Credit Courses (1 Credit)

If you have an unweighted high school GPA of 3.0 and earn a grade of “B” or better in any of the full-credit (1 credit) courses listed below, you will have demonstrated readiness for college-level work.

### Half Credit Courses (0.5 Credit)

If you have an unweighted high school GPA of 3.0 and achieve a grade of “B” or better in two half-credit (0.5 credit) courses with the same name listed below, you will have demonstrated readiness for college-level work. Any grades that are not “B” or better will not demonstrate readiness for college-level work.

### Courses Taught at a Florida Public High School

- Mathematics
- Algebra 1/Algebra 1 Honors
- Algebra 2/Algebra 2 Honors
- Pre-Calculus
- Calculus
- Geometry
- Probability and Statistics
- Math for College Algebra
- Math for College Statistics
- Math for College Liberal Arts
- English Language and Literature
- English 4/English 4 Honors

### Accelerated High School Courses

- Mathematics
  - Advanced International Certificate of Education (AICE) high school mathematics courses
  - Advanced Placement (AP) high school mathematics courses
  - International Baccalaureate (IB) high school mathematics courses
- English Language and English Literature
  - Advanced International Certificate of Education (AICE) high school English courses
  - Advanced Placement (AP) high school English courses
  - International Baccalaureate (IB) high school English courses
