# COLLEGE READINESS PROGRAMS/TRIO

Reach your academic and career goals with the help of Indian River State College’s TRIO and college readiness programs.

If you’re from a low-income background, are the first in the family to attend college or have a disability, TRIO is here for you.

TRIO programs are designed to help you build confidence in your future. Through TRIO, you’ll receive academic and personal support so you’re ready for life in college and beyond.

## Free for eligible students

TRIO programs are provided at no cost to students who are eligible to participate. If you are accepted into a TRIO program, you will not have to pay.

## TRIO programs at Indian River State College

###

### **Student Support Services**

Get personalized support to explore your talents and abilities, helping you develop a vision for your life that includes academic and personal excellence.

Open to: Current and incoming Indian River State College students

[Apply to SSS](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/student-support-services-trio-program-application-2024-2025-1.pdf)

### **Educational Talent Search**

The TRIO Educational Talent Search (ETS) program helps middle and high school students achieve academic success and prepare for life after high school through academic advising, college and career exploration, financial aid guidance, and postsecondary planning.

Open to: Eligible middle and high school students attending participating target schools in St. Lucie County.

[St. Lucie County application](https://botform.compansol.com/240465967778072)

### **Upward Bound**

Upward Bound provides comprehensive academic support, college preparation, and personal development opportunities. Upward Bound’s program is year-round and includes a six-week intensive summer program.

Open to: High school students

[St. Lucie County Application](https://botform.compansol.com/230794975677982)

[Indian River County Application](https://botform.compansol.com/222355072209046)

### **Upward Bound Math and Science**

The Upward Bound Math and Science program is designed to inspire and support students in science, technology, engineering, and math (STEM) fields.

Open to: High school students from Fort Pierce Westwood Academy, Fort Pierce Central and Port St. Lucie High School.

[Apply to Upward Bound Math & Science](https://botform.compansol.com/210625123886859)
