# CAREER PATHWAYS PROGRAMS

Arduino Bootcamp (Molly Bartels/IRSC)

Get a head start on your future career through hands-on training and real workforce experience with Indian River State College’s Career Pathways Program.

Career Pathways are high school workforce training programs that lead to a high school diploma, industry certifications and future college credit. Our programs are designed to prepare you for entry-level positions in high-demand career fields and ensure you are ready for college.

## Earn Credit Toward Your College Degree

Students are eligible to earn 9 credits toward their degree at Indian River State College:

- Introduction to Business (3 credits)
- Essentials of Contemporary Leadership (3 credits)
- College Computing (3 credits) **or** Intro to Computer Application for Business (3 credits)

Most Career Pathway Completers also earn additional credits, depending on the career pathway program you choose.

## Open to Public School Students in Local Counties

Career Pathway Programs are open only to public high students in Indian River, Marin, Okeechobee, St. Lucie and Marion counties, thanks to articulation agreements between each county’s school district and The River.
