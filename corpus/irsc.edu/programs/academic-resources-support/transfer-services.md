# TRANSFER SERVICES

Transfer seamlessly to other colleges.

Looking to transfer into a bachelor’s degree program? Indian River State College offers many [bachelor’s programs](https://irsc.edu/program_type/bachelorsdegrees/ "Bachelor’s Degrees"), allowing you to continue your education without the need to transfer.

For students who do transfer, we have designed several of our associate and baccalaureate degrees to transfer seamlessly to other public and private universities.

And we maintain transfer agreements with colleges and universities throughout Florida and beyond. Our goal is to make it easy and affordable to earn a bachelor’s or post-graduate degree without losing credit for work you complete at Indian River State College.

Transferring can be a complicated process, and we are here to help. We offer transfer workshops on a regular basis via both face-to-face and online. We can provide support for your application process and help you find financial aid and scholarship sources.

## We’re Here for You

At Transfer services, we offer a comprehensive suite of resources and support to help students transition seamlessly to a Bachelor’s program at Indian River State College or postgraduate degree programs at other colleges and universities, by helping you transfer successfully. In addition to these resources, students can benefit from personalized transfer advising, assistance with applications, scholarship opportunities, and financial aid options. All services are available in-person, by phone, and online.

## Transfer Agreements

When you transfer to a new college or university, it is sometimes difficult to get credit for all the courses you have already completed. Students may even need to retake courses or earn more credits to enter as a junior.

Indian River State College makes this process easier through transfer agreements with many colleges and universities. These agreements cover specific associate and bachelor’s degrees. They spell out the courses you must complete successfully for a seamless transfer experience. They may also include waived fees and tuition discounts for Indian River State College grads.

Review the agreements for colleges that interest you. Then work with your advisor to make sure you take the right courses. Remember that if you change your major or program, courses taken for your previous program may not count toward your new program.

 Florida Atlantic University (FAU)

With the Indian River State College/FAU Link program, incoming freshman students have exclusive access to FAU.\* This initiative ensures an easy, worry-free transition to FAU to earn your bachelor’s degree:

- Guaranteed entrance to FAU: students graduating with A.A. degrees are guaranteed admission to FAU.\*
- Priority admissions to FAU: provides smooth transition through the FAU admissions process that guarantees a quick admission decision. Plus, you receive priority registration at FAU.
- Convenient classroom locations: FAU regional campuses located across south Florida: Boca Raton, Treasure Coast, Jupiter and Broward County
- Enriched academic advising: students receive comprehensive on-site advising from both Indian River State College and FAU advisors
- Small class sizes
- Access to the best faculty
- More bang for your buck

\*Consistent with university policy. Limited-access and restricted-access programs may require an additional admission process.

Once you have indicated your participation in the program, Indian River State College and FAU work together to prepare you for completion of your bachelor’s degree. Apply to Indian River State College, submit the [IRSC/FAU Link application](https://www.fau.edu/admissions/link2fau-irsc/), complete your Indian River State College admissions requirements, and meet with your assigned advisor. Apply to FAU during the term in which you will complete 30 credits at Indian River State College, complete your A.A. Degree and transfer to FAU.

 Florida International University (FIU)

IRSC and Florida International University (FIU) have established an articulation agreement for students in IRSC’s A.S. in Hospitality and Tourism Management program.

The agreement provides a defined transfer pathway into FIU’s Chaplin School of Hospitality & Tourism Management, where eligible IRSC graduates can complete a B.S. in Hospitality Management. The pathway aligns IRSC coursework with FIU requirements, helping students transfer with confidence, avoid unnecessary duplicate coursework, and complete their bachelor’s degree one semester sooner—saving more than $1,250 in tuition.

Students may complete the FIU bachelor’s program online or on FIU’s Miami campus. This is a program-specific pathway for Hospitality and Tourism Management students, not a general transfer agreement for all IRSC programs.

Check out the [Connect4Success](https://admissions.fiu.edu/how-to-apply/connect4success/index.html) webpage for details on the pathway.

## Transfer Resources

Transferring to another state college or university is a big decision. These resources help you understand Florida’s 2+2 admissions program and transfer student admission requirements, and offer access to college and graduate test prep materials and resources that can help you with college selection (catalogs, profiles and more).

- [College Source](https://cso.collegesource.com/) to view college catalogs, institutional profiles and related resources for colleges and universities nationwide
- [FastWeb](https://www.fastweb.com/) is a free scholarship search platform that connects students to college scholarships, trade school scholarships, financial aid news, and resources
- [Transferology](https://www.transferology.com/index.htm) lets you create a free account to view your college transfer options and see how your college-level classes transfer to over 400 schools nationwide
- [Florida Shines](https://www.floridashines.org/succeed-in-college/transfer-schools) to understand Florida’s 2+2 admission program requirements, specific transfer degree requirements by school, and the impact of changing schools and majors
- [Princeton Review](https://www.princetonreview.com/) for test prep resources, admissions counseling, and nationwide program and institution search
- [University of Florida Academic Advising Center](https://www.advising.ufl.edu/clas-transfer-center/prospective-students/) for prospective transfer student information, including admissions requirements and advising resources.
