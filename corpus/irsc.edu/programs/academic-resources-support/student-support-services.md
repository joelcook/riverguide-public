# STUDENT SUPPORT SERVICES

Empowering you to finish strong. We provide the dedicated support that builds confidence and ensures your college completion.

Students who succeed in college learn to advocate for themselves. They learn to become leaders. They aspire to academic and personal excellence.

These are all skills you can learn, and the Student Support Services program is here to help you learn them. Through academic, cultural, career-oriented and social activities, you will explore your personal talents and abilities.

Along the way, you will gain confidence, develop a vision for your life, and set the highest standards for yourself. You’ll develop a sense of community and social responsibility while you learn to use all the campus resources available to you.

Student Support Services is a federally funded TRIO program and is free of charge to eligible students.

## Program Activities

Our goal is to help students stay in college, graduate, and make the transition from one level of higher education to the next. To help you do that, we offer:

- Academic advising
- Personal and career counseling
- One-on-one and small-group tutoring
- Day trips to state colleges and universities
- Transfer assistance
- Cultural enrichment activities
- Volunteer opportunities and community service projects
- A learning lab with textbooks and other instructional resources
- Success workshops that help you build skills in studying, notetaking, time management and personal finance

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for the Student Support Services program, you must be:

- A current or prospective student
- Pursuing an associate degree or career training

And be one of the following:

- Low income
- A first-generation college student
- Disabled

## Apply for Student Support Services

Ready to reach your goals? Apply to be part of the program.

### Download the Application

Download the TRIO Student Support Services Application form and save it to your computer.

[Application](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/student-support-services-trio-program-application-2024-2025-1.pdf)

### Complete the Application

Fill out the form and don’t forget to save your work.

### Send in the Application

Email the completed form to [studentsupportservices@irsc.edu](mailto:studentsupportservices@irsc.edu).

### Have Your Intake Interview

If you qualify, we will contact you by phone and email to schedule an intake interview.
