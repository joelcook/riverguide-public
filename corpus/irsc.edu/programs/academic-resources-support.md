# ACADEMIC RESOURCES & SUPPORT

Whether you’re with us for a few months or a few years, and no matter if you’re attending on campus or online, Indian River State College is committed to your academic, professional, and personal success.

[**Access The River Line**](https://irsc.edu/admissions/qless/)

## Helping You Thrive

At Indian River State College, you’ll learn in a supportive, caring environment where everyone wants to see you thrive: In the classroom. In the labs and shops. On the job.

Whether you need assistance choosing classes, want some career guidance, or could use a little homework help, you’ll find a friendly expert ready to see you succeed.
