# PERSONAL ENRICHMENT COURSES

Unleash your creativity and learn new skills through our personal enrichment offerings. Choose from courses in photography and computer technology or explore exciting youth summer programs.

[See Courses and Register](https://rivercontinuinged.gosignmeup.com/Public/Course/Browse)

Personal enrichment course offerings include:

## Photography Courses

### Beginning Photography

This engaging course provides a clear and concise introduction to the fundamentals of photography, ideal for new camera owners and a valuable refresher for experienced photographers. Explore essential concepts and techniques to start capturing compelling images with confidence.

### Intermediate Photography

Elevate your photographic skills and discover your unique visual style. This course builds on Beginning Photography with additional instruction in camera operation while emphasizing the elements and principles of strong composition and effective post-production techniques. Explore creative possibilities with indoor, nighttime and auxiliary flash photography, as well as introductory studio lighting and portrait and still life lighting styles.

*Prerequisite: Beginning Photography*

### Advanced Photography

Designed for serious amateurs and professionals, this in-depth class explores advanced applications of various techniques using both film and digital equipment across different camera formats. Sharpen your technical proficiency through extensive hands-on practice and constructive critique.

*Prerequisites: Beginning and Intermediate Photography*

[Register Now](https://rivercontinuinged.gosignmeup.com/Public/Course/Browse)

## Computer and Technology Courses

### Excel – Basic to Intermediate

Build a strong foundation in Microsoft Excel. Gain comfort in the program’s core functions and progress to intermediate proficiency.

### Excel – Advanced

Take your Excel skills to an expert level. This advanced course focuses on using tables, pivot tables, macros, and sophisticated formulas and functions to perform complex operations. Learn to create 3D charts for better data visualization and master features like VLOOKUP, COUNTIF, IF, DATE, and NOW.
