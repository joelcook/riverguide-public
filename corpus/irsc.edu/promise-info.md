## **A LOCAL PROMISE THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING**

---

### **Free Tuition**

100% of in-state tuition covered for AA, AS, or AAS degrees.

(Excludes lab fees, books, supplies.)

### **Graduate in
2 Years**

Finish your degree in 6 terms or less.

### **Local Eligibility**

For 2026 grads from public, charter, approved private, or in-district homeschool programs in Indian River, Martin, Okeechobee, or
St. Lucie counties.

### **Broad Eligibility**

No income limits. Apply, meet deadlines, graduate, and start strong.

## **HOW IT WORKS**

### **1**
