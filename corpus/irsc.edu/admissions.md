# ADMISSIONS

At Indian River State College, we strive to make education accessible and affordable with an education plan tailored to you and your goals. Start your journey with us and find the support and care you need at every step of the way.

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

[Access The River Line](https://irsc.edu/admissions/qless/)

## Ready to thrive? We’re here to help get you started.

We’re so excited you’re considering applying to the River. We’ve designed our programs to be career-ready, giving you the skills and education you can apply on the job on day one. Plus, our financial aid options and low cost mean little or no debt after graduation. Our dedicated admissions and advising staff is ready to help you take the next step on your path to accomplishing your education goals.

### Why Choose Indian River State College?

- One of the most affordable colleges in the nation (Dept. of Education’s college affordability data)
- 91% of students graduate without any debt
- 42% of undergraduates receive grant or scholarship aid
- 69% of first-time, full-time students receive financial aid
- 25:1 student-to-faculty ratio

Get all of the info you need to apply, whether you’re a first-year, returning, transfer or international student.

- programs
- [Associate’s Degrees](https://irsc.edu/program_type/associatedegrees/ "Associate Degrees")—our 2-year A.A., A.A.S. and A.S. programs and Technical Certificates
- [Career Training and Apprenticeships](https://irsc.edu/program_type/careertrainingapprenticeshipprograms/ "Career Training & Apprenticeship Programs")—technical, vocational, and hands-on programs and certificates
- [Dual Enrollment](https://irsc.edu/2025/04/21/dual-enrollment/ "Dual Enrollment")—our program for eligible public, charter, private and home education students, who can earn credit toward both their high school diploma and an associate degree or career training certificate at the same time
