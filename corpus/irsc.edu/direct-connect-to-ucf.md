# DIRECTCONNECT TO UCF®

Indian River State College students now have a guaranteed\* path to the University of Central Florida through DirectConnect to UCF®. Complete your associate degree at The River and continue your academic journey with personalized support, streamlined transfer, and cost savings.

[Sign Up for DirectConnect to UCF®](https://directconnect.ucf.edu/?utm_source=theriver&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=dc_signup)

## Why Choose DirectConnect to UCF?

- **Guaranteed Admission** – Continue your studies at UCF after earning your A.A. or statewide articulated A.S. at The River. \*
- **Personal Success Team** – From day one, you’ll be supported by a dedicated team to guide your transition.
- **Access UCF Services Early** – Attend UCF transfer events, use student services, and get connected—before you transfer.
- **Save Money** – Complete your first two years at The River at a fraction of the cost of university tuition.
- **Faster Path to Graduation** – Stay on track with clear milestones and expert guidance.

\*Consistent with university policy. Admission to limited-access programs may require additional requirements.

## How It Works

### 1. Qualify

Enroll in an Associate of Arts (A.A.) or statewide articulated Associate of Science (A.S.) degree program at Indian River State College.

### 2. Sign Up

Visit the DirectConnect to UCF® website and sign up for the program to get started as early as possible.

### 3. Get Support

Meet your UCF Success Team, receive personalized guidance, and begin preparing for your next steps.

### 4. Apply to UCF

Once you’ve earned at least 45 college credits, begin the official UCF application process.

### 5. Transition Seamlessly

Attend orientation, register for classes, and continue your journey as a Knight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is DirectConnect to UCF®?

A pathway that guarantees admission\* to UCF for eligible The River graduates.

 Is DirectConnect different from a 2+2 transfer?

Yes. It includes built-in support services, earlier engagement, and a Success Team from UCF.

 Can I sign up if I’m a dual-enrolled student?

Yes. Signing up early helps you receive guidance throughout your time at The River.

 Is admission really guaranteed?

Yes, consistent with university policy and completion of your A.A. or statewide articulated A.S. at The River.\*
