## STUDENT RECORDS

## Order Official Transcripts

If you don’t have access to Workday:

1. Go to [Parchment.com](https://www.parchment.com/) (no Student ID number or password required)
2. Login if you already have an account, or click Order Now
3. Enter ‘Indian River State College’ in the Search bar
4. Follow the instructions

[Parchment’s Help Center](https://www.parchment.com/chat-support/) has a list of FAQ’s and Help Articles to assist you. If you need further assistance, contact [Parchment](https://www.parchment.com/chat-support/) directly.

If you have access to Workday:

1. Log into [The River Portal](https://go.irsc.edu/), and select Workday Student
2. Select Student Academics Hub
3. To send a transcript through the FASTER system to a Florida ***public*** college, university, or agency, choose the link that says **FASTER Transcript Order**
4. To send a transcript to yourself or any other individual or institution, choose the link that says **Parchment Transcript Order**

ATTENTION: If you are currently enrolled or have just completed your course(s), please verify that your grades/degrees/certificates are posted and correct in Workday BEFORE ordering transcripts. If your class has ended and your grades are not in Workday, please contact your instructor. If your degree or certificate has not been posted, contact Advising. You should also verify that you have no holds on your account prior to ordering.

Should you have any questions or need additional information, please log in to [The River Line](https://irsc.edu/admissions/qless/ "Qless")/Student Records for assistance.

## View Unofficial Transcript

1. Log into [The River Portal](https://go.irsc.edu/), and select Workday
2. Select Student Academics Hub, and then Generate Unofficial Transcript
3. Click the Confirm box, and then OK
4. Once the transcript is generated, you may print/save a copy for your own records

## Request a Name, SSN, or DOB Change

Name, social security number and date of birth can be changed in person at all Indian River State College campuses or by email at [records@irsc.edu](mailto:records@irsc.edu?subject=).

The Student Records Department must have a signed [Student Record Change form](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/student-record-change-fillable-form-IRSC-44-20223.pdf) from the student. The form must be accompanied by the required supporting documents and a photo ID.

## Obtain an Education Verification

To verify your enrollment for auto insurance, health insurance, housing, scholarships, or for any other reason, you may use the National Student Clearinghouse’s Student Self-Service site accessed through [The River Portal](https://go.irsc.edu/). If you qualify, the site will provide you with an official Enrollment Certificate or Good Student Discount Certificate free of charge. Additional information about your loans and enrollment can also be viewed from this site. To access it, click on the Student Academics Hub from your Workday student account homepage. On the left side, click on Verifications. You will be redirected to the Clearinghouse’s self-service site.

## Sending Transcripts to Indian River State College

Among the supporting documents required for admission to Indian River State College are official, sealed transcripts from the high school that you graduated from, as well as any colleges or universities that you have attended. We prefer to have transcripts sent to us via FASTER or other electronic delivery services like Parchment or the National Student Clearinghouse.

## Contact Student Records

The Student Records Department is located in the W-Building on Massey Campus in Fort Pierce. Contact us via The River Line/Student Records or [records@irsc.edu](mailto:records@irsc.edu).
