# INSTITUTIONAL REVIEW BOARD

Indian River State College is committed to safeguarding the welfare of human subjects by ensuring compliance with all federal regulations governing the protection of human subjects and facilitating the research efforts of our faculty, students and staff. Additional questions concerning the board can be directed to [IRB@irsc.edu](mailto:IRB@irsc.edu).
