# Statement on Academic and Operational Excellence

Indian River State College is committed to providing an affordable, high-quality education that empowers students to achieve their academic goals, enter rewarding careers, and strengthen the Treasure Coast.

In response to U.S. Secretary of Education Linda McMahon’s National Call to Action to colleges and universities, the College reaffirms its longstanding commitment to academic excellence, transparency, accountability, student success, and responsible stewardship of public resources. As a proud member of the Florida College System, Indian River State College prepares students for success through rigorous academic programs, workforce training, transfer pathways, and partnerships that support the economic vitality of Florida’s Treasure Coast and beyond. The College remains focused on delivering measurable outcomes, expanding opportunity, and ensuring that higher education remains accessible, valuable, and responsive to the needs of students, employers, and communities.

## Transparency and Student Access

Indian River State College is dedicated to providing clear, accurate information about admissions requirements, academic programs, tuition and fees, financial aid opportunities, transfer pathways, and career outcomes. As an institution that values access and opportunity, the College helps students make informed decisions about their educational future while maintaining high expectations for academic progress and achievement.
Our mission extends beyond access. We are committed to helping every student translate educational opportunity into meaningful credentials, successful transfer outcomes, rewarding careers, and lifelong learning.

## Free Expression and Open Inquiry

The College supports the free exchange of ideas and respectful dialogue as essential components of higher education. Students, faculty, and staff are encouraged to engage thoughtfully with diverse viewpoints in an environment that promotes intellectual curiosity, civil discourse, and mutual respect.
Indian River State College is committed to maintaining a safe and productive learning environment where lawful expression is protected and where instruction, research, student activities, and campus operations can continue without disruption.

## Academic Excellence and Innovation

Academic excellence is the foundation of the College’s mission. Indian River State College invests in outstanding faculty, rigorous curricula, innovative teaching practices, and continuous assessment to ensure student learning and achievement.

Through degree programs, technical education, workforce certifications, and applied learning experiences, the College equips students with the knowledge, critical thinking skills, and practical experience needed to succeed in a rapidly evolving economy. Academic programs are regularly evaluated to ensure they remain relevant, rigorous, and aligned with employer needs, transfer expectations, and professional standards.

## Affordability, Value, and Student Success

Providing exceptional value remains a core priority. Indian River State College works to keep higher education affordable while expanding access to scholarships, financial aid, and student support services.

The College continually evaluates student outcomes, workforce alignment, and program effectiveness to ensure students receive a strong return on their educational investment. By focusing on completion, career readiness, transfer success, and economic mobility, the College helps students achieve their goals while meeting the workforce needs of the region and state.

## Workforce Development and Community Impact

For more than six decades, Indian River State College has served as a catalyst for workforce development, economic growth, and community advancement. Through partnerships with business, industry, government, healthcare providers, and community organizations, the College develops programs that respond to current and emerging workforce demands.

The College remains committed to preparing the next generation of leaders, innovators, educators, healthcare professionals, public servants, and skilled workers who will contribute to the prosperity and well-being of Florida and the nation.

## Responsible Stewardship and Public Trust

Indian River State College recognizes the responsibility entrusted to public institutions of higher education. The College is committed to transparency, accountability, fiscal responsibility, and continuous improvement in support of students and taxpayers.

By maintaining a focus on academic quality, student achievement, workforce relevance, and public service, Indian River State College will continue to advance its mission and uphold the trust placed in it by students, families, employers, and the communities it serves.

Indian River State College remains steadfast in its commitment to transforming lives through education and creating opportunities that strengthen our region, state, and nation for generations to come.
