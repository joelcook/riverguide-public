# DISTRICT BOARD OF TRUSTEES

The Indian River State College District Board of Trustees is composed of nine members appointed by the Governor of Florida. Three members are from St. Lucie County and two members each are from Indian River, Martin, and Okeechobee Counties. To see upcoming board meetings, please see our [events page](https://connect.irsc.edu/).

## Board Agendas and Minutes

In our ongoing commitment to transparency and student-centered governance, Indian River State College makes Board meeting agendas available to the public one week prior to each scheduled meeting. Official meeting minutes are reviewed and confirmed at the subsequent Board meeting. Once approved, they are promptly posted here for public access. This process ensures that our community remains informed and engaged as we continue to make decisions that shape the future of The River.
