# VISIT US

Visit **Indian River State College (The River)** and experience our campuses across Florida’s Treasure Coast. Whether you schedule an **IRSC campus tour in person** or explore online, visiting campus is one of the best ways to see what makes The River special.

During a visit, you can explore classrooms, meet students, and learn about programs that support your goals. In addition, campus tours help future students understand what college life is really like before enrolling.

Our campuses serve students throughout **Fort Pierce, Port St. Lucie, Stuart, Vero Beach, and Okeechobee**. Because each location offers different programs and resources, students can choose the campus that best supports their academic and career plans.

**Massey Campus — Fort Pierce**

The **Massey Campus in Fort Pierce** is the primary campus of Indian River State College and serves as the central hub for academic programs and student services.

Students will find:

- Comprehensive degree programs
- Student success services
- Libraries and learning labs
- Campus dining and student spaces
