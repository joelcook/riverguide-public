  ## **AI Strategic Leadership Bootcamp**

  Live, Instructor-Led Executive Short Course

  💵 Tuition: $85.00
  📅 Program Length: 1 week
  🕔 Sessions: 2 live sessions
  ⏱️ Total Course Hours: 4 hours
  ⏱️ Session Length: 2 hours

  ### Lead AI adoption with greater clarity and confidence.

  AI Strategic Leadership Bootcamp is a focused executive course designed for C-suite executives, senior leaders, and professionals responsible for guiding organizational strategy and change. No technical background is required.

  Through two live, instructor-led sessions, participants will strengthen their understanding of artificial intelligence, assess their organization’s readiness, identify valuable AI opportunities, and develop a practical approach to responsible AI adoption.

  Topics include:

  - Artificial intelligence fundamentals for leaders
  - Generative AI, large language models, and AI agents
  - Evaluating organizational readiness for AI
  - Prompt engineering for strategic briefs and executive communications
  - Identifying and prioritizing valuable AI opportunities
  - Evaluating whether to build or purchase AI solutions
  - Return on investment and organizational impact
  - AI-supported leadership and decision-making
  - Leading AI-enabled teams
  - Organizational change management
  - AI governance, ethics, risk, and regulation

  Participants will create and present a personal 90-day AI leadership strategy based on their organization’s goals, opportunities, risks, and readiness.

  This is a synchronous course. Participants must attend both scheduled sessions in real time to participate in instructor-led discussions, guided assessments, strategic exercises, and the final leadership strategy presentation.

  ## **AI for Human Resources**

  Live, Instructor-Led Short Course

  💵 Tuition: $125.00
  📅 Program Length: 1 week
  🕔 Sessions: 3 live sessions
  ⏱️ Total Course Hours: 6 hours
  ⏱️ Session Length: 2 hours

  ### Bring practical AI into everyday HR work.

  AI for Human Resources is a hands-on course designed to help HR professionals use artificial intelligence throughout the employee lifecycle. No coding background is required.

  During three live, instructor-led sessions, participants will explore how AI can support recruitment, employee communication, policy development, routine HR processes, and other people-focused responsibilities.

  Topics include:

  - The role of AI in modern human resources
  - Prompt engineering for HR responsibilities
  - Creating job descriptions and recruitment materials
  - Developing interview guides and evaluation materials
  - Drafting policies and employee communications
  - Building AI-assisted recruitment workflows
  - Exploring AI tools designed for HR functions
  - Automating routine HR activities
  - Evaluating AI tools for workplace use
  - Responsible use of AI in employee-related work
  - Privacy, accuracy, fairness, and bias considerations

  Participants will complete an applied project by selecting a real HR challenge and developing an AI-supported toolkit or workflow to address it.

  This is a synchronous course. Participants must attend the scheduled sessions in real time to take part in live demonstrations, guided activities, discussions, and the final project presentation.

  [Register Here](https://rivercontinuinged.gosignmeup.com/public/Course/browse?courseid=26815)

  ## **Junior AI Engineer**

  Live, Instructor-Led Certificate Program

  💵 Tuition: $250.00
  📅 Program Length: 4 weeks
  🕔 Sessions: 12 live sessions
  ⏱️ Total Course Hours: 24 hours

  ### Turn AI concepts into working solutions.

  Junior AI Engineer is a four-week certificate program that provides practical experience with artificial intelligence, generative AI, AI agents, machine learning, and data science.

  Through live instruction, demonstrations, guided activities, and hands-on projects, students will learn how AI systems work and how they can be used to create applications, automate workflows, and work with data.

  Topics include:

  - Artificial intelligence fundamentals
  - Context engineering
  - Generative AI and chatbot development
  - Retrieval-augmented generation
  - Workflow automation and AI agents
  - Data preparation and visualization
  - Machine-learning fundamentals
  - Deep-learning fundamentals
  - Responsible AI practices
  - Building and deploying AI-powered applications

  Students will complete a capstone project by building and deploying an AI-powered application.

  This is a synchronous program. Students must attend the scheduled sessions in real time to participate in instructor demonstrations, guided technical activities, project support, and the final capstone.

  - Have academic and research training opportunities at The River’s Center for Laser and Fiber Optics Education

  ## AVAILABLE PROGRAMS

  - Associate in Science in Electronics Engineering Technology
  - Solar Energy Technician
  - Lasers and Photonics
  - Robotics and Simulation Technician
  - Basic Electronics Certificate
  - Electronic Technology Certificate

  ## Associate in Science in Electronics Engineering Technology

  Associate’s Degree

  The demand for electronic engineering technicians is at an all-time high as the world’s technology production increases. Any advanced or high-tech field—such as robots and automation systems—relies on electronic components and circuitry. In our program, you’ll learn to install, service, maintain and repair electronic components and systems.

  With your degree, you’ll be prepared for jobs in telecommunications, medicine, manufacturing, national defense, space exploration, nanotechnology and more.

  [Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/electronics-engineering-technology/associate-in-science-degree-in-electronics-engineering-technology)

  ## Solar Energy Technician

  Technical Certificate

  Learn the fundamentals of installing and repairing solar power systems and gain a deeper understanding of how solar technologies work.

  Indian River State College is a leader in solar energy technician training. We are a recognized NABCEP Associate Solar Heating Training Provider, which recognizes our quality instruction in renewable energy.

  [Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/electronics-engineering-technology/solar-energy-technician/)

  ## Lasers and Photonics

  Technical Certificate

  Explore the role that light plays in the world of technology and electronics with our lasers and photonics certificate. Learn how to manipulate light to create lasers and other tools that rely on optical technologies.

  [Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/electronics-engineering-technology/lasers-and-photonics-certificate/)

  ## Robotics and Simulation Technician

  Technical Certificate

  As robotics plays a larger role in manufacturing and everyday life, technicians are in high demand. Learn how to create, manufacture and program robots for a range of industries.

  [Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/electronics-engineering-technology/robotics-and-simulation-technician/)

  ## Basic Electronics Certificate

  Technical Certificate

  Explore the foundations of electronics with courses in DC and AC circuits, electronic components and the role of light in electronic engineering.

  [Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/electronics-engineering-technology/basic-electronics-certificate/)

  ## Electronic Technology Certificate

  Technical Certificate

  Gain a broader education in electronics technology with this certificate, which combines courses from the electronics engineering technology program and other certificates. Explore circuits, electronic devices, robotics and more.

  [Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/electronics-engineering-technology/electronic-technology-certificate/)

  # BIOLOGY

  Gain a deeper understanding of life and living organisms with a degree in biology from Indian River State College. Learn how the study of biology can help us solve problems for humans, animals, plants and the environment.

  [Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

  [Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

  ## THE RIVER…

  - Is one of the 10 best colleges for a bachelor’s degree in biology, based on tuition, median salaries, and median debt
  - Is where you can earn a top return on your investment for your bachelor’s in biology
  - Lets you explore research and advanced studies in our state-of-the-art facilities with sophisticated labs and equipment

  ## AVAILABLE PROGRAMS

  - Associate in Arts
  - Associate in Arts – Preprofessional
  - Bachelor of Science in Biology
  - Certificate of Professional Preparation (CPP) in Biomedical Sciences

  ## Associate in Arts

  Associate’s Degree

  The associate’s degree biology track provides a foundation for a career in a range of fields and industries. The associate degree program is designed to transfer into the Bachelor of Science in biology program at The River or another Florida public university.

  [Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/biology-general-track/)

  ## Associate in Arts – Preprofessional

  Associate’s Degree

  If you want to become a doctor or other medical professional, choose the preprofessional track in our associate degree program. This degree track is designed to transfer into the Bachelor of Science in Biology program at The River or another Florida public university.

  [Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/biology-preprofessional-track/)

  ## Bachelor of Science in Biology

  Bachelor’s Degree

  Earn your bachelor’s degree in biology and open doors to a wide range of careers in health, medicine, research, the environment, agriculture, education and more.  You can explore many different fields of biology at The River, including biotechnology, microbiology, genetics, plant or marine biology, and ecology.

  [Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-program-in-biology/bachelor-of-science-degree-in-biolog/)

  ## Certificate of Professional Preparation (CPP) in Biomedical Sciences

  Certificate of Professional Preperation

  This program is open only to students who have already earned their Bachelor of Science degree in biology. You may be eligible for transfer credit if you attended another college for your degree. We will evaluate your transcript to determine if any equivalent courses can apply to your certification from The River.

  If you want to attend medical school or another doctoral professional school, the Certificate of Professional Preparation (CPP) in Biomedical Sciences can help you get there. This program will prepare you for the challenging graduate school curriculum and required entrance exams with courses in biochemistry, genetics, immunology and human anatomy.

  [Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/certificate-of-professional-preparation)
