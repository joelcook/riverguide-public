# TRANSIT

At Indian River State College, we know that getting to class, work, and campus events shouldn’t be a barrier to your success. That’s why The River partners with local agencies to provide **free or low-cost transit options** across our four-county service area. Whether you’re traveling between campuses or commuting from home, there’s a solution to help you get where you need to go.

## Fixed-Route Bus Service (ART & GoLine)

Students can ride area buses that connect directly to multiple Indian River State College campuses.

Massey Campus (Fort Pierce)

- ART Route 3 – Eastbound: arrives every hour at :37 past the hour
- GoLine Route 15: provides service from Indian River County to Massey Campus with arrivals at 7:30, 8:30, 9:30 a.m. and 3:30, 4:30, 5:30 p.m.

Pruitt Campus (St. Lucie West)

- ART Route 6 – Westbound: arrives every hour at :55 past the hour

English as a Second Language Center

- ART Route 6 – Eastbound: arrives every hour at :41 past the hour

Connections to other Treasure Coast Routes

- ART Route 7 connects with Indian River transit
- ART Route 1 meets Martin County routes at Walmart and Treasure Coast Mall

[View maps](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ART-FR-Zone-123-Freebee39.pdf) and schedules: [GoLine Transit](https://golineirt.com/) | [ART Rider Guide](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ART-Rider-Guide.pdf)

## Rides-On-Demand (Microtransit Service)

When the fixed-route bus doesn’t quite fit your schedule, try Rides-On-Demand (formerly ART-On-Demand).

- Book a ride through the mobile app or by phone.
- Vans will pick you up and drop you off within the service area.
- Perfect for shorter trips between campuses or to connect with a main bus route.

Download instructions and QR codes available in [English](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rides-on-Demand-Getting-Started-Brochure-Design-File-English.pdf), [Spanish](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rides-on-Demand-Getting-Started-Brochure-Design-File-Spanish.pdf), and [Creole](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rides-on-Demand-Getting-Started-Brochure-Design-File-Creole.pdf).

## Freebee Service

Freebee offers free, on-demand rides in select zones using eco-friendly electric vehicles.

- Request a ride through the Freebee app.
- Available in parts of St. Lucie County and Indian River County.
- Great for quick trips to nearby destinations around campus.

Download instructions and QR codes available in [English](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ART-Trifold-Brochure-English-11-5-24-v1.pdf), [Spanish](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ART-Trifold-Brochure-Spanish-11-5-24-v1.pdf), and [Creole](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ART-Trifold-Brochure-Creole-11-5-24-v1.pdf).
