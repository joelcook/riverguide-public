# LIFE ON CAMPUS

Your life at the River goes way beyond academics.

## There’s always something happening on campus at the River.

When our students aren’t in class, you’ll find them grabbing a bite with friends, participating in student organizations, working out at the gym or pool, and much more.

We welcome the Treasure Coast community to campus, too. Join us as we cheer on our championship athletics, catch theater performances, and look up in wonder at our planetarium.

## Housing and Dining

You’ll find great on-campus dining options at our Massey, Dixon Hendry, and Pruitt campuses. Find anything from homestyle favorites to grilled delights to vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options. Tired of commuting? Check out our apartment-style housing.

[Housing and Dining](https://irsc.edu/student-life/life-campus/housing-dining/)

## Arts and Culture

Arts and culture at the River enrich our students and enhance our community. You’ll find theater, music, lecture series, planetarium programs, student organizations, and more.

[Arts and Culture](https://irsc.edu/student-life/life-campus/arts-culture/)

## Athletics and Recreation

Whether you want to compete for a national championship or just stay in shape, there’s a place for you at the River. Enjoy intramural sports, an aquatics complex, a workout facility, and racquetball, tennis, and basketball courts.

[Athletics and Recreation](https://irsc.edu/student-life/life-campus/athletics-recreation/)
