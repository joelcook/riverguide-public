# CLUBS AND ORGANIZATIONS

With more than 50 student-run organizations, there’s no shortage of ways to make friends, have fun and gain leadership experience at The River.

## Find Your Flow at The River

Make friends. Create memories. Sharpen your leadership skills. Indian River State College is home to 50+ student groups and a jam-packed calendar of events and activities.

Whether you want to perform, play, lead, serve or relax, there’s a group or space for you. Get started by exploring RiverLife Powered by Lounge, our online database. From there, you can connect with others and find ways to get involved.

[Explore Clubs and Organizations](https://lounge.live/)

## MORE WAYS TO ENGAGE

Your experience at The River is what you make it. Dive in, meet new people, and build memories that last a lifetime. Discover events, activities, and student groups that help you grow, lead, and belong.

[Explore RiverLife Powered by Lounge](https://lounge.live/)

## Student Government Association

Help shape the student experience at The River while gaining leadership experience. SGA joins together representatives from across the college at all program types and degree levels.

[Discover SGA](https://irsc.campuslabs.com/engage/organization/sga)

## Honor Societies

These academic and leadership groups recognize outstanding achievement and offer an array of professional development opportunities.

[Learn More](https://irsc.edu/student-experience/honor-societies/)

## First Year Experience (FYE)

FYE begins with New Student Orientation and lasts throughout the year. Through these experiences, you’ll get to know other students and learn more about yourself.
