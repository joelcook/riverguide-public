# HOUSING AND DINING

Eat, sleep, unwind, and save time with on-campus housing and dining.

Your days on campus at the River are busy. Our on-campus dining options let you unwind and save time while getting the nutritious meals you need to fuel your studies. For the ultimate in convenience and community, check out our apartment-style student housing.

## Commuting to Campus

Most of our students commute to one of our five, conveniently located campuses. You’ll find that parking is abundant and several campuses are served by public transportation, including bus service from [Indian River County GoLine](https://www.golineirt.com/) and [St. Lucie County Area Regional Transit](https://www.stlucieco.gov/departments-and-services/area-regional-transit).

## River Hammock Student Housing

River Hammock is an apartment-style student residence complex open only to Indian River State College students. Located on our Massey campus in Fort Pierce, it offers convenience and comfort at a price you can easily afford

We designed these beautiful apartments with you in mind. Each features a central living area, dining room and kitchen. Four individual bedrooms with two full baths provide privacy and an outstanding environment for studying. Each apartment includes a complete kitchen appliance package, central heat and air conditioning, telephone and cable TV outlets, and deadbolt locks. Your monthly rent includes utilities and Wi-Fi.

The Alto “Bud” Adams Jr. River Hammock is owned and operated by the Indian River State College Foundation, Inc. Get more information and lease forms at the Housing Office in the small annex building south of the gymnasium (Building P) on the Massey campus.

## DINING ON CAMPUS

No one should learn on an empty stomach. You’ll find on-campus dining options at our Massey, Dixon Hendry, and Pruitt campuses. They don’t just offer great food—they’re great places to study and socialize.

Visit our dining website for more information about dining options, menus, and hours.

[Dining Menu and Hours](https://www.indianrivermetz.com/)

## The River Café

The River Café, located in the Koblegard Student Union on the Massey campus, offers breakfast, lunch, beverages and snacks every weekday. Vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options are available.

## The Landing Café

Take a break from classes or research at The Landing Café on the Massey campus. You’ll find Starbucks hot and cold coffees, espresso lattes, frozen drinks, and seasonal favorites.

## The Perch Café at Okeechobee

Located on the Dixon Hendry campus. The Perch Café at Okeechobee serves Starbucks handcrafted hot and cold drinks.

## The Perch Café at Pruitt

Located in Building J, The Perch is your one-stop shop for fresh, made-to-order food throughout the week. Specialty sandwiches, house-made flatbreads, fresh salads, smoothies, and more.
