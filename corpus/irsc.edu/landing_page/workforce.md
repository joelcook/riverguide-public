## EXPLORE PROGRAMS THAT FIT YOUR LIFE

The River’s workforce programs are built with local industry in mind, so every course helps you gain practical skills, earn in-demand credentials, and step into opportunity quickly.

Not sure which path is right for you? We’ll help you find your flow in a program that fits your life and schedule.

### **Advanced Manufacturing**

Step into the future of manufacturing with training in automation, CNC machining, and more. Our partnerships with local employers connect your skills directly to job opportunities.
