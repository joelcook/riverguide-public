## A Trusted FMCSA Training Provider

Indian River State College is an approved training provider listed in FMCSA’s Training Provider Registry. We follow all Federal Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) requirements and go beyond them with extensive behind-the-wheel instruction.

Our curriculum is designed to make graduates job-ready for the demands of the road. We take pride in producing graduates who are highly regarded by employers across the U.S. for their strong preparation and practical training.

## **Already Have a CDL? Add School Bus & Passenger Training**

Expand your skills with School Bus and Passenger Vehicle training designed for drivers who already hold a CDL.

Quick details:

- Cost: $800
- 4 days of training, with testing the following week
- Curriculum aligned with 49 CFR part 380.503
