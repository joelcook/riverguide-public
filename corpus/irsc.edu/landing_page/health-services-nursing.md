## **EXPLORE AVAILABLE PROGRAMS**

### **Associate in Science Degree in Nursing (RN)**

2-year traditional track and 1.5-year “Bridge to RN” track

### **Bachelor of Science in Nursing (RN to BSN)**

10-course online program with clinical learning experiences

### **Practical Nursing (LPN)**

1.5-year program that prepares students to sit for the NCLEX-PN exam

## **With The River, You Can:**
