## FIND THE PROGRAM THAT FITS YOU

Whether you’re starting fresh or building on experience, discover hands-on healthcare programs designed to match your goals.

**Healthcare Management**

Prepare to lead in hospitals, clinics, and care systems with a flexible program that blends business skills and healthcare expertise.

[Explore Program](https://irsc.edu/program/healthcare-management/)

 **Health Services Management**

Gain the knowledge to oversee health operations and support patient-focused services—perfect for career growth or a fresh start.

[Explore Program](https://irsc.edu/program/health-services-management/)

 **Medical Assisting**

Jump into patient care with hands-on training in clinical and administrative skills. This fast-track program opens doors quickly.

[Explore Program](https://irsc.edu/program/medical-assisting/)

 **Medical Laboratory Technology**

Run the lab behind the care. Learn to perform critical diagnostic tests and work with cutting-edge equipment.

[Explore Program](https://irsc.edu/program/medical-laboratory-technology/)

 **Nursing**

Earn your RN and start making a difference immediately—with clinical experience and career-ready confidence.

[Explore Program](https://irsc.edu/program/nursing/)

 **Pharmacy Technician**

Train for a high-demand role in pharmacies and hospitals. Learn to prepare medications, manage inventory, and support patient safety.

[Explore Program](https://irsc.edu/program/pharmacy-technician/)

 **Phlebotomy**

Master the skill of blood collection and become an essential part of diagnostic care. Quick training. Lasting impact.

[Explore Program](https://irsc.edu/program/phlebotomy/)

 **Physical Therapist Assistant**

Help people move better, feel better, and recover faster. Graduate ready to work side-by-side with licensed PTs.

[Explore Program](https://irsc.edu/program/physical-therapist-assistant/)
