## **Transform Spaces with Intention**

With our interior design program, learn how to create practical, visually appealing spaces for residential and commercial clients. Build technical skills, sharpen your eye for detail, and gain real-world experience through internships, field trips, guest speakers, and a hands-on thesis project.

## **Choose a Path That Fits Your Goals**

Earn your associate degree in 2 years, or complete a technical certificate and jumpstart your specialized career in kitchen/bath design or home staging. After completing our program, you’ll gain the core skills you need to sit for the National Interior Design Examination and apply for your professional licensure in Florida. Discover creative roles in architecture firms, design agencies, or individual consulting.
