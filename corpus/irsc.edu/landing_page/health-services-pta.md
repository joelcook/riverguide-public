## **Explore a High-Demand Role in Healthcare**

Open the door to a rewarding career in healthcare as a physical therapist assistant. Through hands-on patient care experience, students will learn how to provide pain relief and restore mobility under clinical supervision. Start your journey at The River today and gain the skills to assist patients confidently on their road to recovery.

## **Move Forward With a PTA Degree**

Our 2-year Physical Therapist Assistant degree will prepare you to take the National Physical Therapist Assistant Exam (NPTE-PTA). Earn your PTA license and go on to perform rehabilitation work in hospitals, physical therapy offices, skilled nursing facilities, and home healthcare settings.
