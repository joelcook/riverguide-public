# STUDENT LIFE

At The River, your education extends beyond the classroom with diverse student life opportunities, fostering leadership, personal growth, and community engagement that prepare you for success both inside and outside of the classroom.

At Indian River State College, students have countless ways to get involved and enrich their college experience. With over 50 clubs and organizations, students can build leadership skills, make lasting friendships, and create unforgettable memories. From connecting with Peer Leaders in the First Year Experience program to engaging in club activities, joining the Student Government Association (SGA), participating in volunteer opportunities, attending wellness workshops, and more, there’s something for everyone. Whether you’re enjoying student socials or exploring leadership pathways, you’ll find a perfect balance of academics, personal growth, and professional development.

Make your time at Indian River State College truly exceptional!

## First Year Experience Program

The First Year Experience (FYE) Program helps first-time, degree-seeking students adjust to college life. With support for academics, personal growth, and student involvement, the program offers workshops, events, and one-on-one guidance from a Peer Leader—an upper-level college student who provides guidance navigating college life. This program helps students build a strong foundation for their college journey and future success.

## Student Government Association (SGA)

The Student Government Association (SGA) at Indian River State College represents all students and advocates for their needs. SGA organizes events, supports student clubs, and works closely with college leadership to ensure student voices count in college decision-making. Student Government is a great way to get involved, give back,  make an impact, and develop leadership skills.

## Student Leadership Programs

The River Pathways program is a free student leadership opportunity at Indian River State College designed to help students build valuable skills for success. Through various paths focused on career readiness, community service, digital literacy, and leadership development, students gain hands-on experience, mentorship, and the tools they need to thrive professionally.

Students have the flexibility to complete one or more paths of their choosing in 2 years or less. In return, they earn cord(s) for graduation, certificates of completion, and a letter of recommendation from the Associate Vice President of Student Life!

## FL-SARA Complaint Process

All students are encouraged to work through the Ombudsman/Student Advocate and the internal complaint and appeal process provided by Indian River State College. However, if students feel further investigation is warranted, they can contact The Florida Department of Education at <https://www.fldoe.org/schools/higher-ed/fl-college-system/about-us/concerns-complaints.stml>.

Distance Education students who have completed an applicable internal institutional grievance process and applicable state grievance process may also appeal non-instructional complaints to the FL-SARA PRDEC Council. For additional information on the complaint process, [visit FL-SARA Complaint Process](https://www.fldoe.org/sara/student-concerns.stml).
