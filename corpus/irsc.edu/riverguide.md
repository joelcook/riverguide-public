# Understand Your River Guide Degree Audit

Your River Guide Degree Audit helps you see how your courses count toward your program, track your progress, and identify requirements you still need to complete. Use this guide to explore your audit and prepare for a conversation with your assigned academic advisor.

###

1. Log in to your [River Portal](https://go.irsc.edu/?_gl=1*l9s763*_ga*ODQwMDEwMjI2LjE3NDQxMzU4NTE.*_ga_92DLFN5PBG*czE3OTIxNTUxMDIkbzQwNyRnMSR0MTc5MjE2NjM5NiRqNjAkbDAkaDA.*_gcl_au*MTQ1OTMzNDg5LjE3ODQ1NzYzODU.).
2. Select the River Guide Degree Audit tile.

###

The first section of your audit shows your student information, including your:

- Student ID
- Assigned academic advisor
- Account holds
- Academic standing

If you are working toward more than one program, use the **Degree** dropdown to select and view the audit for each program.

Use the Degree dropdown to switch between programs.

###

The second section shows your cumulative GPA and the percentage of your degree requirements and credits completed.

Keep In-progress classes and Preregistered classes checked to include your current courses and any future courses you have registered for in the audit.

Keep both class options checked to include current and future registered courses.

###

The third section summarizes the requirements you must meet to complete your program.

If a requirement has not been met, follow the link on the right side of that requirement for more information and options for completing it.

Follow the links beside unmet requirements to explore what you still need to complete.

###

Expand the sections below the program summary to review individual requirements, completed courses, grades, credits earned, and how your credits apply to your degree. These sections also identify credits that have not been applied to your program.

Completed requirements

For a completed requirement, you can review the course code and title, grade earned, number of credits, term completed, and whether the course was repeated.

 Incomplete requirements

For an incomplete requirement, select a linked course code to learn more about a course that can fulfill it.

Expand a section to see its requirements and course information.

Review completed coursework and explore linked courses for unmet requirements.

###

Use the symbols beside each requirement to check its status:

- Green check: Complete. The requirement has been met.
- Blue symbol: In progress. The legend labels this status as complete with classes in progress or preregistered.
- Red circle: Not complete. The requirement has not been met.
- Yellow symbol: Nearly complete. See your advisor.

The legend also uses **@** for any class number and **(R)** for a repeated class. You can find the full legend at the bottom of your audit.

Refer to the legend at the bottom of your audit for symbol definitions.

###

Students planning to graduate must submit a graduation application through the River Guide Degree Audit card in the New River Portal.

To apply:

- Log in to the new [River Portal](https://go.irsc.edu/?_gl=1*1iq6wfe*_ga*ODQwMDEwMjI2LjE3NDQxMzU4NTE.*_ga_92DLFN5PBG*czE3OTIxNjk2MzkkbzQwOCRnMSR0MTc5MjE3MDkxMSRqNjAkbDAkaDA.*_gcl_au*MTQ1OTMzNDg5LjE3ODQ1NzYzODU.)
- Select the River Guide Degree Audit card
- Complete and submit your graduation application

Important: Submitting a graduation application does not guarantee that your degree or certificate will be awarded. Your academic record must be reviewed to confirm that all graduation requirements have been successfully satisfied.
