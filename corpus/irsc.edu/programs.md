# ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

Explore 100+ programs designed to unlock your potential.

[View All Programs](https://irsc.edu/search-programs/)

Ready to transform your future? The River offers career-focused programs to help you reach your goals. Options range from bachelor’s and associate degrees to technical certificates and career training.

Opportunities like internships, capstone projects, and service-learning help you graduate job-ready. Whether you’re aiming to climb the ladder or explore a new path, we’re here to support you every step of the way.
