# ECONOMICS

The River’s economics track can help you prepare for a career in business, finance, data analysis, policy, or research—any field that values an understanding of how markets and people interact.

## THE RIVER CAN HELP YOU…

- Grasp key concepts in macroeconomics and microeconomics, including supply and demand, market structures, fiscal and monetary policies, and economic indicators.​
- Meet the requirements and foundational knowledge required to transfer to a four-year institution.​
- Use statistical methods and mathematical models to analyze economic data and interpret real-world economic phenomena.​

## Economics

Associate in Arts

Through the Economics track of the River’s Associate in Arts degree, you’ll gain a solid foundation in economic theory, quantitative analysis, and critical thinking. Courses cover essential topics such as macroeconomics, microeconomics, statistics and accounting, equipping you with the skills to analyze complex economic issues and make informed decisions.

Upon completing the program, you can transfer your credits to a four-year baccalaureate program at a university to continue your studies. In addition to work in the private sector, economists find rewarding careers in federal, state, and local government agencies.

## WHY STUDY AT THE RIVER?

### **Guaranteed Transfer**

Complete your first two years of college at Indian River State College, and you’re guaranteed the right to transfer as a junior to a Bachelor’s Degree program at a Florida public university—or continue in a Bachelor’s Degree program right here at the River.

### **Flexible Learning**

We offer course schedules and modalities that help you earn your degree while balancing work, life and more.

### **Supportive Environment**

We are committed to providing quality, student-centered learning. Our faculty members and staff offer a variety of resources and assistance to help you succeed.
