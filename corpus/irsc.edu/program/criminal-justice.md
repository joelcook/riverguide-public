# CRIMINAL JUSTICE

Protect and serve the public with one of our degree programs in criminal justice at Indian River State College—home to some of the most advanced training experiences in the country.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## AT THE RIVER, YOU…

- Can get hands-on learning experience at the Institute for Cold Case Investigation, where you’ll work with local law enforcement on open, unsolved criminal investigations.
- Will take courses at the Treasure Coast Public Safety Training Complex, which is one of the most comprehensive and technologically advanced public safety training facilities in the country.
- Will learn about criminal and constitutional law, the court system and the function of police in a democracy.

## Associate in Arts

Associate’s Degrees

An associate degree lets you build a strong foundation in corrections and the law. Learn more about the wide range of career paths available in the criminal justice fields, from law enforcement to crime scene investigation to working within the court system.

When you complete your associate degree at The River, you are guaranteed the right to transfer as a junior to a bachelor’s degree program at a Florida public university—or continue in a bachelor’s degree program here, like the Bachelor of Science in Criminal Justice.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/criminal-justice-track/)

## Associate in Science Degree in Criminal Justice Technology

Associate’s Degrees

You’ll take courses in criminal justice, corrections and the law, along with elective choices in areas like evidence, terrorism and crime scene investigations.

You can also save money in this program by taking advantage of our Open Educational Resources for criminal justice courses, which provide customized materials and eliminate the need and cost for textbooks.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/criminal-justice-technology/associate-of-science-in-criminal-justice-technology/)

## Associate in Science Degree in Crime Scene Technology

Associate’s Degrees

The Indian River State College, Public Service Education Division’s Associate in Science in Crime Scene Investigations Technician is designed to prepare students for employment in field related to crime scene investigation.

Those who complete the program will be able to locate, preserve, develop, collect, analyze and present physical evidence relating to a crime.

Students will learn the skills necessary to accurately map out, collect and log evidence, develop and preserve fingerprints, photograph crime scene and evidentiary materials, write reports, and present courtroom testimony all while maintain their personal safety on a crime scene and in a crime laboratory.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/crime-scene-technology)

## Criminal Justice (B.S.)

Bachelor’s Degrees

Start or advance your career in law enforcement and corrections with a bachelor’s degree in criminal justice.

This program will prepare you for a leadership role in law enforcement at the local, state or federal levels, including probation, corrections, juvenile justice and private security services. This program also provides a strong foundation for graduate school or law school.

You’ll take classes with hands-on experiences and dive deep into a wide range of subjects that are relevant throughout the criminal justice field.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-program-in-criminal-justice/bachelor-of-science-in-criminal-justice/)
