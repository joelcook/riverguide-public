# DIGITAL MEDIA TECHNOLOGY

Digital media careers are on the rise. Prepare for exciting roles in growing and emerging fields like animation, simulation, gaming, web design, and content marketing.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## A DIGITAL MEDIA DEGREE FROM THE RIVER PREPARES YOU TO…

- Create new worlds and characters through game design, film animation, or web simulations
- Design and develop creative multimedia solutions for businesses in any industry
- Prepare for roles in industries including graphic and web design, broadcasting or audio and video production

## AVAILABLE PROGRAMS

- Bachelor of Applied Science Degree in Digital Media
- Associate in Science in Digital Media Technology
- Digital Media Support
- Digital Media Production

## Bachelor of Applied Science Degree in Digital Media

Bachelor’s Degree

The Bachelor of Applied Science (B.A.S.) in Digital Media is designed to provide advanced skills in the theoretical and practical aspects critical to this industry: graphic design, visual communication, animation, gaming and modeling. The upper-level coursework will broaden students’ knowledge in technical applications, art and design, interactive media, animation, management and entrepreneurial practices, scriptwriting, ethics, critical and creative thinking in media, and the interdisciplinary nature of art, technology, and science.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-programs-in-digital-media/bachelor-of-applied-science-degree-in-digital-media)

## Associate in Science in Digital Media Technology

Associate’s Degree

Our hands-on graphic design program includes a mix of theory and practice. You’ll take courses in web design and development, digital video and audio, animation, motion graphics, vector design, and more.

Customize your degree with electives of your choice, whether you want to dive deeper into programming or explore advertising.

Complete your first two years of college at The River, and you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/digital-media-technology/associate-in-science-degree-in-digital-media-technology/)
