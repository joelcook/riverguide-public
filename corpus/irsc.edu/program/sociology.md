# SOCIOLOGY

Discover how humans interact with each other, organizations, communities and society at large. You’ll also explore today’s most pressing problems—and discuss solutions.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/admissions-process/)

## THIS PROGRAM WILL…

- Build a foundation for continued study in sociology or other behavioral sciences
- Prepare you for entry-level roles in business, human services and other fields
- Deepen your knowledge of contemporary social issues

## AVAILABLE PROGRAM

- Associate in Arts

## Associate in Arts

Associate Degrees

Through a range of introductory and elective courses, you’ll explore sociological concepts and discuss contemporary societal issues. You’ll develop research, writing and critical thinking skills that will be valuable for careers of all kinds.

After you complete your A.A. in sociology, you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.
