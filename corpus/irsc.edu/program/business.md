## Bachelor of Applied Science in Organizational Management

Bachelor’s Degrees

Students will develop and apply the critical skills needed to launch, manage, and lead a successful small business.  Throughout the program, students will create, refine, and present a personalized business plan, gaining hands-on experience in real-world entrepreneurship.  It is ideal for individuals currently in the workforce who wish to enhance their leadership skills, take on more responsibilities, and advance their careers.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-programs-in-organizational-management/bachelor-of-applied-science-degree-in-organizational-management/)

## Career Development Program

Since 1979, The River’s Career Development Program has helped Treasure Coast residents build the skills and confidence to enter the workforce or return to school. Dedicated counselors help participants with referrals, career counseling, financial aid information and more. For more information, call 772-462-7685 or 1-866-792-4772.
