# HEALTH NAVIGATOR SPECIALIST

Be the bridge between patients and their healthcare providers as a health navigator specialist. Earn this certificate while working on your associate degree in health science and prepare to start working in the growing healthcare field.

[Request Info: healthscience@irsc.edu](mailto:healthscience@irsc.edu)

## AT THE RIVER, YOU…

- Will gain the foundational skills and knowledge you need to pursue a career in healthcare.
- Can pursue a range of health and medical training and degree programs that match your goals and interests.

## AVAILABLE PROGRAM

## Health Navigator Specialist Certificate

Technical Certificate

Guide patients and their families through the healthcare system with your health navigator specialist certificate. You’ll be qualified to help people receive proper care and access the resources they need in hospitals, home care and long-term care settings.
