# ACCOUNTING

Master the fundamentals of finance and accounting and unlock a stable, lucrative career. Learn in-demand skills and fill critical roles in any industry.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## AT THE RIVER, YOU WILL…

- Learn to update and maintain accounting records, prepare financial statements and more.
- Gain experience with specialized financial software.
- Prepare for roles like accountant, financial manager, bookkeeper, auditor, budget analyst and fraud examiner.

## AVAILABLE PROGRAMS

- Associate in Arts
- Associate in Science in Accounting Technology
- Bachelor of Science in Accounting
- Accounting Technology Specialist
- Accounting Technology Operations
- Accounting Technology Management

## Associate in Arts

Associate Degree

The associate’s degree accounting track provides a foundation for a career in a range of fields and industries. The associate degree program is designed to transfer into the Bachelor of Science in accounting program at The River or another Florida public university.

[Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/accounting-track/)

## Associate in Science in Accounting Technology

Associate Degree

Launch your accounting career with the in-demand skills employers need. Gain hands-on experience with accounting tools and software and graduate ready to take on an intermediate-level accounting position in any industry.

[Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/accounting-technology/associate-in-science-degree-in-accounting-technology/)

## Bachelor of Science in Accounting

Bachelor’s Degree

Get ready to sit for the Florida Certified Public Accountant exam and excel in careers ranging from accountant to financial manager. With a 97% job placement rate for accounting bachelor’s degree graduates, you can feel confident in your future in financial services.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-program-in-accounting/)
