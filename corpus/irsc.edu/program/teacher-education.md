## Educator Preparation Institute

Certificates of Professional Preparation

## Is EPI Right for Me?

### Already Teaching on a Temporary Certificate

In many cases, EPI is not required. Verify requirements on your Statement of Eligibility (SOE) and with your district certification specialist or EPI lead Professor Lalla.

### Have a Non‑Education Bachelor’s Degree

EPI is the standard route. You’ll complete professional education competencies, FTCE exams, and a mentored practicum.

### Finishing AA or Changing Careers

If you do not yet hold a bachelor’s, consider IRSC’s B.S. in Elementary Education or ESE. EPI is for bachelor’s degree holders.

Note: If your employer does not offer an Educator Competence/Employer Program, EPI can meet the professional preparation requirement.

## How the EPI Works

### Program Components

- Professional education coursework (7 or 10 courses, based on SOE/subject area)
- Florida Teacher Certification Examinations (FTCE): General Knowledge (GK), Professional Education (PEd), and Subject Area Examination (SAE)
- Mentored EPI Practicum with portfolio demonstrating the Florida Educator Accomplished Practices (FEAPs)

### Typical Timeline

- Obtain a Statement of Eligibility (SOE) showing “Eligible” for your subject area
- Apply to IRSC as a EPI student and upon acceptance complete onboarding
- Begin coursework and schedule FTCE exams at appropriate milestones
- Complete the EPI Practicum with portfolio
- Submit documentation to FLDOE to upgrade to a Professional Certificate

## Admission & Eligibility

### Eligibility Checklist

- Bachelor’s degree from a regionally accredited institution
- SOE indicating subject area eligibility (may require passing an FTCE Subject Area Exam)
- Professional dispositions and availability for practicum

### Looking Ahead…

A Level 2 background screening will be required later in the program for school/district placement. We mention it here so applicants are aware early: those with prior issues that may prevent clearance could face challenges progressing through the program.

### How to Apply

- Complete an IRSC application, selecting EPI as your program.
- Email your SOE and completed data form to [mlalla@irsc.edu](https://web.archive.org/web/20260315233800/mailto:mlalla@irsc.edu).
- Begin coursework; track FEAPs evidence for the practicum portfolio.

## **Autism Spectrum Disorder Endorsement**

Two Autism Endorsement courses are offered each semester. All courses are delivered online and facilitated by an instructor who guides course activities, assignments, and communication.

## Course Schedule

### **Fall Courses**

- EEX 4603 — Positive Behavior Support
- **EEX 4232 — Identification and Assessment of Students with Autism**

### Spring Courses

- **EEX 4094 — Educating Students with Autism**
- **EEX 4760 — Assistive Technology for Students with Autism**

## How to Register

Registration is completed online through Indian River State College. Students should apply as a baccalaureate-level, nondegree-seeking student using one of the following program codes:

- **H0010:** Nondegree, employment-related
- **H0020:** Nondegree, personal use

Use the registration checklist below to prepare the required application materials. All admission documents must be received before the applicable registration deadline.

## Registration Checklist

### 1. Complete the Application for Admission

Complete the [IRSC Application for Admission](https://irsc.edu/) and submit your Statement of Residency online.

Applications may be submitted before the semester in which you plan to enroll. Review IRSC’s residency requirements to determine whether you qualify as a Florida resident for tuition purposes.

### 2. Submit Your Teaching Certificate and Transcripts

Provide the following documents:

- A copy of your Florida Professional Educator Certificate or Temporary Certificate
- A copy of your college transcripts

Documents may be scanned and emailed to [Hadeel Mustafa](mailto:hmustafa@irsc.edu).

### **3. Pay the Application Fee**

Students enrolling in baccalaureate-level courses must pay a one-time, nonrefundable **$30 application fee**.

The fee may be submitted online with the Application for Admission.

## Program Costs

Current baccalaureate-level tuition for Florida residents is **$117.10 per credit hour**. Each course is three credit hours.

Additional expenses include:

- A **$15 distance learning fee** per course
- Four required textbooks for the complete endorsement program

Tuition, fees, and textbook costs are subject to change.

## Need Assistance?

For questions about the application or registration process, contact [Hadeel Mustafa](mailto:hmustafa@irsc.edu).
