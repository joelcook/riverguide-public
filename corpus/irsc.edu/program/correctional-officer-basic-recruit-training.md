## STUDENTS IN THIS PROGRAM WILL…

- Learn duties essential to the **care, custody, and control** of individuals in prisons, jails, and other correctional facilities.
- Receive training in **firearms, self-defense, and first aid**.
- Participate in **simulated activities** that reinforce classroom instruction and build practical skills.
- Develop professionalism, discipline, and situational awareness required for correctional environments.

## Correctional Officer Basic Recruit Training

Career and Technical Education Programs

Credits or Hours: 444

The Correctional Officer Basic Recruit Training program is a **minimum standards class regulated by Florida Statutes**. It is a highly structured and disciplined program with special rules, policies, and procedures.

Program Overview

- Total training hours: 444
- Program length: approximately four months
- Schedule: Daytime, Monday through Friday
- Offered once or twice per year, based on student enrollment and regional agency demand
- Student-recruits wear academy uniforms and participate in a structured classroom and training environment

Graduates of this program are eligible to take the **Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE) State Certification Examination** and may then seek employment with local or state correctional agencies.

**Important Financial Aid Note:**
This program is **not eligible for financial aid**.

### Law Enforcement Officer Crossover from Correctional Officer Training

Currently certified correctional officers may be eligible to transition into law enforcement through the **Law Enforcement Officer Crossover from Correctional Officer Training** program.

This pathway allows certified correctional officers to complete required law enforcement coursework without attending a separate Law Enforcement-to-Corrections academy.

Crossover Program Highlights:

- Total training hours: 222
- Offered based on demand
- Designed for currently certified correctional officers

Accreditation and Certification

The **Indian River State College Criminal Justice Institute** is certified by the **Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE)** to conduct authorized Criminal Justice Standards and Training Commission courses.
