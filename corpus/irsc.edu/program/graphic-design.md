# GRAPHIC DESIGN

From online media outlets to glossy magazines and from short-form content to comic books, graphic design is all around us. Express your creativity and find a career you love with an associate degree in graphic design.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## A GRAPHIC DESIGN DEGREE…

- Combines innovative thinking, design philosophy and technology
- Prepares you to work in a variety of creative settings such as design agencies and media outlets
- Opens doors for continued study and skill development at the bachelor’s level

## AVAILABLE PROGRAMS

- Associate in Science in Graphic Design
- Graphic Design Support
- Graphic Design Production

## Associate in Science in Graphic Design

Associate’s Degree

Our hands-on graphic design program includes a mix of theory and practice. You’ll take courses in web design and development, digital video and audio, animation, motion graphics, vector design and more. Customize your degree with electives of your choice, whether you want to dive deeper into programming or explore advertising.

Complete your first two years of college at The River, and you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/graphics-technology/associate-in-science-degree-in-graphics-technology/)

## Graphic Design Support

Technical Certificate

Prepare for entry-level graphic design careers with coursework in digital photography, image editing, graphic layout and publishing processes.

[Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/graphics-technology/graphic-design-support-certificate/)

## Graphic Design Production

Technical Certificate

This program offers advanced preparation for a career as a graphic production artist. Coursework includes digital imaging, publication design and typography.

[Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/graphics-technology/graphic-design-production-certificate/)
