## Bachelor of Science in Information Technology Management and Cyber Security

Bachelor’s Degree

Credits or Hours: 120

Learn advanced skills in building, maintaining and securing computer systems. With your bachelor’s in information technology management and cyber security, you will be prepared to troubleshoot problems and manage both large and small IT departments.

During your capstone semester in this program, you can work with businesses to solve complex cyber security issues, go hands-on with the latest secure hardware and software and compete in virtual hackathons. You’ll gain valuable experience that you can take into the workforce after you graduate.

Our graduateshave found careers at firms including AT&T, Comcast, Florida Power & Light, Pratt & Whitney, U.S. Sugar, Northrop Grumman and Boeing.

## Computer Information Data Specialist

Technical Certificate

Credits or Hours: 60

Learn the skills you need to work on and service personal computers, including how to install, configure, upgrade, troubleshoot and repair computer hardware and software. This certificate is designed to teach you the knowledge you need to take the A+ Certification Exam through the Computer Technology Industry Association.

## Help Desk Support Technician
