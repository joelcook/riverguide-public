## Associate in Science in Building Construction Technology

Associate Degrees

### Build the Skills for a Career in Construction

Develop the technical, management, and business skills needed to support construction projects from planning through completion. Gain practical knowledge of construction methods, materials, project management, building systems, and industry standards.

### What You’ll Learn

Coursework covers key areas of the construction industry, including:

- Construction and Building: Building materials, construction methods, plumbing, and electrical systems
- Project Management: Construction management, scheduling, and site supervision
- Estimating and Contracting: Bidding, estimating, and city, county, state, and federal contracting
- Technical Knowledge: Plans interpretation, building codes and regulations, and construction office practices

### What Can You Do With an A.S. in Building Construction?

An A.S. in Building Construction can prepare you for careers with home builders, general contractors, construction companies, and related organizations. Potential roles include:

- Estimator
- Asst. Project manager
- Asst. Site supervisor
- Asst. Construction planner
- Asst. Project coordinator
- Asst. Superintendent
