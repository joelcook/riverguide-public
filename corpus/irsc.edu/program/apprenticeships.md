## **Frequently Asked Questions**

What is an apprenticeship at Indian River State College?

An apprenticeship at Indian River State College combines paid, on-the-job training with classroom instruction, allowing students to gain real-world experience while learning industry-specific skills.

 Do apprentices get paid while in the program?

Yes. Indian River State College apprenticeships are designed as “earn while you learn” programs, meaning participants receive wages while gaining hands-on experience in their chosen field.

 What types of apprenticeship programs are available?

Indian River State College offers apprenticeships in a variety of industries, including electrical, plumbing, HVAC, welding, culinary operations, healthcare, and more, giving students multiple career pathways to choose from.

 What are the requirements to enroll in an apprenticeship?

Requirements vary by program, but typically include being employed by a sponsoring employer, meeting basic education requirements (such as a high school diploma or GED for some programs), and completing the application process.

 What do students receive after completing the program?

Graduates earn a certificate of completion from both the State of Florida and Indian River State College, and may be eligible for industry licensure or certification, depending on the program.

### **What is a Participating Employer?**

A participating employer is a business or organization that hires, pays, and trains apprentices through a structured on-the-job training model. Apprentices are paired with experienced employees who serve as mentors. These mentors guide skill development and evaluate progress throughout the program. Employers play a key role in ensuring apprentices gain the technical knowledge and hands-on experience needed to succeed in their occupation.

To participate, employers sign a formal agreement with Indian River State College’s Apprenticeship Program. This agreement outlines expectations such as:

- Training standards
- Mentorship requirements
- Progressive wage increases
- Appropriate supervision ratios
- Compliance with industry and program standards
