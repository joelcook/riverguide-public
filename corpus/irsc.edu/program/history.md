# HISTORY

Learn more about the present by exploring the past. An associate degree in history is excellent preparation for bachelor’s programs. Studying history also builds the critical thinking and analytical skills that today’s employers are looking for.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## A HISTORY DEGREE PREPARES YOU TO…

- Find entry-level positions in a variety of professional settings
- Succeed in a related bachelor’s degree program
- Think critically about how past events impact our world today

## AVAILABLE PROGRAM

- Associate in Arts

## Associate in Arts

Associate’s Degree

Explore various eras of American and world history through a variety of required and elective courses.

Complete your first two years of college at The River, and you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/degree-history-track/)
