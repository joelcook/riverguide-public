**Turn Caring into Your Career**
Get ready to join one of the fastest-growing careers in the healthcare field with our Medical Assisting program. This full-time, one-year (900 clock-hour) certificate program will prepare you for specific clinical, laboratory and administrative roles as a Medical Assistant. You might work in a physician’s office, clinic or other health care setting.

Our program, which has a selective admissions process, combines classroom theory and clinical practice. You’ll learn to perform a wide range of tasks ranging from examination room techniques to assisting with minor surgery, administering medications, educating patients and performing diagnostic procedures including drawing blood and electrocardiography. You’ll also be prepared to schedule appointments, maintain patient files and complete insurance forms.

You will get hands-on experience to make you job-ready upon completion of the program. This includes a supervised, uncompensated internship in a physician’s office or other appropriate facility.

Students who enroll in the full-time day program attend classes Monday through Friday, approximately 6 to 8 hours a day. The 170-hour mandatory clinical rotation is held during daytime hours only.

At the end of the program, you will be eligible to sit for the Certified Medical Assistant Certification Examination to earn the CMA (AAMA) credential. Our program boasts a higher-than-average pass rate, so you can be confident in the skills and knowledge you gain at the River.

### Accreditation

The Indian River State College Medical Assisting program is accredited by the Commission on Accreditation of Allied Health Education Programs ([www.caahep.org](http://www.caahep.org/)) upon the recommendation of the Medical Assisting Education Review Board (MAERB).

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Medical Assisting program at IRSC?

The Medical Assisting program at Indian River State College is a one-year, full-time certificate program that prepares students for clinical, laboratory, and administrative roles in healthcare settings.

 How long does the Medical Assisting program take to complete?

The program can be completed in approximately one year and includes 1,300 clock hours of training.

 What skills will I learn in the Medical Assisting program?

Students gain hands-on experience in clinical procedures, phlebotomy, electrocardiography (EKG), laboratory techniques, patient care, and medical office administration.

 Is the IRSC Medical Assisting program accredited?

Yes, the program is accredited by the Commission on Accreditation of Allied Health Education Programs (CAAHEP) upon recommendation of the Medical Assisting Education Review Board (MAERB).

 What certification can I earn after completing the program?

Graduates are eligible to sit for the Certified Medical Assistant (CMA) Certification Examination offered by the American Association of Medical Assistants (AAMA).

 When can I apply to the Medical Assisting program?

The application period typically opens in January, with a deadline of May 11, 2026, for Fall 2026 admission.

 What kind of jobs can I get after completing the program?

Graduates can pursue entry-level positions as medical assistants in physician offices, clinics, hospitals, and other healthcare facilities.

 Does the program include hands-on training?

Yes, the program includes hands-on clinical and laboratory training to prepare students for real-world healthcare environments.
