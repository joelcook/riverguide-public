# FOREIGN LANGUAGE

Open up a world of communications—and broaden your career opportunities—with an associate degree in foreign languages. Expand your language and cultural skills in Spanish, French, or American Sign Language (ASL).

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## THIS DEGREE WILL…

- Enhance your foreign language abilities
- Broaden your worldview and appreciation for other cultures
- Set you up for success in a bachelor’s degree program

## AVAILABLE PROGRAM

- Associate in Arts

## Associate in Arts

Associate’s Degree

Strengthen your speaking, reading, writing, and listening skills in Spanish, or French. You can also develop conversational abilities and deepen your proficiency in ASL.

Complete your first two years of college at The River, and you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/foreign-language-track/)
