- New students will have to submit an application, which includes a one-time $20 application fee. A valid SSN or ITIN must be entered in the application.

The English as a Second Language Program is for non-native English speakers to improve their English skills in order to successfully begin living and working in the United States. Students learn skills that can be applied to daily life and help students transition to higher education or the workforce. Our ESL Program has a variety of schedule options. We offer morning and evening classes at every location 2-4 days per week. We also offer online classes for students with a busier schedule.

## HOW TO APPLY

### Complete an Application

Create an account and complete the Non-Credit Adult ESL Application. There is a one-time, $20 application fee for new students.  Make sure you submit the application upon processing the payment.

### Pay $30 Tuition

The $30 tuition payment can be made online through your MyPioneerPortal or in person at your nearest campus cashier’s office. The River Portal access is granted within 3 days of the application. An email will be sent to the email listed in the application with the instructions for how to activate your account.

### Complete Online Intake Forms

Once you have obtained your Student ID, please complete the online intake forms for the program. These forms consist of the Code of Conduct, Image/Video Release, and Attendance Agreements.

### Schedule Your Placement Exam

New students must take the CASAS placement exam.  Please call or visit your nearest campus location listed to make the appointment.
