# INTERIOR DESIGN

Transform spaces, inspire lives and see your ideas come to life. Interior designers help create safe, practical and welcoming spaces for residential and commercial clients. You’ll learn from an expert faculty and connect with industry professionals through field trips and guest speakers.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## THE RIVER’S INTERIOR DESIGN PROGRAM PREPARES YOU TO…

- Develop practical and visually appealing design solutions for residential and commercial clients
- Work in a variety of settings, such as architecture firms, design agencies or individual consulting
- Take the National Interior Design Examination to become licensed in the state of Florida

## AVAILABLE PROGRAMS

- Associate in Science in Interior Design Technology
- Kitchen and Bath Design
- Home Staging

## Associate in Science in Interior Design Technology

Associate’s Degree

Gain theoretical knowledge, technical skills, and business practices to prepare for an exciting interior design career. You’ll explore design for commercial and residential spaces, with coursework covering everything from AutoCAD and building codes to lighting and textiles.

This associate degree program prepares you for professional interior design licensure in Florida.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/interior-design-technology/associate-of-science-in-interior-design-technology/)

## Kitchen and Bath Design

Technical Certificate

Gain specialized skills and knowledge about two important rooms for interior design professionals: the kitchen and bathroom.

[Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/interior-design-technology/kitchen-and-bath-design-certificate/)

## Home Staging

Technical Certificate

Learn about textiles, lighting, materials and more as you prepare for exciting real interior staging careers in real estate, rentals, tourism and advertising.
