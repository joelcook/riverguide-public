# SUPERVISION AND MANAGEMENT FOR INDUSTRY

Prepare for supervisory and management roles while developing technical skills in your chosen trade. This unique, flexible associate degree is also an excellent pathway to The River’s bachelor’s degree in organizational management.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

## THIS PROGRAM WILL…

- Introduce you to the fundamentals of supervision, management and leadership.
- Allow you to complete up to four technical certificates in a field or industry of your choice.
- Provide solid preparation for continued studies in business or organizational management.

## AVAILABLE PROGRAM

## Associate in Science in Supervision and Management for Industry

Associate Degrees

Credits or Hours: 60 credits

During this flexible program, you’ll develop skills and knowledge that prepare you to supervise and lead in industries of all kinds. Your required courses will explore topics like:

- Business communication
- Business law
- Finance
- Management
- Marketing
- Supervision

You can customize your program by choosing electives that match your interests and career goals. Options may include:

- Contemporary leadership
- Entrepreneurship
- Human resource management
- Ecommerce
- Transportation and logistics
- Technical writing

You’ll also have the option to complete an industry practicum elective, which will give you hands-on experience in a specific field.

**Additional Program Options: Add a Technical Certificate**
This unique program also allows you to earn two to four technical certificates while pursuing your associate degree. Choose from approved areas such as automotive, barbering, cosmetology, HVAC and welding.
