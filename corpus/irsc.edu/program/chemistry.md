## Chemistry

Associate’s Degree

Studying chemistry can open doors to a range of careers in fields from biotechnology and medicine to petrochemical and pharmaceutical industries.

This associate degree in chemistry will give you a firm foundation for further study. When you complete your first two years of college at The River, you can transfer as a junior to a bachelor’s degree program at a Florida public university or continue in a bachelor’s degree program here.
