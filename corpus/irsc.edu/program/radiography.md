**Program Overview**

Indian River State College offers several degrees and certificates that lead to professional licensure in the State of Florida. For radiographers, licensure requires successful completion of a recognized educational program and a passing score on the national certification exam administered by the American Registry of Radiologic Technologists (ARRT). Upon completion of this program, graduates are eligible to apply to sit for the ARRT certification examination. .

**Licensure and Certification**

Graduates of the Radiography program are eligible to take the ARRT national certification examination. The ARRT certification is recognized in all 50 states; however, some states—including New York and California—require additional certification or state-specific exams. If you plan to pursue licensure outside of Florida, please contact the appropriate state licensing agency to review specific state requirements.

[**Find state licensing information, contacts for state radiation control program offices and states that license or regulate by modality.**](https://www.asrt.org/main/standards-and-regulations/legislation-regulations-and-advocacy/states-that-regulate)

****Program Highlights****

- **Comprehensive Coursework:** Topics include radiographic procedures, pathology, patient care, radiation safety and protection, and radiographic physics.

- **Clinical Education:** Competency-based training at affiliated healthcare facilities within Indian River, St. Lucie, Martin, and Okeechobee counties. Clinical rotations prepare students to perform diagnostic imaging procedures safely and professionally.

- **Schedule:** Classes typically meet four to five days per week, between 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. Clinical shifts are scheduled between the hours of 7am to 7pm and may occasionally include evenings or weekends.

- **Travel Requirement:** Students must be prepared to travel to clinical sites within the College’s four-county service district, all located within approximately one hour of the Massey Campus.

****Accreditation****

The Radiography program at Indian River State College is fully accredited by the Joint Review Committee on Education in Radiologic Technology (JRCERT), the only accrediting agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education for radiography programs. The program’s current accreditation award is 8 years. General program accreditation information and the current accreditation award letter can be found [here](https://www.jrcert.org/programs/indian-river-state-college/).

## WHY STUDY RADIOGRAPHY AT THE RIVER?

### **Job-Ready Training**

Get hands-on experience in our state-of-the-art labs and through real-world clinical education at our community partners’ healthcare facilities.

### **Strong Local Network**

The River’s Radiography program is recognized by and has strong ties with our local healthcare community partners. This provides our students with unique job and learning opportunities.

### **Gain National Certification**

Each student who completes the radiography A.S. program is eligible to apply to take the American Registry of Radiologic Technologists (ARRT)  in Florida. Having ARRT credentials tells employers that you’re a qualified professional committed to providing high-quality patient care.
