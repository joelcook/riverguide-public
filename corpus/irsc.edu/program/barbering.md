## Barbering

Career and Technical Education Programs

The barbering program at Indian River State College is divided into two equal parts: theory coursework and a supervised clinical experience at our on-site salon/barbershop.

Throughout both parts of the program, you’ll learn about:

- Cutting
- Styling
- Shaving
- Mustache and beard design
- Chemistry of hair coloring
- Customer service/professional image
- Barbering and salon management

After successfully completing the program, you’ll meet the educational requirements to sit for the [Florida state barbering exam](https://www.myfloridalicense.com/CheckListDetail.asp?SID=&xactCode=1010&clientCode=0301&XACT_DEFN_ID=5060).

**Disclaimers:** Indian River State College offers a number of degrees and certificates leading to professional licensure in the state of Florida. Some professions require both certification and the successful completion of additional criteria such as licensure exams, field placements, or clinical hours. Student eligibility to apply to sit for the State Barber licensing exam in the State of Florida is defined in 476.144, F.S., and also requires possession of a valid form of identification and a Social Security number.
