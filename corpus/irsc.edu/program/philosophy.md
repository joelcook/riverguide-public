- Become a creative problem-solver, a sought-after asset in many professions
- Communicate clearly, effectively, and persuasively in the workplace

## Associate in Arts

Associate’s Degree

Build foundational study in philosophy, history, logic, and ethics through a range of required and elective courses.

Complete your first two years of college at The River, and you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/philosophy-track/)
