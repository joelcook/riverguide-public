**Help Keep Healthcare Running**
Get ready to join one of the fastest-growing careers in the healthcare field with our Medical Coding/Billing certificate program. You will learn how to navigate and use coding systems and insurance policies to tackle key operational functions in healthcare clinics, hospitals, government agencies and more.

Our program consists of lectures and hands-on learning through labs and practicum experiences. You will gain a foundation in all of the essential areas needed for a successful career, including:

- Medical terminology
- Anatomy and physiology
- Coding systems
- Pharmacology
- Health care delivery systems
- Basics of medical records services
- Ethical and legal responsibilities
- Safety/security procedures
- Basic data processing
- Employability skills

Upon completion, you’ll be eligible to sit for national certification exams like the Certified Coding Associate (CCA), Certified Coding Specialist (CCS), and Certified Professional Coder (CPC), opening doors to roles such as medical record coder, coding technician or coding clerk.

Medical coding and billing professionals enjoy flexible roles, as many positions are fully remote or hybrid. You may also choose to pursue opportunities to advance into auditing, compliance or health information management.
