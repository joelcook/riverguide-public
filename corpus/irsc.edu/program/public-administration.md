# PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION

Prepare to serve your community. A degree in public administration gets you ready for a variety of early-career roles in government, law enforcement, emergency management and nonprofit agencies.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## STUDYING PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION…

- Prepares you for careers in government, law enforcement, nonprofit management and emergency services.
- Deepens your knowledge of local, state and national government and related public service agencies.
- Broadens your understanding of ethical and economic issues in today’s society.

## Associate in Arts

Associate Degrees

Explore topics like American government, international relations, economics and business technology as you prepare for entry-level roles in public service.

After you complete your A.A., you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/public-administration-track/)

## Bachelor of Science in Public Administration

Bachelor’s Degree

Credits or Hours: 120 credits

The River’s online public administration bachelor’s program was named one of the top 10 in the country in 2023 by *Forbes Magazine*.

Prepare you for rewarding careers in the public sector, whether at the local, county, state, or federal level. Explore topics such as American government, urban and regional planning, organizational behavior, and program management.

Choose a Career-Focused Concentration

Expand your education and boost your job opportunities by choosing one of our two career-focused concentrations:

- **Emergency Planning and Management** – prepare to help others and your community in a time of need; gain the skills you need to plan, respond, and lead in a time of crisis
- **Public Policy and Leadership** – develop the knowledge needed to land management positions in the public sector

[Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-programs-in-public-administration/bachelor-of-science-degree-in-public-administration/)
