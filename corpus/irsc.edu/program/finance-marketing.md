# FINANCE & MARKETING

Become a key player in business. Learn how to make smart money decisions and develop marketing plans that drive sales.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## AT THE RIVER, YOU WILL…

- Understand how to raise, spend, and analyze funds to keep any business on track
- Learn to market products and increase profits
- Prepare for roles like financial planner, budget analyst, credit analyst or marketing manager
