## Associate in Arts

Associate’s Degree

Explore the subjects that are the foundation of engineering in our associate’s degree program. With courses in calculus, physics, math and chemistry, you will earn the credit you need to transfer to a four-year university and continue your education in engineering.

When you complete your first two years of college at The River, you can transfer as a junior to the Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering program at the University of Central Florida.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/engineering-track/)
