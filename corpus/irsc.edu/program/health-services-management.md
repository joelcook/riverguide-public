## Associate in Science in Health Services Management

Associate’s Degree

With your degree in health services management, you will be prepared to start your career working in medical offices and clinics. Health services managers typically oversee or coordinate the front-office operations at a clinic or doctor’s office. Day-to-day responsibilities can include working with the public to set appointments, maintaining confidential medical records and assisting with billing or accounting.

After you complete your associate degree, you can continue in the healthcare management bachelor’s degree program at Indian River State College or another Florida public university.
