## Associate in Arts

Associate Degree

Physicists use math and sophisticated equipment to explore motion, energy, structure and matter — all of which help us understand the natural world. Physics plays a role in virtually every scientific field, which means studying physics gives you a wide range of career opportunities in research, education, engineering, software development, computer networks and more.

When you complete your first two years of college at The River, you can transfer as a junior to the Bachelor of Science in Physics program at the University of Central Florida.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/physics-track/)
