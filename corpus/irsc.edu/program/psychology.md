# PSYCHOLOGY

A deep understanding of human behavior is beneficial in careers of all kinds. An associate degree in psychology prepares you for entry-level positions or continued study.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## THIS PROGRAM WILL…

- Build a foundation for bachelor’s programs in psychology or other social and behavioral sciences.
- Pepare you for entry-level careers in human services, sales, business and more.
- Deepen your knowledge of human behavior and interaction.

## AVAILABLE PROGRAM

- Associate in Arts

## Associate in Arts

Associate Degrees

Explore topics like human development, personality, abnormal psychology, and social problems. Learn about influential figures like Maslow, Freud, Skinner, and Jung.

After you complete your A.A. in psychology, you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/psychology-track/)
