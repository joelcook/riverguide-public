# POLITICAL SCIENCE

Explore the systems that govern and influence us, locally, nationally, and globally. Gain critical thinking, communication, and problem-solving skills as you prepare for public-facing careers of all kinds—or for university transfer.

## STUDYING POLITICAL SCIENCE AT THE RIVER…

- Prepares you for careers in government, public service, advocacy, media, business and more
- Broadens your perspective of local, state, national and international government
- Deepens your understanding of ethical issues and pressing societal problems

## Associate in Arts

Associate Degrees

Gain knowledge about American and international governments through a variety of required and elective political science courses.

Complete your first two years of college at The River, and you’re guaranteed the right to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.
