# HEALTHCARE MANAGEMENT

Be a leader in the rapidly growing health industry. With a degree in healthcare management, you will be prepared to plan, direct and coordinate medical and health services in hospitals, clinics and offices.

[Request Info: healthscience@irsc.edu](mailto:healthscience@irsc.edu)

## AT THE RIVER, YOU…

- Can learn the skills to start or advance your career as a manager in the healthcare industry
- Can get hands-on experiences and in-depth knowledge of healthcare management through case studies and practicum and capstone courses
- Can join the Health Occupations Students of America (HOSA), where you can develop the personal and professional skills needed in healthcare

## Bachelor’s Degree in Healthcare Management

Bachelor’s Degree

### Application Dates

- Fall 2026: April 7- July 20
- Spring 2027: July 21-November 16
- Fall 2027: April 6- July 19

The healthcare management program is a selective-admissions program. [Download the Healthcare Management Application Booklet](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/BS-HCM-App-Booklet-7-2026.pdf) for the admissions requirements.

Learn the skills and knowledge to work as a manager in a hospital, clinic, outpatient surgery center, physician’s office, public health agency, long-term care facility or laboratory. Through your courses, you’ll learn about leadership, communications, marketing, finances and legal compliance.

As a healthcare manager, you will keep healthcare operations running smoothly. Your day-to-day responsibilities could include making high-level decisions, solving problems, reviewing and monitoring resources and information, and overseeing a team of medical and administrative employees.
