## Associate in Arts

Associate’s Degree

What you learn in our math degree program can transfer into virtually any scientific field, letting you explore the subjects and topics that interest you while keeping your options open.

This associate degree in math will give you a firm foundation for transferring to a four-year university and even going on to an advanced degree. When you complete your first two years of college at The River, you can transfer as a junior to a bachelor’s degree program at a Florida public university or continue in a bachelor’s degree program here.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/mathematics-track/)
