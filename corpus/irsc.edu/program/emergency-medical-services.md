## Associate in Science Degree in Emergency Medical Services

Associate Degrees

Note that illicit drug use, background check findings, convictions and immunization status may prevent future employment. In addition, externship sites and employers may have regulations about drug use, criminal backgrounds and immunization status.

Take the courses you need to become a skilled EMT and paramedic with an associate degree in emergency medical services.

This program combines the general education foundation of an Associate in Science degree with the 53 credits required by the National EMS Education Standards in State of Florida statutes and administrative code for emergency medical technicians and paramedics.

The Indian River State College EMS Program is accredited by the Commission on Accreditation of Allied Health Education Programs (www.caahep.org) upon the recommendation of the Committee on Accreditation of Educational Programs for the Emergency Medical Services Professional (CoAEMSP).

## Emergency Medical Technician (EMT)

Technical Certificate

[Download Schedule (PDF)](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/EMT-Academic-Calendar.-2026-2027.pdf)

The Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) program prepares students for entry-level roles in emergency medical services, public safety communications, and ambulance operations.

### Admissions Requirements

Admissions requirements for the EMT program are outlined in the EMT Application Book and include:

- Be 18 years of age or older.
- Be a high school graduate or possess an equivalent credential (GED).
- Have a minimum 2.0 cumulative GPA.
- Complete fingerprinting and a Level 2 background check.
- Submit an FBI/FDLE criminal background inquiry and drug screening conducted through DISA Healthcare – Complio.
- Complete the required Physical Examination and Immunization Form.
- If applicable, have been honorably released or discharged from the U.S. military.
- Have no felony convictions or misdemeanor convictions involving perjury, false statements, or domestic violence.
- Applicants may also be disqualified for a misdemeanor involving moral turpitude, generally defined as conduct contrary to justice, honesty, modesty, or good morals.
- Be physically capable of participating in vigorous physical activities without restrictions.
- Possess a valid Florida Driver’s License. Applicants may be disqualified based on the number or type of traffic violations appearing on their driver’s license history report.
- Be a U.S. citizen.
- Be drug-free.
- Have no history of formal or informal criminal gang membership or association with gang members, as defined by Florida Statute 874.03.

## Paramedic

Technical Certificate

[View Information Session](https://youtu.be/aBpE51oEzhs)

The Paramedic program prepares students to provide advanced life support care to patients experiencing serious illness or injury.

As a paramedic, you will:

- Assess complex medical emergencies
- Prioritize patient treatment
- Administer advanced medical interventions
- Support patient stabilization and transport

Trainings include:

- Classroom instruction
- Clinical experiences
- Supervised field experiences

### Accreditation
