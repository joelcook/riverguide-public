# PARALEGAL STUDIES

If you’re detail-oriented, fascinated by fine print, and enjoy an office atmosphere, paralegal studies may be ideal for you. You’ll be ready for employment in law firms, business, government and more. This program also prepares you for paralegal certification.

[Request Info](https://info.irsc.edu/)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## PARALEGALS WORK IN…

- Law offices and legal departments of major corporations
- Government agencies and nonprofits
- Banks, insurance agencies and other financial institutions

## AVAILABLE PROGRAM

- Associate in Science in Paralegal Studies/Legal Assisting

## Associate in Science in Paralegal Studies/Legal Assisting

Associate Degrees

Dive into topics like business law, civil litigation, contracts, and estate planning. Build legal research and writing skills and learn more about law office management. You’ll get to pick from a wide range of electives that match your career interests.

After you complete your associate degree, you’re qualified to continue in a bachelor’s degree program here or at another Florida public university.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/paralegal-studieslegal-assisting/associate-in-science-degree-in-paralegal-studies-legal-assisting/)

## CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

At Indian River State College, our students who study Paralegal Studies/Legal Assisting can go into these careers:

- Paralegals and Legal Assistants
- Title Examiners, Abstractors, and Searchers
- Law Office Administrators
- Case Managers
