# CULINARY MANAGEMENT

Prepare for a career as a chef with essential kitchen management skills. Learn to orchestrate food preparation, control costs, and schedule staff while maintaining the highest standards of quality and safety.

[Request Info](https://irsc.formstack.com/forms/culinary_inquiry)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## AT THE RIVER, YOU WILL…

- Master a wide range of cooking and baking techniques and learn to prepare food in large quantities.
- Learn to manage food prices, handle inventory, and ensure food safety.
- Develop leadership skills to help you run a busy kitchen.

## LEARN AT A LEADING CULINARY INSTITUTE

### Committed to Culinary Excellence

Students at the Indian River State College Culinary Institute compete in cook-offs at the local and international level. It was the second culinary school in the U.S. to send students to Germany to compete as professionals in the IKA Culinary Olympics, and it regularly partners with local high schools for creative cooking competitions.

### Designed for Students Like You

The River offers affordable culinary school programs designed to fit around your busy life and work schedule. With small class sizes, a friendly team environment, and support from our faculty, we’re committed to your success.
