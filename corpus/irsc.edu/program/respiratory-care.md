# RESPIRATORY CARE

Become a vital part of the healthcare team with our Respiratory Care program designed to prepare you for a dynamic and in-demand career in respiratory therapy.

## THE RIVER CAN HELP YOU…

- Qualify for the National Board for Respiratory Care Registry (NBRC) exam. Upon successful completion of the NBRC exams, you will be awarded the Registered Respiratory Therapist (R.R.T.) credential.
- Prepare to work in all aspects of cardiopulmonary care, from routine therapy to life-threatening emergencies. Get ready to help a patient population that ranges from newborns to senior citizens.
- Build personal, leadership and career skills needed by healthcare providers through our active chapter of Health Occupation Students of America (HOSA).

## Associate in Science in Respiratory Care

Associate Degrees

**Application Window**: January 11, 2027 through April 30, 2027
**Program Start**: Fall 2027

[Respiratory Care Application Booklet](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Respiratory-Care-2027-prog_Application-Book-7-2026-1.pdf)

Indian River State College offers a number of different degrees and certificates leading to professional licensure in the state of Florida. Some professions require both a degree and the successful completion of additional criteria such as licensure exams, field placements, or clinical hours. Student eligibility to apply to sit for the Registered Respiratory Therapy (RRT) in the State of Florida is defined in, F.S., 468.355 and requires possession of a valid form of identification. Please visit the Florida Senate, F.S., 468.355 website to ensure your eligibility.

Completion of the Respiratory Care program requirements makes you eligible to apply to take the Registered Respiratory Therapy (RRT) in Florida.

If you may choose to reside outside of Florida, Indian River State College cannot confirm whether this degree program meets educational requirements for professional licensure/certification in your state. Some states have requirements in addition to the program’s curriculum. If you wish to take the Registered Respiratory Therapy (RRT) exam in another state, contact the regulation of professions and occupations in the state to determine whether this degree will meet their licensure requirements.

[Professional Licensure and Certification Disclosure Table for Registered Respiratory Therapist](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/professional-licensure-and-certification-disclosure-table-9-17-2026.pdf)

Start your rewarding career as a Registered Respiratory Therapist (RRT) with an associate degree from the River. This 21-month, full-time program combines in-person and online academic coursework with hands-on clinical experience, giving graduates the knowledge and skills needed to care for patients with cardiopulmonary disorders and others.

Career Opportunities
Graduates of the program are well-prepared to work in diverse healthcare settings, including hospitals, clinics, and home care, providing critical care to patients. Demand for skilled respiratory therapists is high and is expected to continue to grow, providing strong job prospects for RRTs.

Accreditation
The Indian River State College Associate in Science in Respiratory Care program at Indian River State College is accredited by the [Commission on Accreditation for Respiratory Care (CoARC).](https://www.coarc.com/) Program #200380. CoARC accredits respiratory therapy education programs in the United States. To achieve this end, it utilizes an “outcome-based” process. Programmatic outcomes are performance indicators that reflect the extent to which the educational goals of the program are achieved and by which program effectiveness is documented.
