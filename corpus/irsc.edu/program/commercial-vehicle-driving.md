## THE RIVER’S COMMERCIAL VEHICLE PROGRAM IS…

- **A fast-track to a new career**. Complete **CDL Class A training in as little as 4 weeks** or **CDL Class B training in as little as 1 week**
- **Flexible scheduling**, with new classes starting monthly
- **Hands-on learning**, including classroom instruction, observation, and behind-the-wheel driving

## A Trusted FMCSA Training Provider

Indian River State College is an **approved training provider listed in the [FMCSA Training Provider Registry](https://tpr.fmcsa.dot.gov/ "FMCSA Training Provider Registry")**. Additionally, we strictly adhere to all Federal Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) requirements while going beyond minimum compliance.

Furthermore, students receive extensive hands-on instruction in critical driving mechanics—including sliding the fifth wheel, adjusting trailer tandems, and proper coupling procedures—in addition to required ELDT theory and skills. Our CDL curriculum is designed to prepare graduates for **real-world commercial driving jobs**, not just to pass a state exam. Employers across the U.S. recognize The River’s graduates as **job-ready professional drivers**.

## Commercial Vehicle Driving

### Career and Technical Education Programs

Ready to get behind the wheel of your future? Indian River State College offers **career-focused CDL training in Florida** designed to get you road-ready fast.

Our instructors provide classroom instruction, observation, and extensive driving time to ensure you’re prepared for the **Florida DMV CDL exam** and on-the-job driving.

#### Program Cost and Format

- CDL Class A: $7,500
- CDL Class B: $2,500
- CDL Class B with School Bus/Passenger Endorsement: $3,300

#### Schedule Options

- Daytime: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM
- Evening: Monday–Thursday, 5:00 PM–10:00 PM

Tuition covers instruction and training. Additional required fees (such as medical exams and permits) are listed below.

---

### Course Breakdown

#### CDL Class A Training

- 40 hours of in-person classroom instruction
- 120 hours of behind-the-wheel driving (in person)

#### CDL Class B Training

- **12 hours** of online classroom training
- **28 hours** of behind-the-wheel training (in-person)

### How to Pay for CDL Training

Financing your CDL training in Florida may be easier than you think:

- Apply for a **[Sallie Mae loan](https://www.salliemae.com/student-loans/ "Sallie Mae loan")**
- If denied, you may qualify for **WIOA grant funding through [CareerSource](https://careersourceflorida.com/ "CareerSource")**. *CareerSource requires an acceptance letter from The River’s CDL program before approving funding*
- Additional funding options may be available for eligible students

### How to Get Started with CDL Training

1. Prepare for your permit test using the free CDL Prep App
   - [Download the app by *Jeffrey Diaz*](https://www.jeffreydiaz.com/cdl-prep-app.html "Download the app by Jeffrey Diaz")
   - Complete *General Knowledge* and *Air Brakes* modules
   - Practice until scoring 90–100% before scheduling your DMV permit test
2. Obtain Required Documents:
   - **DOT Medical Card:** $80 (with proof of enrollment)
     - Providers: OCC Medical in Fort Pierce or Dr. Okman in Port St. Lucie**Florida CDL Permit:** $6.25 at the DMV\* *Be sure to bring your DOT medical card when you visit the DMV for your permit.*
3. Complete the Student Application
   - Download the [CDL Student Application](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/IRSC-Student-Application-2-Updated.pdf)
   - Submit the form to [**Jimmy Kern (jkern@irsc.edu)**](mailto:jkern@irsc.edu "Jimmy Kern (jkern@irsc.edu)")
   - Then **create an account and register** at **[rivercontinuinged](https://rivercontinuinged.gosignmeup.com/Public/Course/Browse)**[**.gosignmeup.com**](http://rivercontinuinged.gosignmeup.com)

## Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to complete CDL training in Florida at Indian River State College?

CDL training at Indian River State College can be completed in as little as **1–4 weeks**, depending on whether you pursue **Class A or Class B** training.

 What is the difference between CDL Class A and CDL Class B training?

CDL Class A allows drivers to operate combination vehicles like tractor-trailers, while CDL Class B focuses on single vehicles such as box trucks, dump trucks, and buses.

 How much does CDL training cost at Indian River State College?

The cost of CDL training in Florida at Indian River State College depends on the program:

- CDL Class A: $7,500
- CDL Class B: $2,500
- CDL Class B with School Bus/Passenger Endorsement: $3,300

These tuition rates include classroom instruction and hands-on behind-the-wheel training.

 Are there financial aid or grant options available for CDL training?

Yes. Students may apply for **Sallie Mae education loans** or qualify for WIOA grant funding through CareerSource Florida. CareerSource requires an acceptance letter from Indian River State College’s CDL program before approving funding. Additional financial assistance may be available for eligible students.

 Is Indian River State College an approved CDL training provider?

Yes. Indian River State College is listed in the **FMCSA Training Provider Registry** and meets all federal ELDT requirements.
