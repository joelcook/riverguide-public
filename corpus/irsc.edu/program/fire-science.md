Get hands-on training to help people in need, save lives and protect property through our fire academy and fire science degree programs.

## Associate in Science Degree in Fire Science Technology

Associate’s Degree

Credits or Hours: 60

Earn your associate degree in fire science technology through our flexible program. This program is designed to work with your busy schedule while teaching you the skills and knowledge you need to advance your career.

You will learn about the science behind fire hazards and fire control, including the most effective operating procedures for fires and other emergencies.

With this degree, you’ll be prepared to work as a leader in firefighting or pursue related careers like investigator, fire protection and detection specialist, fire engineer or safety inspector.

## Basic Recruit Fire Academy

Career and Technical Education Programs

[Download Events Schedule (PDF)](https://irsc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FIRE-CALENDAR-9-17-2026.pdf)

Location: Treasure Coast Public Safety Complex

The Indian River State College Basic Recruit Fire Academy is certified by the Florida Bureau of Firefighter Standards and Training and serves fire service agencies throughout the Treasure Coast region.

The Fire Academy manages both:

- The Basic Recruit Fire Academy
- Advanced Fire Training Course

From the Firefighter Physical Agility Test (FPAT) through academy admission, IRSC provides the training, testing, and resources necessary to begin a firefighting career.

### Admissions Requirements

Applicants must:

- Be 18 years of age or older
- Be a high school graduate or equivalent (GED)
- Be a U.S. citizen
- Possess a valid Florida driver’s license
- Meet driving history requirements
- Be of good moral character
- Have no felony convictions, misdemeanors involving perjury, false statements, domestic violence, or crimes involving moral turpitude
- Have no affiliation with a criminal gang (as defined by FSS 874.03)
- Have been honorably discharged from the military (if applicable)
- Hold a current Florida EMT certification (EMT training is offered at IRSC)
- 2.0 minimum cumulative GPA required
- Applicants must also Successfully complete and Pass:
  - Drug screening
  - Fingerprinting
  - Level 2 background check
  - Medical and hearing examinations
  - Physical ability requirements without restrictions

Scholarship opportunities may be available. Visit <https://irsc.student.awardedsoftware.com> for additional information.

Step 3: FPAT Practice Session (Mandatory) and Test

- Report to Treasure Coast Public Safety Training Complex

Step 4: Fire Academy Acceptance Process

Applicants who successfully advance in the selection process will receive a welcome email with instructions for completing the next phase of Fire Academy admission.

This process includes:

- Complete formal Fire Academy application
- COMPLIO Requirements

  - Tobacco Affidavit
  - Drug Screening
  - Physical Examination
  - Background Check
  - Motor Vehicle Driving History
- Attend Mandatory Orientation
- Complete SCBA Fitting
