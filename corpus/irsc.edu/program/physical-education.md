# PHYSICAL EDUCATION

Turn your love of sports and physical fitness into a career. With a degree in physical education, you can help kids develop athletic skills, a love of teamwork and leadership qualities.

## Associate in Arts

Associate’s Degree

With your associate’s degree, you can teach in preschools or as a teaching assistant in grade schools. After you complete your associate’s degree, you can also continue in a bachelor’s degree program at Indian River State College or another Florida public university — which will prepare you to teach at the K-12 level.

With your bachelor’s degree, you will be qualified to teach as a physical education instructor or coach in schools or other instructional settings.

[Degree Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree/physical-education-track/)
