# HEALTHCARE INFORMATICS SPECIALIST

Start or advance your career in healthcare with training in data management and analytics. Our healthcare informatics specialist program prepares you for the evolving healthcare industry where hospitals and clinics rely on data to make clinical and business decisions.

[Request Info: healthscience@irsc.edu](mailto:healthscience@irsc.edu)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## AT THE RIVER, YOU…

- You may be eligible to take the AHIMA Certified Health Data Analyst (CHDA) exam.
- Will learn about electronic health record systems, data management, data analysis, and the legal and ethical requirements of working with health data.
- Can join the Health Occupations Students of America, where you can develop the personal and professional skills needed in healthcare.

## AVAILABLE PROGRAM

- Healthcare Informatics Specialist

## Healthcare Informatics Specialist

Technical Certificate

Application Window: **January 11, 2027 through July 19, 2027**
Program Start: Fall 2027

You will need to complete an advising session before you can be admitted to this program.

Learn the skills you need to become a health informatics specialist and work in the in-demand health information management industry. Graduates are prepared to work on-site in hospitals, surgery centers, clinics or remotely from home.
