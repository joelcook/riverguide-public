# HOSPITALITY AND TOURISM

Unlock a world of opportunities in one of Florida’s most important industries. Learn the essential skills to manage and market hospitality businesses from resorts to restaurants and shape unforgettable customer experiences.

[Request Info](https://irsc.formstack.com/forms/hospitality_inquiry)

[Apply](https://irsc.edu/admissions/apply/)

## AT THE RIVER, YOU WILL…

- Learn to plan events, manage staff, and deliver exceptional guest services
- Build leadership and administrative skills to manage businesses ranging from country clubs to casinos
- Prepare for roles in the travel, food and beverage, and conferences and events industry

## AVAILABLE PROGRAMS

- Associate in Science in Hospitality and Tourism Management
- Guest Services Specialist
- Event Planning Management
- Rooms Division Management

## Associate in Science in Hospitality and Tourism Management

Associate’s Degree

Prepare for an entry-level management position in the food and beverage or tourism industry. Build a well-rounded resume with coursework on topics including marketing, food and beverage management, and guest services.
