## Logistics and Transportation

Technical Certificates

Prepare for careers up and down the supply chain with a certificate in logistics and transportation from Indian River State College. You’ll explore topics such as operations management, inventory management, transportation services, supply chain and supervision.

This technical certificate can be earned as a standalone credential or as part of an associate degree program in areas such as business administration or supervision and management for industry.

[Certificate Requirements and Courses](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/logistics-and-transportation-specialist/)
