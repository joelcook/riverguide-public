# Cambridge Advanced Certificate (AICE)

The Cambridge Advanced Certificate of Education (AICE) program offers challenging curricula at high schools around the world and is designed to prepare students for exams leading to the award of college level coursework.

Information about the Cambridge AICE program is available at [https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-advanced/cambridge-aice-diploma/](http://www.cie.org.uk.usa/).
