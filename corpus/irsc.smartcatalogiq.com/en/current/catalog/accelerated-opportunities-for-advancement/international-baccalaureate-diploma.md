# International Baccalaureate Diploma

The International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma program offers challenging curricula at high schools around the world and is designed to prepare students for exams leading to the award of college level coursework.

Information about the IB program is available at [www.ibo.org](http://www.ibo.org).
