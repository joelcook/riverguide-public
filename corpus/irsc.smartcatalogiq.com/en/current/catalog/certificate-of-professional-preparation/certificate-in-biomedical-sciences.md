# Certificate in Biomedical Sciences

This program is for baccalaureate program for students who are preparing for graduate-level medical school, dental school, pharmacy school or other health-related professional school.  It is a 15-semester hour program in upper-level biology courses that will prepare students for the graduate school curriculum and entrance exams.  Transfer credit will be given for successfully equivalent courses listed on the student’s official transcript.
