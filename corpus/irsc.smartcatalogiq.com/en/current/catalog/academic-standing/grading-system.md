# Grading System

At the end of each semester, students may view their grades through [The River Portal](https://irsc.okta.com/login/default). Students may also review their academic progress by accessing the River Guide Degree Audit, also known as Degree Works, and check their enrollment status each semester within [The River Portal](https://irsc.okta.com/login/default).

The following grading system is used:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **A** | Excellent (4 grade points per semester hour) |
| **B** | Good (3 grade points per semester hour) |
| **C** | Average (2 grade points per semester hour) |
| **D** | Poor (1 grade point per semester hour) |
| **F** | Failure (no grade points) |
| **I\*** | Incomplete (no grade points) |
| **S** | Satisfactory (no grade points) |
| **U** | Unsatisfactory (no grade points) |
| **W** | Withdrawn (no grade points) |
| **NR** | Not Reported (no grade points) |

Some courses require a grade of C or better for graduation. To verify which courses require a C, students can review their River Guide Degree Audit, also known as Degree Works, or contact an advisor.

\*An incomplete grade (I), for which coursework is not completed by the end of the following semester will be converted to an F or a U, depending upon the grading method of the class.

Given appropriate advisement, a student may take a course for S (Satisfactory) or U (Unsatisfactory) graded credit and count it towards their program. Courses taken for S or U graded credit will not convert to any other type of grade.

The student’s grade point average is computed by dividing the total number of quality points earned by the total number of hours attempted.
