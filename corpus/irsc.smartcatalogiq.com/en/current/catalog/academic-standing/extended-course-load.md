# Extended Course Load

Students may take up to 19 credit hours during the Fall or Spring semesters (12 credit hours during Summer - Session A or Session B). Academically superior students with a 3.0 or higher cumulative grade point average and an academic record that reflects successful full-time enrollment may file an appeal to take additional credits.
