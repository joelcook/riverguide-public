# Mission Statement

**Indian River State College is dedicated to transforming lives by offering high-quality, affordable, and accessible education.**

**The River is committed to:**

- Creating a superior teaching and learning environment
- Developing a highly-skilled workforce
- Cultivating student success
- Promoting civic responsibility
- Embracing students of all backgrounds, ages, and abilities
- Stimulating economic growth
- Fostering community engagement
- Providing cultural enrichment and lifelong learning
