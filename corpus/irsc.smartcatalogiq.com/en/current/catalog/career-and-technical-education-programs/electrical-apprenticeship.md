# Electrical Apprenticeship

**8220 hours**

This program is delivered in partnership with State of Florida registered apprenticeship sponsors, and prepares students for advancement as residential or commercial electricians in the construction industry. The program utilizes a cooperative method of learning, requiring on-the-job training and classroom instruction, providing the student with theoretical and practical skills necessary in the electrical trades.
