# Welding Apprenticeship

**2671 hours**

This program is delivered in partnership with State of Florida registered apprenticeship sponsors, and prepares students for advancement as a welder fabricator in the manufacturing industry. The program utilizes a cooperative method of instruction, requiring on-the-job training for which the student receives compensation, and classroom instruction.
