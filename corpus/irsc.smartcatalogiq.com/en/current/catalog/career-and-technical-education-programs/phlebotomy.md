# Phlebotomy

**165 hours**

**(Selective Admission)**

This program teaches phlebotomy in a clinical setting, including classroom and patient care experiences in laboratory and health care facilities. Upon provisional acceptance, a complete health screen, drug screen and Florida Department of Law Enforcement check is required.
