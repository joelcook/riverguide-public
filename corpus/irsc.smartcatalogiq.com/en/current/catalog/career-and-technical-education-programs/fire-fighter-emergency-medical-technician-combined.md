# Fire Fighter - Emergency Medical Technician - Combined

**792 hours**

**Selective Admission**

This program provides the training required for students to become certified fire fighters as well as licensed Emergency Medical Technicians. It is not intended for those who are currently certified/licensed as either Fire Fighters or Emergency Medical Technicians. Students wishing to add an additional certification to an existing credential must enroll in either the [Firefighting - Basic Recruit Training](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/career-and-technical-education-programs/firefighting-basic-recruit-training) program or the [Emergency Medical Technician (EMT)](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/emergency-medical-services/emergency-medical-technician-certificate-60650) program.

This is a selective admission program. applicants must complete and pass a multi-step application process to be accepted.

Please note, each academy has a preliminary application time frame. All dates are subject to change and classes may fill prior to the end of the Preliminary Application Dateline.
