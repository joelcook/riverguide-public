# Home Health Aide

**165 hours**

**(Selective Admission)**

Home Health Care entails multiple types of health care services entering private residences to support patient’s needs. The Home Health Aides are part of the team providing essentials skills needed to assist patients regain their independence. The Home Health Aide program introduces students to the topics and skills relevant to provide patient care assistance in the home care setting.
