# Cosmetology Specialist - Facials

**220 hours**

A Florida specialty registration is required to perform facial specialty services. Facial services are the massaging or treating of the face or scalp with oils, creams, lotions, or other preparations. Applicants must complete 220 school hours in a Florida facial specialty program. The fee for the Facial Specialist application is $75.00. For more information, please visit <https://www.myfloridalicense.com/CheckListDetail.asp?SID=&xactCode=1030&clientCode=0508&XACT_DEFN_ID=5351>.
