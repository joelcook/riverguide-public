# Purpose

The purpose of Administrative Procedure (AP-3.13) is to support the IRSC District Board of Trustees Policy number 6Hx11-3.13 by rendering a process through which a complaint may be filed toward resolution of issues, and may be pursued in a fashion devoid of coercion, interference, restraint, discrimination or reprisal.

This procedure shall apply only to discrimination or harassment complaints based on race, color, national origin, ethnicity, sex, pregnancy, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, marital status, veteran status, or genetic information.
