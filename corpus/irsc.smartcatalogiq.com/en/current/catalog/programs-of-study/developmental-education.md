# Developmental Education

Indian River State College encourages all students to take advantage of placement testing to ensure appropriate placement into developmental or college-level courses in English, reading, and math. The P.E.R.T. is administered by The River for this purpose.

Required placement scores and exemptions to P.E.R.T. testing and are listed on <https://irsc.edu/programs/academic-resources-support/assessment-centers/pert-test/> and outlined in Section 1008.30, Florida Statues.

Developmental courses may not be used to meet degree requirements.

Alternative instructional opportunities are available for developmental instruction. Information is available at [Contact Your Advisor](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/services-to-students/contact-your-advisor).
