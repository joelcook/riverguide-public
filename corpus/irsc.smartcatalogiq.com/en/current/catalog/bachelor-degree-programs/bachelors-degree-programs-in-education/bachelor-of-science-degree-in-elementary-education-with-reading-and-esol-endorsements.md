# Bachelor of Science Degree in Elementary Education with Reading and ESOL Endorsements

**120 Credits**

As an elementary teacher, you will help children gain the foundation necessary to be successful throughout their academic career. You will have the opportunity to shape lives and futures. This program will prepare you to teach various subjects to elementary-aged children\*.

**Requirements for graduation from the Bachelor of Science Degree in Elementary Education with Reading and ESOL Endorsements are:**

1. Satisfactory completion of all three (3) parts of the Florida Teacher Certification Exam (FTCE). Mandatory checkpoints are required for program progression.
2. A passing score on the FTCE General Knowledge (GK) Exam is a pre-requisite to [LAE 4416](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/lae-elementary-education/4000/lae-4416) (GK English Language Arts and Essay),[EDG 4940](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/4000/edg-4940) (GK Math), [RED 4654](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4654) (GK Reading), and [RED 4854](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4854) (GK Reading).
3. Students must maintain a cumulative GPA of not less than a 2.5 on a 4.0 scale in all courses attempted (including transfer hours, but, excluding college preparatory courses) and in all courses attempted at Indian River State College.
4. Successful completion of Student Teaching ([EDE 4936](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ede-elementary-education/4000/ede-4936) [and](http://www.cmsiq.com/en/Catalogs/Indian%20River%20State%20College/2015-2016/Catalog/Courses/EEX-EDUCATION/4000/<a class='sc-courselink' href='/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4940'>EEX-4940</a>) [EDE 4941](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ede-elementary-education/4000/ede-4941)).
5. All Baccalaureate Education majors must achieve a grade of "C" or better in all content area courses within their declared Program of Study. Content area courses for Elementary Education with Reading and ESOL majors are: [EDG 3620](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/3000/edg-3620), [MAE 4310](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/4000/mae-4310), [MAE 4326](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/4000/mae-4326), [SCE 3310](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sce-education/3000/sce-3310), [SSE 3312](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sse-social-science-education/sse-3312), [RED 3342](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/3000/red-3342), [RED 4519](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4519),[RED 4654](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4654), [EEX 4066](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4066), and [LAE 4416](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/lae-elementary-education/4000/lae-4416).

Refer to [General Education Requirements](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-general-education-requirements) for additional requirements.

Also required: Completion of all lower-division state-mandated common prerequisites including the Education prerequisites: [EDF 2005](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/2000/edf-2005).

*\*This program includes multiple field experience requirements in Florida schools.*
