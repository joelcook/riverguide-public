# Bachelor of Science Degree in Exceptional Student Education with Reading and ESOL Endorsements

**121 Credits**

Helping children and young people overcome challenges to learning is extremely rewarding. As a specialist in Exceptional Student Education you will create opportunities that help every child succeed in the classroom. Through this IRSC program, you will gain a firm foundation in educational methods and classroom experience to help each child reach his or her full potential.\*

**Requirements for graduation from the Bachelor of Science Degree in Exceptional Student Education with Reading and ESOL Endorsements are:**

1. Satisfactory completion of all three (3) parts of the Florida Teacher Certification Exam (FTCE). Mandatory checkpoints are required for program progression.
2. A passing score on the FTCE General Knowledge (GK) Exam is a pre-requisite to [EDG 4376](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/4000/edg-4376) (GK English Language Arts and Writing), [EDG 4940](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/4000/edg-4940) (GK Math), [RED 4654](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4654) (GK Reading), and [RED 4854](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4854) (GK Reading).
3. Students must achieve a cumulative GPA of not less than a 2.5 on a 4.0 scale in all courses attempted (including transfer hours, but, excluding college preparatory courses) and in all courses attempted at Indian River State College.
4. Successful completion of Student Teaching ([EEX 4945](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4945) and [EEX 4941](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4941)).
5. All Baccalaureate Education majors must achieve a grade of "C" or better in all content area courses within their declared Program of Study. Content area courses for Exceptional Student Education with Reading and ESOL majors are: [EEX 3103](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/3000/eex-3103), [EEX 4221](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4221), [EEX 4601](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4601), [EEX 4264](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4264), [EEX 4265](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4265), [RED 3342](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/3000/red-3342), [RED 4519](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4519), [RED 4654](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4654).

Refer to [General Education Requirements](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-general-education-requirements) for additional requirements.

Also required: Completion of all lower-division state-mandated common prerequisites including the Education prerequisites: [EDF 2005](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/2000/edf-2005).

*\*This program includes multiple field experience requirements in Florida schools.*
