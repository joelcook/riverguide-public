### Additional Electives - 30 credits

Additional credits may be from lower or upper level courses.

Lower division coursework may be from the A.A. or A.S. degree. Recommended areas of study are criminal justice, fire science, paralegal, homeland security, emergency medical services, or other public safety academic areas.

It is strongly recommended that coursework include key skill-building courses such as:
