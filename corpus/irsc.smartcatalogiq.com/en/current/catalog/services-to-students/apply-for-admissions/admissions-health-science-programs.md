# Admissions - Health Science Programs

Applicants to the Health Science programs at The River are subject to special admission requirements and dates. Program application deadlines may be found at <https://www.irsc.edu/programs/health-sciences.html>.

In addition to admission requirements for most of the programs at Indian River State College, the Health Sciences Division requires applicants to meet additional “Selective Admission” conditions. These include:

- payment of a $30 non-refundable/non-transferable application fee for each program
- appropriate academic preparation
- acceptable scores on various aptitude tests

Students who need remediation before being accepted into the Health Science programs are referred to the Tutoring Center, where individualized instruction is available.

Program information outlining the "Selective Admission" criteria is available at <https://www.irsc.edu/programs/health-sciences.html>.
