# Admissions – Baccalaureate Programs

Anyone planning to enroll in an Indian River State College Baccalaureate program must complete the Application for Admission and Residency Declaration and pay the $30 non-refundable application fee. Applicants may complete this form online at [www.irsc.edu](http://www.irsc.edu). Admission is open to all who meet the admission requirements. Applicants must provide official transcripts for all prior college coursework. Students must demonstrate successful completion of developmental course requirements in English, reading and math prior to admission into a Baccalaureate program.

Applications for IRSC’s[*Bachelor of Applied Science Degree in Organizational Management*](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-programs-in-organizational-management) program are accepted year-round, and students may start during any semester of the academic year. Requirements for admission to the B.A.S. program in Organizational Management are:

1. An Associate in Science (A.S.) or an Associate in Arts (A.A.) Degree (or equivalent) from a regionally accredited postsecondary institution. .
2. A cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of 2.0 in Associate level coursework.

Applications for IRSC’s [*Bachelor of Applied Science Degree in Digital Media*](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-programs-in-digital-media) program are accepted year-round, and students may start during any semester of the academic year. Requirements for admission to the B.A.S. program in Digital Media are:

1. An Associate in Science (A.S.) or Associate in Arts (A.A.) Degree (or equivalent) from a regionally accredited postsecondary institution. An A.S. Degree in Graphic Design or Digital Media is preferred.
2. A cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of 2.0 in Associate level coursework.

Applications for IRSC’s [*Bachelor of Science Degree in Education*](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-programs-in-education) programs are accepted year-round, and students may start during any semester of the academic year. No provisional admission is allowed. Requirements for admission to the B.S. programs in Education programs are:

1. An Associate in Arts Degree (or equivalent) from a regionally accredited post-secondary institution.  A private or out-of-state AA transfer may have additional requirements.
2. Completion of lower-division state-mandated common prerequisite: [EDF 2005](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/2000/edf-2005).
3. A cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of 2.5 in Associate level coursework.

Equivalent Prerequisites may be accepted (see the Common Prerequisite Manual at [www.flvc.org](http://www.flvc.org) or contact an IRSC Advisor for details).

**Note:** All students accepted into an Education Baccalaureate Degree program must pass a Level II background check by the Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE) and the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) prior to beginning any course with a field experience component. Background checks will be administered by the school district(s) where field experiences are to be conducted. Students who cannot obtain a satisfactory background check will be prohibited from registering for courses with a field experience component and may be dismissed from the Education program.

Applications for IRSC’s [Bachelor of Science in Nursing (B.S.N.)](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-program-in-nursing) program are accepted each semester. Requirements for admission to the R.N.–B.S.N. program are:

Successful completion\* of [BSC 2085](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085) and [BSC 2085L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085l); [BSC 2086](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086) and [BSC 2086L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086l); and [MCB 2010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/2000/mcb-2010) and [MCB 2010L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/2000/mcb-2010l).
