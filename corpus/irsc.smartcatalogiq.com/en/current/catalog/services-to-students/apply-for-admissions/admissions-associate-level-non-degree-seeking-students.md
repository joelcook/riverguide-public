# Admissions - Associate-Level, Non-Degree Seeking Students

Anyone planning to enroll in Indian River State College as an Associate level, non-degree seeking student must complete an Indian River State College Application for Admission and Residency Declaration. Applicants may apply at [www.irsc.edu](http://www.irsc.edu).

Non-degree seeking students must also meet applicable placement testing requirements and satisfy course prerequisites. Upon completing 15 college credits at The River, the student's status will be reviewed, and appropriate advisement will be provided to support achievement of the student's goals.
