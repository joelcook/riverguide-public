# Placement Testing

Indian River State College strongly encourages all students to take advantage of placement testing to ensure appropriate placement into developmental or college-level courses in English, reading, and math. The P.E.R.T. is administered by Indian River State College for this purpose.

Required placement scores and exemptions to placement testing are listed on [https://irsc.edu/admissions/pert-test.html](https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Firsc.edu%2Fadmissions%2Fpert-test.html&data=05%7C02%7Cfarbelo%40irsc.edu%7C1bfdb3f3059341d7fe9e08dc99f85d86%7Cb00d4427a6e6417c8e9cd6915752f5c6%7C0%7C0%7C638554539222235348%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=mp0YDJ7wQs%2FDZKGmPgudVaqoPCyYNIRa%2Fgo%2F9nDheck%3D&reserved=0) and outlined in Section 1008.30, Florida Statues.  The P.E.R.T. is offered at each campus.
