# High School Transcripts

The College accepts high school credentials in accordance with Florida Statutes (1003.428), State Board of Education Rules, and IRSC Administrative Procedure 7.11. An official transcript must be submitted from a regionally accredited or Indian River Stage College approved secondary school or the equivalent (GED®, etc.).
