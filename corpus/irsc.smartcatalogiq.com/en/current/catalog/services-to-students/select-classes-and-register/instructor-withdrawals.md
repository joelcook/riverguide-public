# Instructor Withdrawals

Provided the student is not enrolled for the third attempt, Instructor Withdrawals may be used for documented extenuating circumstance such as deaths, illnesses, etc. After the grading window has closed, students should submit documentation to their instructor(s) and request an Instructor Withdrawal. Instructor Withdrawals must be approved by Dr. Belmont, Provost/Vice President of Academic Affairs, CAO.

Note that students who are receiving Bright Futures scholarships and other forms of financial aid may incur negative financial consequences as a result of class withdrawals. For this reason, students should consult with a Financial Aid Specialist to discuss the possible ramifications of withdrawing from a course prior to requesting an Instructor Withdrawal.
