# Apply for Financial Aid

Indian River State College believes that no person should be denied a college education due to a lack of funds.

The College and the Indian River State College Foundation make available to eligible students a variety of scholarships, grants, work opportunities, and loans. Applying for these opportunities is strongly encouraged as many students do qualify for some type of scholarship or financial aid.

Apply at [www.irsc.edu](http://www.irsc.edu), and click on the Financial Aid link.

**Federal and State Financial Aid programs are offered to eligible students at The River. These include:**

- Federal Pell Grant
- Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant (FSEOG)
- Florida Student Assistance Grant (FSAG)
- Florida Bright Futures Scholarship Program
- Federal TEACH Grant
- Federal Work Study Program (FWSP)
- Federal Direct Student Loans

Applications and information on these programs are available to students through their high school guidance counselor or at [www.irsc.edu](http://www.irsc.edu). Since financial aid applications may take 6 weeks to process, students are encouraged to apply early.

- Federal Aid recipients must meet Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) guidelines (See [Satisfactory Academic Progress for Financial Aid](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/services-to-students/apply-for-financial-aid/satisfactory-academic-progress-for-financial-aid)).
- Federal Financial Aid eligibility requirements will only allow payments for classes that are a part of the student's program of study. Up to 30 credits of developmental education coursework will count for financial aid purposes.
