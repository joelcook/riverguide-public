# Sign Up for New Student Orientation

Orientation offers a mix of academic, educational and social programming designed to prepare you for a successful college career.  Fall orientation will be held in-person with an online course component ([SLS 1501](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sls-college-orientation/1000/sls-1501)). Orientation is a mandatory 2-day event for ALL new students.

During New Student Orientation you can:

• Tour the campus
• Meet fellow students
• Learn about resources to help you succeed
• Meet The River's representatives ready to assist you

More information about New Student Orientation can be found by visiting the [The River's New Student Orientation Webpage](https://irsc.edu/admissions/new-student-orientation.html).
