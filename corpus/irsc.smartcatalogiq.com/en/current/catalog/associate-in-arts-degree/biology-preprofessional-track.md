# Associate in Arts - Biology (Preprofessional) track

**Students intending to transfer into one of the state’s universities to complete a four-year degree are encouraged to review the admission requirements (including prerequisites) of their intended institution.**

**Recommended curriculum for Biology majors seeking to enter graduate or professional schools after college. This track is designed for students planning to transfer into the IRSC Bachelor of Science program in Biology. Course requirements may differ slightly if planning to transfer to another institution, so students should seek guidance from that institution.**
