# Associate in Arts - Secondary Education track

Recommended curriculum for Education majors seeking to continue for a Bachelors degree in Education. This track is designed for students planning to transfer into the IRSC Bachelors degree in Education. Course requirements may differ slightly if planning to transfer to another institution, so students should seek guidance from that institution.
