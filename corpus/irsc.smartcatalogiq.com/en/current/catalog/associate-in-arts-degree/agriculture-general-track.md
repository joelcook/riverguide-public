# Associate in Arts - Agriculture (General) track

**Students intending to transfer into one of the state’s universities to complete a four-year degree are encouraged to review the admission requirements (including prerequisites) of their intended institution.**

Recommended curriculum for Agriculture majors seeking to transfer to University of Florida to pursue the B. S. in Food Science.

Course requirement may differ slightly if planning to transfer to another institution so students should seek guidance from that institution. Also, course requirements may differ slightly if planning to enter a different field of Agriculture (Animal Science, Soil & Water Science, Plant Science, etc.) so students should seek guidance from their transfer institution.
