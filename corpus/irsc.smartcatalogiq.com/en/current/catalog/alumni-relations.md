# Alumni Relations

The Indian River State College Alumni Association was established to meet the needs of Indian River State College alumni.

Administered by the Indian River State College Foundation, Inc., the Alumni Association maintains an electronic database of Indian River State College alumni and provides updates and current events at the College via periodic electronic newsletters.

In addition, the Alumni Association identifies resources at Indian River State College that will enhance personal and professional goals of Indian River State College alumni.

Alumni are welcome to visit the Foundation Office on Massey Campus in Fort Pierce to share information and suggestions for making the College and community a better place to live and learn.

The Foundation’s website, [www.irscfoundation.org/](http://www.irscfoundation.org/) offers a variety of options for Indian River State College alumni, including purchasing a commemorative brick on Alumni Row, accessing the *Connections Newsletter*, along with reviewing the arts and events schedules.

For more information, contact the Alumni Development Coordinator at (772) 462-7254.
