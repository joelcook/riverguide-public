# Honors Socities

Honor societies recognize and celebrate high academic achievement while promoting scholarship, leadership, service, and personal growth among students. These organizations encourage the development of character, foster meaningful connections among peers, and provide opportunities for engagement beyond the classroom. Through recognition programs, service initiatives, and leadership experiences, honor societies support student success and acknowledge outstanding academic and co-curricular accomplishments. For specific information about each honor society, please visit Indian River State College’s student engagement portal [Explore - RiverLife](https://irsc.campuslabs.com/engage).

**Phi Theta Kappa (PTK)**

Phi Theta Kappa is the internationally recognized honor society for two-year colleges that fosters scholarship, leadership, service, and fellowship. Eligible students are invited each semester to apply based on academic achievement. Membership offers opportunities for academic enrichment, leadership development, and recognition of scholastic excellence.

**Alpha Phi Sigma (Criminal Justice Honor Society)**

Alpha Phi Sigma recognizes academic excellence, leadership, service, and ethical standards within criminal justice disciplines. Members engage in activities that promote analytical thinking, rigorous scholarship, and professional development in the field of criminal justice.

**Nursing Honor Society**

The IRSC Nursing Honor Society honors nursing students and nurse leaders who have demonstrated outstanding academic achievement and commitment to excellence in the nursing profession. Membership is by invitation, recognizing scholarly performance and leadership in nursing.

**Kappa Delta Pi (Education Honor Society)**

Kappa Delta Pi is an invitation-only educational honor society that recognizes high academic achievement among future educators. Members are provided with opportunities for professional growth, service learning, and leadership within education.

**Lambda Nu (Imaging Sciences Honor Society)**

Lambda Nu is a national honor society for students in radiologic and imaging sciences. The society promotes high academic and clinical standards, fosters mentorship, and shares knowledge about imaging sciences.

**Sigma Alpha Pi – National Society of Leadership and Success (NSLS)**

Sigma Alpha Pi at IRSC emphasizes leadership development, community involvement, and personal growth. Invitations are extended to students who demonstrate academic success and leadership potential, with opportunities to engage in workshops and service experiences.

**Sigma Beta Delta (Business Honor Society)**

Sigma Beta Delta recognizes superior academic achievement in business administration and organizational management programs. Membership honors students’ commitment to excellence in business scholarship and ethical leadership.

**SALUTE Veterans National Honor Society**

SALUTE Veterans National Honor Society acknowledges the academic achievement and leadership of student-veterans and National Guard members. It fosters a community that supports veterans’ transition to academic success and service.
