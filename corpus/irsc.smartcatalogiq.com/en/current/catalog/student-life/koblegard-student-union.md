# Koblegard Student Union

The Koblegard Student Union (KSU) at Indian River State College serves as a central hub for student engagement and support, housing a wide range of student-focused offices and services. These include the Office of Student Life, the First Year Experience Program, Student Conduct, Residence Life, the College Ombudsman/Student Advocate, the River Shop bookstore, the Student Engagement Center, the campus mailroom, and the River Café. Together, these resources are designed to support student success, foster community, and enhance the overall collegiate experience. Students can obtain The River parking decals for their vehicles and student identification cards at the Office of Student Life — both are provided at no cost and are required for accessing campus services and parking privileges.

For additional information about Student Life programs, events, and involvement opportunities, students are encouraged to explore RiverLife through the student portal at <https://irsc.campuslabs.com/engage>. The Office of Student Life may also be contacted by email at StudentLife@irsc.edu, visited in person at KSU Suite 112, or reached by phone at (772) 462-4706.
