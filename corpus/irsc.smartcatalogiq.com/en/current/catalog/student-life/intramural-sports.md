# Intramural Sports

Indian River State College Intramural Sports program is available to all students attending The River. These activities provide students with an opportunity to participate in various sports; promoting social interaction, health, and student-friendly rivalry through many different forms of competition, making the program fun.

Intramural sporting events are held at the Massey Campus in Fort Pierce. Information on Intramural Sports can be obtained by contacting the Office of Student Life at [studentlife@irsc.edu](mailto:studentlife@irsc.edu) or at the KSU. When events are planned with established times and venues, flyers will be posted on social media platforms and the RiverLife student engagement portal <https://irsc.campuslabs.com/engage/>.
