# Academic Awards

**Dean's List**

The Dean’s List designation is posted on the official transcript at the conclusion of each semester. Eligible students receive a congratulatory letter from the Provost in recognition of their academic achievement.

To qualify for this honor, students must complete a minimum of 12 credit hours in a given semester and earn a Grade Point Average (GPA) of 3.5 or higher, with no grade lower than a C. Courses graded using the S/U (Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory) method are not eligible for consideration.

**President's List**

The President's List designation is posted on the transcript at the end of the Fall and Spring semesters, and students receive a congratulatory letter from the President of the College.

All students who have completed a minimum of 15 credit hours or more in a given semester with the letter grade of A and a semester GPA of 4.0 will be eligible. Courses with an S/U grading method are not eligible.

**Graduating with Honors**

Graduating students' academic achievements are recognized during the Spring or Fall Commencement Ceremony as follows:

- GPA of 3.85 to 4.00 - Graduated with Highest Honors (Summa Cum Laude)
- GPA of 3.70 to 3.84 - Graduated with High Honors (Magna Cum Laude)
- GPA of 3.50 to 3.69 - Graduated with Honors (Cum Laude)

Students who earn Academic Honors will have it designated on their transcript.

Computation of Academic Awards for the [Baccalaureate Degree](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs) is based on all coursework attempted (excluding developmental education).

Computation of Academic Awards for the [Associate in Arts Degree](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/associate-in-arts-degree) is based on all coursework attempted (excluding developmental education and those occupational courses that may not be counted toward an A.A. Degree).

Computation of Academic Awards for the [Associate in Science](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates) and the [Associate in Applied Science](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates) degrees are based on all coursework attempted (excluding developmental education).

For all degrees, undergraduate coursework attempted at other postsecondary institutions will also be used in this computation.
