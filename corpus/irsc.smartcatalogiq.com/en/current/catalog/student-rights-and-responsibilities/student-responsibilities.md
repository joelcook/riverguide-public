# Student Responsibilities

Indian River State College students are responsible to:

- Become familiar with and adhere to Student Standards of Conduct, including
  - Respect each students’ right to learn
  - Make responsible use of facilities and equipment, including electronic communications
  - Demonstrate respect towards faculty, staff, and administrators
  - Adhere to standards of academic honesty
  - Refrain from activities or conduct that might discredit or disrupt the College
- Comply with non-discrimination/non-harassment policy
- Comply with anti-hazing policy
- Comply with intellectual property policy
- Comply with alcohol/drug-free campus policy
- Comply with tobacco/smoke free campus policy
- Comply with firearms/weapons policy
