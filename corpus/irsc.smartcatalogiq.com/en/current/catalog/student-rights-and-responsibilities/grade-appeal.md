# Grade Appeal

Criteria for final grade determination and assignment are the prerogative of the instructor of record for all College courses. Only the primary instructor can change the final grade. Students who are not satisfied with their grade should first consult with their instructor to resolve the grade dispute.

If this step has been taken and the matter remains unresolved, students may then meet with the Executive Dean responsible for the course to have their grades reviewed and explained to them. After completing the above process, students who still wish to appeal their grade may meet with the College Provost to discuss the grade. If the matter remains unresolved, the student must submit a request using a Grade Appeal Form: <https://cm.maxient.com/reportingform.php?IndianRiver&layout_id=48>. If the Vice President for Student Success or designee finds the request reasonable and appropriately substantiated, the matter will be referred to the Academic Review Committee. A formal meeting will be scheduled to review the student’s case, review any pertinent documentation, and make an objective determination regarding the student’s final grade. After the review is completed, the Academic Review Committee will make a recommendation to the College President who will notify the student in writing of the College’s final decision.

All grade appeals must be initiated within one calendar year after the disputed grade was awarded. Proving grounds for a change of grade is the responsibility of the student.
