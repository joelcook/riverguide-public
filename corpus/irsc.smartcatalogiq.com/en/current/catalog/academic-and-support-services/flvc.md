# Florida Virtual Campus (FLVC)

FLVC has a family of services including:

- [FLVC Library Services](https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Flibraries.flvc.org%2F&data=05%7C01%7Cbsampson%40irsc.edu%7C04f369fb8bfb44f18b4b08daf8d527f1%7Cb00d4427a6e6417c8e9cd6915752f5c6%7C0%7C0%7C638095891623794266%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=Q41G0uDOMqifJgBLvkhsPF%2FzwZnPKSg19s1KgtZg2Vw%3D&reserved=0): operates the library system and services utilized by the State University System and Florida College System, along with a shared catalog and statewide collection of online journals, e-books, and other e-resources
- [Distance Learning and Student Services (DLSS)](https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdlss.flvc.org%2F&data=05%7C01%7Cbsampson%40irsc.edu%7C04f369fb8bfb44f18b4b08daf8d527f1%7Cb00d4427a6e6417c8e9cd6915752f5c6%7C0%7C0%7C638095891623794266%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=r4PYKcWHeBqCRtrwLbQaap3%2F9QPDyXQhkPI3nva%2BW5o%3D&reserved=0): provides innovative educational services for students, educators, and administrators in Florida, DLSS operates several statewide services including FloridaShines.
- [FloridaShines](https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.floridashines.org%2F&data=05%7C01%7Cbsampson%40irsc.edu%7C04f369fb8bfb44f18b4b08daf8d527f1%7Cb00d4427a6e6417c8e9cd6915752f5c6%7C0%7C0%7C638095891623794266%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=j4lz5T5RPYvFwV%2BPnklq1nGMcFfRnyAvIZ6ri9vn%2Bw0%3D&reserved=0): works with the state’s public colleges and universities and other partners to help students succeed in school and beyond. Indian River State College students can obtain transcripts, grades, transient student forms, and transfer program admission information. They also use the online service for many purposes including conducting graduation checks and A.A. Degree transfer evaluations, and gathering information about career exploration.
