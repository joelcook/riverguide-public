# Libraries

Indian River State College Libraries are the center of academic activity for students, faculty, and the campus community. The library is an integral part of the intellectual life on campus, and trained library faculty and staff can provide individual and group assistance and guidance.

Indian River State College students and employees can request items from libraries throughout Florida using inter-library loan. The Miley Library is also a U.S. Federal Depository Library. It receives many Federal documents of interest to the campus community and to the citizens of the Treasure Coast.

Information Literacy and Research Skills ([[LIS 1002](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/lis-computer-science/1000/lis-1002)](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/lis-computer-science/1000/lis-1002)) and Research Strategies for College Students ([[LIS 2004](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/lis-computer-science/2000/lis-2004)](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/lis-computer-science/2000/lis-2004)) are recommended one-credit courses offered each term to teach students how to find, evaluate, and integrate sources into their personal and academic information needs.

**The Charles S. Miley Library – Massey Campus, Fort Pierce**

The Miley Library provides full-service library resources for using and accessing information resources in all formats. The library provides robust physical and digital collections and in-person reference assistance for students. This library has a computer lab and study rooms. The IRSC Tutoring Center is also co-located in the Miley Library.  Visit the Miley Library LibGuide for more information:<https://irsc.libguides.com/mileylibrary>.

**Pruitt Campus Library – Pruitt Campus, Port St. Lucie**

Operated in cooperation between the College and St. Lucie County, the joint-use library offers exceptional resources to students and residents of St. Lucie County, including books, periodicals, and media collections that reflect the college curriculum, as well as a diverse collection of current literature. This library has a computer lab, study rooms, and large children and young adult collections.  Visit the Pruitt Campus Library LibGuide for more information:

<https://irsc.libguides.com/pruitt>

**Brackett Library - Mueller Campus, Vero Beach**Operated in cooperation between the College and the Indian River County Library System, the joint-use library offers exceptional resources to students and residents of Indian River County, including books, periodicals, and media collections that reflect the college curriculum, as well as a diverse collection of current literature. This library has study rooms, and large children and young adult collections. The Marion C. Link Lab provides students and the Mueller Campus community with a teaching computer lab.  The Brackett Preservation Lab (<https://irsc.libguides.com/bpl>), located inside the Link Lab, is a self-service space providing equipment and software for library users to digitize their analog and print memorabilia including VHS and DVD home movies, photographs, negatives, slides, yearbooks, and scrapbooks.  Visit the Brackett Library LibGuide for more information: <https://irsc.libguides.com/brackett>

**Robert Morgade Library - Chastain Campus, Stuart**Operated in cooperation between the College and the Martin County Library System, the joint-use library serves both students and Martin County residents. It provides access to books, periodicals, and media collections that reflect the college curriculum and Martin County resident interests. The library also has large children and young adult collections, computers, study rooms, and a makerspace. Visit the Morgade Library LibGuide for more information: <https://irsc.libguides.com/morgadelibrary>

**Dixon Hendry Library Collection - Okeechobee**This collection provides students with online access to a wealth of information resources, including college library holdings, e-books, online databases, and worldwide Internet resources. The collection is co-located in the Dixon Hendry Tutoring Center (Academic Support Center). Visit the Dixon Hendry Library Collection LibGuide for more information: <https://irsc.libguides.com/dixonhendry>
