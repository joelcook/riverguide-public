# Sending Indian River State College Transcripts

Indian River State College has designated Parchment as its authorized agent for the printing, mailing, and secure electronic delivery of official academic transcripts in PDF format. Transcripts issued through Parchment are official records of the College and include all relevant academic coursework. Parchment is authorized to process and fulfill transcript requests on behalf of Indian River State College and to respond to inquiries related to these transactions. Official student transcripts maintained by Indian River State College are protected under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act of 1974 (FERPA).

**Current Students** may log in to [The River Portal](https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Firsc.okta.com%2Flogin%2Fdefault&data=05%7C01%7Cbsampson%40irsc.edu%7C4aaf4256c9f443400a1f08da38fb7355%7Cb00d4427a6e6417c8e9cd6915752f5c6%7C0%7C0%7C637884949875399217%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=WtRLse9%2FmAEpblS5j3xBU%2F796GJDHWbNteuLj3XRWR8%3D&reserved=0 "MyPioneerPortal") to order their Indian River State College transcript.  Logging into the portal streamlines the process, as your student record information will automatically populate. You will then be redirected to the Indian River State College transcript ordering system through Parchment.

**Alumni** may order official transcripts directly through the [Parchment](http://www.parchment.com) website.

If you have questions about outgoing transcripts, contact Student Records at 772-462-7460 or [records@irsc.edu](mailto:records@irsc.edu "records email").
