# BCN 2598 Sustainable Building Systems

In this course, students explore the integration, interaction, and interdependence of HVAC, plumbing, and lighting systems to reduce energy consumption. Students analyze factors including site selection, intelligent building controls, and how other systems affect performance, with a focus on enhancement of indoor air quality and the use of sustainable/green building materials.

3 credits
