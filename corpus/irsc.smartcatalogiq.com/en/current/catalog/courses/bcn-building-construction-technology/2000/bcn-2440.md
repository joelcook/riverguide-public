# BCN 2440 Concrete Construction Methods

In this course, students explore concrete formwork for residential, light commercial, and heavy construction, using ACI recommended practices and OSHA shoring recommendations. Students also explore panel systems, prefabrication, reinforcing steel, form ties, openings, residential foundations, flatwork, precast concrete, concrete mix, and placement.

3 credits
