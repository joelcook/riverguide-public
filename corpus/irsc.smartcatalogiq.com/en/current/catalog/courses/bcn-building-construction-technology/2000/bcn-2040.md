# BCN 2040 Zero Energy Building Design and Construction

In this course, students explore emerging green building concepts, energy systems, and cost benefit analysis. Students also investigate net zero design, green building, alternative energies, energy conservation, microgeneration technologies, dual use of energy, passive cooling, occupant behavior, retrofitting, and related considerations.

3 credits
