# BCN 1250 Architectural Drafting Principles

In this course, students learn basic hand drafting skills, such as line technique, lettering, scaling, dimensioning and symbols, leading to the development and submittal of a set of architectural drawings. Utilizing drafting equipment, students draw site plans, foundations, wall sections, floor plans, roof design, stairs, elevations, sections, and construction details. Students make a presentation on an architectural topic.

3 credits
