# BCN 1215 Materials & Methods of Construction - Finishes & Systems

In this course, students explore how various materials and construction methods associated with thermal and moisture protection, such as doors and windows, interior finishes, specialties and equipment, electrical systems, and mechanical systems (Master Format sections 7-16), affect the construction cost, total life, and maintenance cost of a building. The merits of new materials and methods are introduced and compared to existing products and methods. Students explore these topics from a builder's perspective emphasizing proper installation procedures and processes.

3 credits
