# BCN 1272 Plans Interpretation - Residential

In this course, students learn the basic processes, terminology, symbols, and conventions used in residential construction. Students develop the skills needed to read and interpret residential construction plans and perform builder’s math calculations related to measurements, materials, and plan information.

3 credits
