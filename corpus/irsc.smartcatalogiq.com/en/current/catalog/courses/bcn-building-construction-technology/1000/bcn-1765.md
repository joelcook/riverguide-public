# BCN 1765 Codes and Regulations

In this course, students explore OSHA regulations and the Florida Building Code. Emphasis is placed on the standards that have the widest application to our local residential and commercial buildings. Students develop procedures for following the Building Code and perform sample checks on code compliance.

3 credits
