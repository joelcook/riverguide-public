# SPN 1120 Elementary Spanish I

This course facilitates the students' acquisition of communicative competencies in the four basic skills of speaking, listening, reading and writing Spanish. This course concurrently focuses on enriching students' cultural understanding of the Spanish-speaking world. This course can be used toward the foreign language requirements for university admission.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

Student must score into college-level English on placement test.
