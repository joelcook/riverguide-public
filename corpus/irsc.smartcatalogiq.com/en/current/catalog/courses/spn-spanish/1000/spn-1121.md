# SPN 1121 Elementary Spanish II

This course continues the students' acquisition of communicative competencies in the four basic skills of speaking listening, reading and writing Spanish begun in [SPN-1120](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/spn-spanish/1000/spn-1120), with a continuing focus on enriching students' cultural understanding of the Spanish-speaking world. This course can be used toward the foreign language requirements for university admission.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[SPN 1120](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/spn-spanish/1000/spn-1120)
