# SPN 2220 Intermediate Spanish I

The course continues the student's acquisition of communicative competencies in the four basic skills begun in [SPN-1120](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/spn-spanish/1000/spn-1120) and [SPN-1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/spn-spanish/1000/spn-1121), with a continuing focus on enriching students' cultural understanding of the Spanish-speaking world. This course can be used toward the foreign language requirements for university admission.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[SPN 1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/spn-spanish/1000/spn-1121) or its equivalent.
