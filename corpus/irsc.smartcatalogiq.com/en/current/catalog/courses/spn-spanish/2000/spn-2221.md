# SPN 2221 Intermediate Spanish II

In this course, students continue to develop communicative competencies in the four basic skills of speaking, listening, reading, and writing in Spanish. They work to reach the intermediate mid-level of language proficiency according to the ACTFL OPI standards.

Students engage in critical thinking, and practice reading and listening comprehension related to the study of Hispanic culture.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[SPN 2220](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/spn-spanish/2000/spn-2220) with grade of C or higher, or permission of instructor.
