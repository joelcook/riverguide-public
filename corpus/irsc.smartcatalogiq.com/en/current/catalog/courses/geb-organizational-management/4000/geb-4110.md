# GEB 4110 Finalizing Your Business Plan

In this course, students bring their business plan development to its culmination. As the final course in the sequence of business plan development, students craft an executive summary that highlights the core aspects of their venture, hone and finalize their business plan through iterative refinement, and prepare a professional presentation. By designing an engaging presentation and presenting their business plan to an audience, students develop critical communication skills and receive actionable feedback to help bring their entrepreneurial vision to life.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENT 4410](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ent-entrepreneurship/4000/ent-4410) and [MAR 3025](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mar-business-administration/3000/mar-3025), both with a grade of 'C' or higher
