# MAE 4932 Seminar in Mathematics Education

In this course students develop instructional strategies, planning techniques, evaluation procedures and class management skills.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Senior level status with all program requirements met, and permission of department. [MAE 4815](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/4000/mae-4815), [STA 2023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sta-mathematics/2000/sta-2023), [MAE 3816](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/3000/mae-3816) and [MHF 4404](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mhf-mathematics/4000/mhf-4404), all with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[MAE 4945](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/4000/mae-4945)
