# MAE 4815 Elements of Algebra

In this course students investigate the conceptual nature of mathematics and algebra. The students explore topics in set theory, number systems, number theory, data analysis, algebraic structures and elementary probability. Development of critical mathematical reasoning and application of theories using technology are emphasized.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

pre/coreq: [MAC 2311](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/2000/mac-2311) with a grade of C or higher
