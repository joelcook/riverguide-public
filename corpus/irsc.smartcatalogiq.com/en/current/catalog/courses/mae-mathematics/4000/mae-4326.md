# MAE 4326 Teaching Elementary School Mathematics II

In this course, students explore Elementary Education K-6 Math content, methods and strategies for teaching measurement, data analysis, statistics, and geometric concepts in elementary classrooms. Knowledge of student reasoning and instructional practices as they pertain to these topics are also included.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EDG 3620](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/3000/edg-3620) with grade of 'C' or higher

### Corequisites

Pre/Co-requisite: [MAE 4310](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/4000/mae-4310) with grade of 'C' or higher
