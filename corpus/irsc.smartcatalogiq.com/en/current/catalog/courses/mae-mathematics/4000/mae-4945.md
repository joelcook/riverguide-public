# MAE 4945 Student Teaching in Mathematics

In this course students demonstrate pre-professional competencies during a 12 week, full-time internship in a public school approved by the department. Contact hours: a minimum of 35 hours per week for 12 weeks.

Senior level status with all program requirements met, and permission of department.

10 credits

### Prerequisites

Senior level status with all program requirements met, and permission of department. Grade of C or higher required for [MAE 4815](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/4000/mae-4815), [STA 2023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sta-mathematics/2000/sta-2023), [MAE 3816](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/3000/mae-3816), [MHF 4404](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mhf-mathematics/4000/mhf-4404)

### Corequisites

[MAE 4932](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/4000/mae-4932)

### Course Fee

Insurance fee $4.75.
