# MAE 2111 Mathematics Content for the Elementary Grades

In this course, students explore how to teach conceptually and developmentally appropriate mathematics content for all students at the PK-6 grade level. Major topics include number sense, concepts, and operations; geometry and measurement; algebraic thinking and the coordinate plane; and probability, statistics, and data interpretation.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

pre/coreq [EDF 2005](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/2000/edf-2005), and [MAT 1033](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mat-mathematics/1000/mat-1033) or [MGF 1130](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mgf-mathematics/1000/mgf-1130), or higher math, with a grade of C or higher
