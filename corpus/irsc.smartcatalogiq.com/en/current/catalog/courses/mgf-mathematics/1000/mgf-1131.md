# MGF 1131 Mathematics in Context

In this course, studentsexperience the practicality of mathematics in a global society. Students engage in the applications of tools and techniques of mathematics in a variety of contextual situations from everyday life, making this course appropriate for students in a wide range of disciplines/programs.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MGF 1130](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mgf-mathematics/1000/mgf-1130) with a grade of "C" or higher, or placement scores.
