# HEV 0181 Behavioral Observation and Screening in Child Care (N)

In this course students explore appropriate techniques necessary for observing the developmental behavior of children, specifically through the use of a checklist, or similar tools to match observed behaviors with the corresponding developmental age level.

6 hours
