# HEV 0115 30-Hour Statewide Childcare Training (N)

In this course students examine the state rules and regulations governing childcare; health, safety, and nutrition; identifying and reporting child abuse; and principles of child growth and development. It presents the skills required to implement a developmentally appropriate, anti-bias program for children ages birth to age five.

30 hours
