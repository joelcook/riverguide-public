# NUR 1021C Intro to Nursing Concepts 1

In this course, students assimilate concepts within the domains of nursing, health, situation, and person. Students acquire foundational knowledge of the concepts discussed throughout the nursing program. Students engage in clinical experiences with clients across the lifespan.

10 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2085](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085), [BSC 2085L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085l), or BSC 2093, BSC 2093L; or [ZOO 3733](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/zoo-biology/3000/zoo-3733), [ZOO 3733L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/zoo-biology/3000/zoo-3733l); and [BSC 2086](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086), [BSC 2086L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086l), or BSC 2094, BSC 2094L or [PCB 4701](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/4000/pcb-4701), [PCB 4701L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/4000/pcb-4701l), all with a grade of "C" or higher; and [HUN 1201](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/hun-nutrition/1000/hun-1201), [PSY 2012](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/psy-psychology/2000/psy-2012), [MAC 1105](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1105) or [MGF 1130](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mgf-mathematics/1000/mgf-1130), [STA 2023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sta-mathematics/2000/sta-2023), or [MAC 2311](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/2000/mac-2311) all with grade of C or higher
