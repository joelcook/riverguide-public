# NUR 1034C Health-Illness-Concepts 2

In this course, students assimilate the concepts within the domains of nursing, health, situation, and person. Students learn the concepts and skills necessary to provide basic nursing care and health maintenance in a variety of settings. Focus is on basic physiological integrity and maternal newborn care of clients. Students engage in clinical experiences with clients across the lifespan.

11 credits
