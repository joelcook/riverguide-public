# NUR 2205C Nursing and Complex Health Concepts 4

In this course, students assimilate the knowledge, skills, and attitudes to safely care for clients with complex health conditions. Students master the concepts of perfusion, quality and safety, managing care, and professional behaviors. Students engage in clinical experiences with clients across the lifespan.

11 credits

### Prerequisites

[NUR 2035C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/nur-nursing-associate-degree-nursing/2000/nur-2035c), with [MCB 2010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/2000/mcb-2010), [MCB 2010L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/2000/mcb-2010l) or [MCB 3023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/3000/mcb-3023), [MCB 3023L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/3000/mcb-3023l), or MCB 3020, MCB 3020L, all with a grade of "C" or higher
