# FRE 1121 Elementary French II

In this course, students build on their French skills, focusing on more advanced speaking, reading, and writing. They learn past and future tense verbs, engage in complex conversations, and explore deeper cultural aspects of French-speaking countries. With interactive activities and cultural studies, students enhance their fluency and broaden their understanding of Francophone traditions and lifestyles. This course can be used toward the foreign language requirements for university admission.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[FRE 1120](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/fre-french/1000/fre-1120) or permission of instructor
