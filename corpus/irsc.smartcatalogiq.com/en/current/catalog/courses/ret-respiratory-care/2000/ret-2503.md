# RET 2503 Cardiopulmonary Diseases

In this course, students engage in an in-depth study of cardiopulmonary diseases including etiology, diagnosis, and treatment.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[RET 1485](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1485) with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RET 2275C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2275c), [RET 2832](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2832)
