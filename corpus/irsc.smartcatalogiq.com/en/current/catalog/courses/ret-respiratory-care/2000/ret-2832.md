# RET 2832 Respiratory Care Clinic I

In this course, students participate in supervised observation and clinical practice of oxygen and aerosol therapy. Associated procedures are presented in [RET-1024](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/current/Catalog/Courses/RET-RESPIRATORY-CARE/1000/RET-1024) and [RET-1274](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/current/Catalog/Courses/RET-RESPIRATORY-CARE/1000/RET-1274). Clinic meets two days per week (16 hours).

5 credits

### Prerequisites

[RET 1024C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1024c), [RET 1274C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1274c) with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RET 2275C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2275c), [RET 2503](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2503)
