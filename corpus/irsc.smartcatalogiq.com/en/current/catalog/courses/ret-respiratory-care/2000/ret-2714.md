# RET 2714 Pediatric/Neonatal Respiratory Care

In this course, students engage in an in-depth understanding of the significant anatomic, physiologic, and pharmacologic concepts as they relate to the pediatric and neonatal patients. Students study disease entities and resuscitation methods specific to neonates and pediatrics.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RET 2264C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2264c) with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RET 2835](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2835)
