# RET 2835 Respiratory Care Clinic IV

This course provides supervised observation and clinical practice of mechanical ventilation on adult and pediatric/neonatal patients along rotations in specialized care settings.

5 credits

### Prerequisites

[RET 2264C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2264c), [RET 2280](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2280), [RET 2834](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2834) all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RET 2714](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2714), [RET 2934C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2934c)
