# RET 2934C Professional Development in Respiratory Care

In this course, students prepare for employment in the field of Respiratory Care. Employability skills, registry preparation, and computer literacy are discussed.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[RET 2264C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2264c), [RET 2280](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2280), [RET 2834](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2834) with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RET 2714](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2714), [RET 2835](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2835)
