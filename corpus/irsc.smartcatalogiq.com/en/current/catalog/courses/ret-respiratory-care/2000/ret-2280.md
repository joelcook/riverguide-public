# RET 2280 Critical Respiratory Care

In this course, students review advanced theories and modalities of respiratory care including hemodynamic monitoring, EKG interpretation, pulmonary and cardiac diagnostic procedures, sleep studies, cardiopulmonary rehabilitation and home care. Students identify, assist, interpret and recommend related procedures and modalities for the management of the cardiopulmonary patient.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RET 2833](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2833) with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RET 2834](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2834), [RET 2264C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2264c)
