# RET 2241 Introduction to ACLS for Respiratory Care

This course reviews Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) and Pediatric Advanced Life Support based on the American Heart Association standards. It prepares students for the ACLS and PALS certification examination.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RET 2280](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2280) with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RET 2835](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2835)
