# RET 2264C Mechanical Ventilation with Lab

In this course, students  examine diagnostic techniques in the field of pulmonary function, which includes spirometry, lung volumes, static and dynamic mechanics, ventilation, and distribution of gases. A lab setting is utilized to reinforce lecture and enable the student to develop the skills necessary to perform in a clinical setting.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

RET 2442 with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RET 2834](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/2000/ret-2834)
