# RET 1024C Introduction to Respiratory Care

In this course, students examine the field of Respiratory Care including terminology, patient care techniques, CPR, and professional ethics. A lab setting is utilized to reinforce lecture and enable the student to develop the necessary skills prior to performance in a clinical rotation.

3 credits

### Corequisites

[RET 1007](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1007), [RET 1485](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1485), [RET 1274C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1274c)
