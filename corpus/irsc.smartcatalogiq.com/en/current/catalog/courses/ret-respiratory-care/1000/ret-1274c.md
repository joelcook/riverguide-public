# RET 1274C Respiratory Care Theory I

In this course, studentsapply the theory of medical gas, humidity, and aerosol therapy along with the equipment associated with their administration. A lab setting is utilized to reinforce lecture and enable the student to develop the skills necessary to perform in a clinical setting.

3 credits

### Corequisites

[RET 1007](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1007), [RET 1024C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1024c), [RET 1485](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1485)
