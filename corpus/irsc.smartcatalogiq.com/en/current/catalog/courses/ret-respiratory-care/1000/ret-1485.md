# RET 1485 Cardiopulmonary Anatomy and Physiology

In this course, students Learn anatomy, physiology, and pathology of the pulmonary and cardiovascular systems and their application to the basics of pulmonary disease.

3 credits

### Corequisites

[RET 1007](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1007), [RET 1024C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1024c), [RET 1274C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ret-respiratory-care/1000/ret-1274c)
