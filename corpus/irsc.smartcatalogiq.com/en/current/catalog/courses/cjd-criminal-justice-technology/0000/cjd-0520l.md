# CJD 0520L 911 Public Safety Telecommunications Lab (N)

This is a preparatory course that prepares students for employment as a dispatcher: police, fire and ambulance. Students will apply the knowledge base gained in [CJD 0520](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cjd-criminal-justice-technology/0000/cjd-0520), utilizing real-world equipment, to gain the critical skills necessary to become successful in the program and the real world.

112 Hours
