# CTS 1650 CCNA1: Introduction to Networks

In this course, students explore the architectures, models, protocols, and networking elements that connect users, devices, applications, and data through the Internet and across modern computer networks - including IP addressing and Ethernet fundamentals. This is the first of a series of courses designed to prepare students for the industry certification exam leading to the Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) certification.

3 credits
