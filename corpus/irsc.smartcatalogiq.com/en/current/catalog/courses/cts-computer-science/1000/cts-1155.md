# CTS 1155 Introduction to Help Desk Concepts

In this course, students learn the business, technical, and interpersonal skills needed to succeed in a Help Desk setting. All major aspects of Help Desk operations are addressed including customer support, processing and resolving incidents, and knowledge management. Students are also presented with the basics of Project Management and the Systems Development Life Cycle.

3 credits
