# CTS 2651 CCNA2: Switching, Routing, & Wireless Essentials

In this second course in the CCNA curriculum, students examine switching, routing, and wireless technologies used to support and secure business networks. In addition to performing common configuration and troubleshooting tasks, students learn to identify and mitigate security threats in both wired and wireless local area network (LAN) environments.

3 credits
