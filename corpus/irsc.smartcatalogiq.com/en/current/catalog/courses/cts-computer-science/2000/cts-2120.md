# CTS 2120 Security Fundamentals (Security+)

In this course, students apply broad-based knowledge to assess the security posture of an enterprise environment and recommend and implement appropriate security solutions; monitor and secure hybrid environments, including cloud, mobile, and IoT; operate with an awareness of applicable laws and policies, including principles of governance, risk, and compliance; identify, analyze, and respond to security events and incidents.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[CET 1179C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cet-electronic-engineering-technology/1000/cet-1179c)
