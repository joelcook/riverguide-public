# CTS 2652 CCNA3: Enterprise Networking

In this third course in the CCNA curriculum, students examine the architectures and considerations related to designing, securing, operating, and troubleshooting enterprise networks.  Wide area network (WAN) technologies, quality of service (QoS) mechanisms, virtualization, and automation concepts are some of the topics discussed.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[CTS 2651](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cts-computer-science/2000/cts-2651)
