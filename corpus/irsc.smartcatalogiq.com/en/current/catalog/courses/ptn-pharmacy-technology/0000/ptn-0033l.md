# PTN 0033L Pharmacy Operations II (N)

In this course, students acquire knowledge of medications, proper usage of automated dispensing cabinets, medication reconciliation, and safe handling of antineoplastic drugs. Students get hands on experience through lab simulation.

80 hours

### Prerequisites

[HSC 0003](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/hsc-health-science/0000/hsc-0003), [HSC 0540](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/hsc-health-science/0000/hsc-0540), [PTN 0000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ptn-pharmacy-technology/0000/ptn-0000)
