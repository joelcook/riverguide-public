# PTN 0015 Pharmaceutical Calculations (N)

In this course, students acquire knowledge of pharmaceutical mathematics including reading, interpreting, and solving calculation problems encountered in the preparation and distribution of drugs. Conversion of measurements within the apothecary, avoirdupois, and metric systems with emphasis on the metric system of weight and volume are covered. Ratio, proportion, percentage, dilution and concentration, milliequivalent units, intravenous flow rates, and dosage problems are presented.

75 hours
