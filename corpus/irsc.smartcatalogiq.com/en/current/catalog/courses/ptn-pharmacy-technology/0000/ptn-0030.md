# PTN 0030 Introduction to Community Pharmacy (N)

In this course students are assigned to a community pharmacy facility to demonstrate skills learned in the classroom/lab courses. Students complete 200 hours of supervised professional duties of a pharmacy technician.

200 hours

### Prerequisites

[PTN 0032L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ptn-pharmacy-technology/0000/ptn-0032l), [PTN 0033L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ptn-pharmacy-technology/0000/ptn-0033l), [PTN 0023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ptn-pharmacy-technology/0000/ptn-0023), [PTN 0015](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ptn-pharmacy-technology/0000/ptn-0015) with grade of C or higher
