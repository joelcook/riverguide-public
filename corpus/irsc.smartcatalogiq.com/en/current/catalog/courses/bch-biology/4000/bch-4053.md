# BCH 4053 Biochemistry I

In this course, students examine the structure and function of proteins, membranes and cellular constituents, enzyme catalysts, and carbohydrate metabolism.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [CHM 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210), [CHM 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210l), [PCB 3063](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063), [PCB 3063L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063l), all with a grade of "C" or higher

### Corequisites

[BCH 4053L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053l), pre/corequisite: [CHM 2211](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2211), [CHM 2211L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2211l)
