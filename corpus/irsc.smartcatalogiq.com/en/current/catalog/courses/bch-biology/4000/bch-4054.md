# BCH 4054 Biochemistry II

In this course, students describe the chemistry of living systems and the biological phenomena that result from the interaction among systems.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BCH 4053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053), [BCH 4053L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053l) , [CHM 2211](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2211), [CHM 2211L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2211l), all with a grade of "C" or higher
