# CJT P445 Community Safety (N)

The Community Safety class introduces the Basic Law Enforcement academy students to the importance of Procedural Justice & Legitimacy, De-escalation and Bias Based Policing. They gain an understanding of the impact on the publics’ confidence and cooperation.

24 hours
