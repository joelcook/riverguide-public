# ACG 4651 Auditing

In this course, students examine the theory and practice of public auditing, common ethical situations, and the legal responsibilities of an auditor. Internal controls, audit evidence, audit procedures, and sampling techniques are reviewed.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ACG 3131](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acg-accounting/3000/acg-3131) with a grade of C or higher
