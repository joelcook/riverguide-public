# ACG 2001 Financial Accounting I

In this course, students explore the fundamentals of accounting with a focus on the basic accounting cycle for a company. Students learn the process of recording, classifying, and summarizing financial data involving accruals and deferrals, merchandising, cash, receivables, and fixed assets.

3 credits
