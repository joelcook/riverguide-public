# ACG 2071 Managerial Accounting

In this course, students explore the fundamentals of accounting with a focus on internal accounting. Students learn cost accounting concepts and relationships, cost-volume-profit analysis, variance analysis, budgeting, pricing decisions, and management accounting analysis for decision making.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ACG 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acg-accounting/2000/acg-2011) with a grade of "C" or higher.

### Course Fee

Lab fee $25.
