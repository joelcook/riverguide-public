# ACG 3401 Accounting Information Systems

In this course, students examine the concepts and theory of accounting information systems and the use of information technology for decision making in accounting and auditing.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ACG 2071](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acg-accounting/2000/acg-2071), and [CGS 1100](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cgs-computer-science/1000/cgs-1100) or [CGS 1060](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cgs-computer-science/1000/cgs-1060), all with a grade of C or higher
