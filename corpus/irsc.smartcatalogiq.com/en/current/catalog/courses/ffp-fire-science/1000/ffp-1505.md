# FFP 1505 Fire Prevention

This course provides an overview of the structure and organization of the fire prevention entities including fire prevention principles, application of codes, laws, and ordinances, methods of developing fire prevention programs, recognition and elimination of fire hazards, problems of public relations, public education, inspection practices, and fire "risk" analyses and practices.

3 credits
