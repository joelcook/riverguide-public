# FFP 2801 Incident Command System

In this course, students learn the essential skills and principles associated with the Incident Command System (ICS). Emphasis is placed on the organizational development of ICS as an incident management tool. Students explore the functional areas within ICS and have opportunities to develop ICS forms for a variety of incident scenarios they are presented with throughout the course.

3 credits
