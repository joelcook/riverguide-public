# FFP 2810 Firefighting Tactics and Strategy I

This course provides an overview of the basic concepts involved in firefighting, including the behavior; firefighting fundamentals; principles of extinguishment; the proper role for utilization of various fire companies, and pre-planning fire problems.

3 credits
