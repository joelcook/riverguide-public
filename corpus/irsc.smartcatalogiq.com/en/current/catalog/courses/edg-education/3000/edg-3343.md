# EDG 3343 Instructional Strategies

In this course, students explore instructional models and strategies. Emphasis is placed on principles of State standards, instructional methods, lesson planning, and instruction. Students develop knowledge of instructional models and lesson plan construction for effective implementation to include all student populations.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214) with grade of 'C' or higher
