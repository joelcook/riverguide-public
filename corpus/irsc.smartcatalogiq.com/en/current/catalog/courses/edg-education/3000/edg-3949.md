# EDG 3949 EPI Practicum

In this practicum course, students observe highly effective teachers in their approved certification area, and practice teaching various concepts to students in local area school districts. Topics include Florida Standards, subject matter competencies, and pedagogy pertinent to the discipline and required for certification. Students are placed with a qualified teacher in their certification area, for a minimum of 40 school-based hours in the classroom setting.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/corequisites: [EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214), [EDG 3343](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/3000/edg-3343) both with grade of 'C' or higher
