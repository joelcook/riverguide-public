# EDG 4940 Math Practicum

In this course, students apply practical strategies in teaching Florida Math content and standards in a K-12 public school setting. Students are assigned a cooperating teacher to provide supervision and feedback, with opportunities to integrate content and pedagogical knowledge in the K-12 classroom. Current background check (fingerprinting) in the district in which the field experience takes place is required for this course.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

If program of study is Elementary Education: [EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214) and [EDG 3620](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/3000/edg-3620) and pass GK Math. If program of study is ESE: [EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214) and pass GK Math. All courses require a grade of C or higher.

### Corequisites

Pre/Corequisite: [EDG 4377](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/4000/edg-4377) or [MAE 4326](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mae-mathematics/4000/mae-4326), either with grade of 'C' or higher
