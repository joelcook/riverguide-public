# EDG 4377 Integrating Mathematics and Science

In this course students explore specialized methods for the creation of instructional curricula and appropriate pedagogic methods for all learners in grades K-12. The development of integrated science and mathematics elementary curricula and the use of instructional approaches that correspond to the capabilities and styles of the various learners are emphasized.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

If program of study is Elementary Education: [EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214) and [EDG 3620](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/3000/edg-3620). If program of study is ESE: [EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214) and pass GK Math. All courses require grade of C or higher.
