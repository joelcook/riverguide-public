# CIS 1210 Cybersecurity 101: Living Safely in a Digital World

In this course, students explore how cybersecurity affects them in their personal and career lives. Students are introduced to topics such as best practices and behaviors related to laptop/desktop systems and mobile devices, accessing free Wi-Fi, utilizing social media, working with personal and work-related email accounts, utilizing the cloud for storage, and cybersecurity terminology.

3 credits
