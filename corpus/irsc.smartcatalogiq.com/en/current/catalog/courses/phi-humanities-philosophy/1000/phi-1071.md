# PHI 1071 Eastern Philosophies

This course examines the history, practices, and basic tenets of the three major Eastern philosophies: Hinduism, Buddhism, and Taoism.  It seeks to provide students with a broad multicultural appreciation of these traditions and their significance for promoting personal and interpersonal happiness.

3 credits
