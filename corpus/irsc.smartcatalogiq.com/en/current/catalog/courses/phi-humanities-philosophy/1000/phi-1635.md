# PHI 1635 Ethical Issues in Health Care

This course focuses on ethical analysis and decision-making in clinical health care. Topics addressed include principles of biomedical ethics, decision-making models, roles of clinicians, moral and legal aspects of confidentiality and informed consent, codes of ethics, end of life decisions, death, advance directives, euthanasia, and assisted suicide.

Gordon Rule course - must achieve a grade of ''C'' or higher for the A.A. Degree.

3 credits
