# PHI 1113 Reason and Emotion

This course examines different philosophical views of the nature of reason and its relationship to human emotion and action and provides a philosophical overview of different types of emotion. Topics discussed include theories of emotions and views of what a human emotion is based on current studies in neuropsychology.

3 credits
