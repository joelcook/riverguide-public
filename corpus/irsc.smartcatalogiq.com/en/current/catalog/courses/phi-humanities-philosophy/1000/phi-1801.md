# PHI 1801 Philosophy of Art

In this course students learn theories on the nature of the arts, including music, painting, photography, street art, and architecture. Drawing from concepts in the Western canon, students are exposed to case-based approaches that challenge our views on the nature of art.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

student must score into college-level English and reading on placement test.
