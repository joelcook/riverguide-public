# PHI 2620 Environmental Ethics

This course covers theoretical and practical issues in environmental ethics. Theories include anthropocentrism, animal rights, biocentrism, ecocentrism, and deep ecology. Students study practical topics such as climate change, population, and capitalism.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

student must score into college-level English and reading on placement test.
