# PHI 2680 Artificial Intelligence and Ethics

This course acquaints students with ethics as relevant to the design, implementation, and administration of artificial intelligence and emerging technologies. It will explore issues such as the biases of algorithms, autonomous warfare, the risks of surveillance technologies, and the effects of technologies on employment. In addition, this course will also delve into the critical examination of the western canon's influence on the development and ethical implications of artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Student must score into college-level English and reading on the placement test.
