# CHM 1045L General Chemistry I Lab

In this course, students perform **l**ab experiments which include the topics of principles of chemistry, atomic and molecular structure, chemical bonding, properties of gases, stoichiometry, liquids, and solids. This course is the lab component of [CHM 1045](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1045).

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[MAT 1033](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mat-mathematics/1000/mat-1033) with a grade of "C" or higher, or placement scores

### Corequisites

[CHM 1045](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1045)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $75.
