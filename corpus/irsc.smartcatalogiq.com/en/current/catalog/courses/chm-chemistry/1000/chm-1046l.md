# CHM 1046L General Chemistry II Lab

This course is the laboratory for [CHM 1046](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1046). Lab experiments include the topics of equilibrium, kinetics, electrochemistry, and descriptive chemistry of some elements.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[CHM 1045](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1045), [CHM 1045L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1045l) with grade of "C" or higher

### Corequisites

[CHM 1046](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1046)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $75.
