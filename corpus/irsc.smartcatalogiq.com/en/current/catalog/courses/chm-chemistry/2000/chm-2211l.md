# CHM 2211L Organic Chemistry II Lab

In this course, students investigate organic reactions and the synthesis of organic compounds such as alcohols, aromatic compounds, carboxylic acids and derivatives, aldehydes, and ketones. Students apply qualitative analysis and spectroscopic techniques, including NMR and IR, to identify and characterize organic compounds.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[CHM 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210), [CHM 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210l) all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites
