# HMV 0944 Apprenticeship - Culinary Arts - Nutrition Principles (N)

Students are introduced to intermediate commercial food and culinary arts techniques and fundamental nutrition principles.  Registration with the American Culinary Federation Educational Foundation Apprenticeship and permission of instructor are required.

48 hours
