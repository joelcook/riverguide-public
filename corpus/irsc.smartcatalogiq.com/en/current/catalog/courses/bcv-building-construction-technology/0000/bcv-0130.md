# BCV 0130 Introduction to Carpentry (N)

This course introduces the competencies essential to the carpentry industry. Topics include introduction to safety practices, fundamental tool operation and safety (manual and power), equipment utilization, identification of building materials, fasteners and hardware, basic math and math applications, basic carpentry communication and blueprint reading.

75 hours
