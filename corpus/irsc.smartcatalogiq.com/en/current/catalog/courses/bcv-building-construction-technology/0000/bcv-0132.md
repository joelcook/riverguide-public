# BCV 0132 Carpentry II (N)

This course develops in-depth skills and knowledge needed for trim and finish carpentry. Specifically, utilizing blueprints and understanding specifications to install exterior coverings and trims, interior doors, interior walls, and ceiling coverings.

400 hours
