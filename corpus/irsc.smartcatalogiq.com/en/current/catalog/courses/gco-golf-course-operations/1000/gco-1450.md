# GCO 1450 Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for Turf

In this course, students are introduced to the principles of combined proper plant selection, correct cultural practices, monitoring of pests and environmental conditions, biological controls and judicious use of pesticides to manage pest problems in golf courses.

3 credits
