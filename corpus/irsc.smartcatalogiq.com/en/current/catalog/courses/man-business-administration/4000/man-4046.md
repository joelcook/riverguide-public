# MAN 4046 Perspective in Leadership

In this course, students explore the theories of leadership and team dynamics. Focus is on the effective leadership of organizations for high performance, recognition and resolution of conflict, and member satisfaction.  Students also examine the process of team development including; motivation, communication techniques, attitudes, behaviors and effective interaction.

3 credits
