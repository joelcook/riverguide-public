# MAN 4442 International Business

In this course, students examine in-depth fundamental concepts and practices of international business. Students explore the dynamics of conducting business across borders, including topics such as globalization, international trade theories, cross-cultural communication, global marketing, and international finance. They learn how political, economic, legal, and cultural factors influence business operations in different countries and examine the challenges and opportunities faced by multinational corporations.

3 credits
