# MAN 3303 Management Concepts

In this course, students review the concepts, principles, and techniques of business leadership. Emphasis is on developing a solid foundation while centering in the real themes, demands, and opportunities of an evolving and dynamic workplace. This course incorporates leadership skill development as it relates to the core aspects of management.

3 credits
