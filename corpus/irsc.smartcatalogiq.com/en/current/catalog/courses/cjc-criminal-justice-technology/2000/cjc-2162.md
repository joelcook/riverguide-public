# CJC 2162 Probation and Parole

In this course, students explore an overview of the history and philosophical foundations of probation and parole in the United States. Students review and evaluate the continuum of correctional options from probation, through prisons, to parole and community-based re-entry services. An examination of the legal issues in probation and parole is considered, as well as the roles of probation and parole officers.

3 credits
