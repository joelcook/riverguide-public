# HSA 4922 Capstone Project in Healthcare Management

In this course, students integrate the knowledge, skills and abilities learned throughout the Healthcare Management program through competency self-assessments, interfacing with a regional healthcare practitioner, and completion of an asynchronous cohort collaborative project. Students strengthen and synthesize skills related to healthcare trends, research, leadership, policy, communication, quality improvement, competencies, human resources, marketing, law, and financial management. Permission to register required.

6 credits
