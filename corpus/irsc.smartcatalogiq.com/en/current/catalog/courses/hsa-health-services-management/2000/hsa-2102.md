# HSA 2102 Introduction to Public Health and Health Navigation

In this course, students explore topics that include the healthcare delivery system, public health, and wellness practices. Students identify the basics of ethical and legal responsibilities in healthcare as well as laws applying to the healthcare field.

3 credits
