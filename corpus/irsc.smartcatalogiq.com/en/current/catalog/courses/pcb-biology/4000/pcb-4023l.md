# PCB 4023L Cell Biology & Physiology Laboratory

In this course, students replicate procedures used to carry and propagate mammalian cell lines in culture, adapt methods used to express recombinant genes of interest in these cells, and learn various methods used to analyze cellular functions.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [BCH 4053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053), [BCH 4053L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053l), all with a "C" or higher

### Corequisites
