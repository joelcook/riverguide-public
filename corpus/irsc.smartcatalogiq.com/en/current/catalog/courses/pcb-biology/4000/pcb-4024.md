# PCB 4024 Molecular Biology

In this course, students examine the molecular mechanisms that regulate gene expression in bacteria and eukaryotes. Topics include bacterial transcription and translation mechanisms and operon-based regulation; eukaryotic RNA polymerases, promoters, and transcription factors; chromatin structure and transcriptional control; RNA processing; and eukaryotic translation.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MCB 3023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/3000/mcb-3023), [MCB 3023L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/3000/mcb-3023l), [PCB 3063](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063), [PCB 3063L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063l), with a grade of "C" or higher.
