# PCB 4701L Human Physiology Laboratory

In this course, students engage in the lab component of Human Physiology ([PCB 4701](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/4000/pcb-4701)), gaining hands-on experience in data collection and analysis related to the physiological mechanisms of various organ systems. Activities include sensory testing, cardiovascular function analysis, reflex testing, and respiratory function studies.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[ZOO 3733](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/zoo-biology/3000/zoo-3733), [ZOO 3733L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/zoo-biology/3000/zoo-3733l), all with "C" or higher

### Corequisites

[PCB 4701](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/4000/pcb-4701)

### Course Fee
