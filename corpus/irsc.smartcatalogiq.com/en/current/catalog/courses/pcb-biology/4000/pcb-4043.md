# PCB 4043 General Ecology

In this course, students explore ecological processes and concepts of natural populations, communities, and ecosystems.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [CHM 1046](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1046), [CHM 1046L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1046l), all with a grade of C or higher
