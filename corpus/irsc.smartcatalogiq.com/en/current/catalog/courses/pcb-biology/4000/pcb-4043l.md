# PCB 4043L General Ecology Laboratory

In this course, students demonstrate fundamental skills, sampling methods and data analysis used in ecological studies of natural populations, communities, and ecosystems.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [CHM 1046](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1046), [CHM 1046L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/1000/chm-1046l), all with grade of "C" or higher

### Corequisites

[PCB 4043](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/4000/pcb-4043)
