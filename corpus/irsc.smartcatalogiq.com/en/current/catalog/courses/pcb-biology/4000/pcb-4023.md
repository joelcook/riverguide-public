# PCB 4023 Cell Biology and Physiology

In this course, students learn how different biochemical, metabolic, and molecular pathways of the cell work together to produce the functions associated with cell metabolism, cell movement, development and differentiation, cell cycle, development and apoptosis.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [BCH 4053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053), [BCH 4053L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053l), all with a grade of "C" or higher.
