# PCB 3063L Introduction to Genetics Laboratory

In this course, students complete the laboratory corequisite for [PCB 3063](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063), exploring fundamental principles and applications of heredity, genetic variation, and molecular genetics in prokaryotic and eukaryotic organisms.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2010), [BSC 2010L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2010l), [CHM 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210), [CHM 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210l) with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PCB 3063](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $100.
