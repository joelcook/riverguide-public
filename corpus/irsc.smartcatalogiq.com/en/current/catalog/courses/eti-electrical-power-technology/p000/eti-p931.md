# ETI P931 Manufacturing Production Tech Certification (N)

This course provides entry-level employees with knowledge and skills preparing for the Manufacturing Skills Standards Council (MSSC) Certified Production Technician credential. Students are awarded the CPT credential at successful completion of all four (4) certification exams.
