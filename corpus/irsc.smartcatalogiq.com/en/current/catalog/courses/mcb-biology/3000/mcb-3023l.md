# MCB 3023L General Microbiology Laboratory

In this course, students engage in laboratory experiences that complement [MCB3023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/3000/mcb-3023). The lab work focuses on the structure, physiology, and ecology of bacteria, protists, viruses, and fungi.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [CHM 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210), [CHM 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210l) with a grade of C or higher.

### Corequisites

[MCB 3023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/3000/mcb-3023)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $100.
