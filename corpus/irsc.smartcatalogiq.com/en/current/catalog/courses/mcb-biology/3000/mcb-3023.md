# MCB 3023 General Microbiology

In this course, students are introduced to the biology of microorganisms, including the structure, physiology, and ecology of bacteria, protists, viruses, and fungi.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [CHM 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210), [CHM 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210l) with a grade of C or higher.
