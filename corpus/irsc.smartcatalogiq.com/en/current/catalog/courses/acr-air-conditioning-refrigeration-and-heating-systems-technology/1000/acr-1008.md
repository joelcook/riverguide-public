# ACR 1008 Principles of A/C and Refrigeration (N)

In this course, students explore the refrigeration history, refrigeration cycle, tools of the trade, tubing skills, charging techniques, safety, and principles of service management.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/corequisite: [ACR 1112](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/1000/acr-1112)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $75.
