# ACR 1112 Basic Electricity for A/C and Refrigeration (N)

In this course, students explore the principles of electricity sources, measurement devices, Ohm's law, circuits, and service management.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/corequisite: [ACR 1008](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/1000/acr-1008)
