# ACR 1740 Components of Refrigeration (N)

In this course, students explore compressors, condensers, evaporators, metering devices, service procedures, and service management.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ACR 1008](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/1000/acr-1008), [ACR 1112](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/1000/acr-1112) with grade of C or higher
