# ACR 1101 Applied Electricity I (Air Conditioning) (N)

In this course, students develop advanced skills in analyzing HVAC electrical systems, understanding component interactions, and diagnosing faults with precision. They apply critical thinking to troubleshoot and test circuits safely, mastering the function and sequence of operation for components like relays, transformers, capacitors, and control boards to perform efficient, real-world repairs.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ACR 1008](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/1000/acr-1008), [ACR 1112](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/1000/acr-1112) with grade of C or higher
