# ACR 1732 HVAC Certifications

In this course, students prepare to take HVAC industry certification exams. Students learn practical knowledge and train to safely perform services on systems containing R-410A refrigerant. Additionally, students explore the basic skills of Green mechanical technology and Green alternatives relating to air conditioning and heating systems. Students prepare to take up to 3 industry certifications at the completion of this course.

3 credits

### Prerequisites
