# ACR 0525 HVAC/R3 (N)

In this course, students explore the installation of a residential heating and air conditioning system, and troubleshoot electrical control systems. Other topics covered include mechanical systems and start up and check out procedures.

150 hours

### Prerequisites

[ACR 0100](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0100), [ACR 0523](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0523)
