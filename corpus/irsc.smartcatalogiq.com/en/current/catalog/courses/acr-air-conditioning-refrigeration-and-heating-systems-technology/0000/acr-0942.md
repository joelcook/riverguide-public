# ACR 0942 Apprenticeship - HVAC 3 (N)

In this course, students continue to expand their knowledge of heating, air conditioning, and refrigeration system components and accessories. Communication and industry related computer skills, along with discussions concerning entrepreneurship will be utilized to further develop the student’s skillset.

90 hours

### Prerequisites

Permission of instructor
