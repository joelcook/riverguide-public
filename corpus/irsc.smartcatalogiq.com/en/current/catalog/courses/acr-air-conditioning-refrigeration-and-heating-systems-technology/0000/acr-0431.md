# ACR 0431 HVAC/R9 (N)

In this course, students explore the advanced technical skills to install, maintain, and repair heating, air conditioning, and refrigeration systems. Topics include identifying and measuring indoor air quality and understanding new technologies in heating, air conditioning and refrigeration installations.

150 hours
