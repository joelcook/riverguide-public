# ACR 0548 HVAC/R4 (N)

**In this course, students explore advanced skills in installation of a residential heating and air conditioning system and the related start up procedures. Other topics include electrical wiring diagrams, troubleshooting electrical components, circuits, and systems, and refrigerant cycles.**

150 hours

### Prerequisites

[ACR 0100](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0100) [ACR 0523](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0523), [ACR 0525](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0525)
