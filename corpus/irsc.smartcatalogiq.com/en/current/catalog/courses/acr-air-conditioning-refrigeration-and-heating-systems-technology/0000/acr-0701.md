# ACR 0701 HVAC/R6 (N)

In this course, students explore solid state electronics as used in heating, air conditioning and refrigeration, indoor air quality standards, mastery of installation, and maintenance and repair of heating, air conditioning, and refrigeration systems.

150 hours

### Prerequisites

[ACR 0523](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0523), [ACR 0100](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0100), [ACR 0525](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0525), [ACR 0548](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0548), [ACR 0607](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acr-air-conditioning-refrigeration-and-heating-systems-technology/0000/acr-0607)
