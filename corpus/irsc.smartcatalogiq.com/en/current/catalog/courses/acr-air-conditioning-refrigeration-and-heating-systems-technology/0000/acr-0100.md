# ACR 0100 HVAC/R2 (N)

In this course, students explore electricity, atomic theory, sources, measuring devices, Ohm's Law, calculating power, circuits, symbols, load devices, controls, conductors, insulators, power distribution, voltage systems, motors, and safety. Employment skills and the value of computer and communication skills are integrated throughout.

150 hours
