# ACR 0578 HVAC/R8 (N)

In this course, students explore the servicing and troubleshooting commercial heating and air conditioning components. Topics include maintaining, troubleshooting, and repairing commercial heating systems, as well as maintaining and repairing thermal storage systems.

150 hours
