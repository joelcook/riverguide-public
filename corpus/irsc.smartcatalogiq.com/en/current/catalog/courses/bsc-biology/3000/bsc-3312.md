# BSC 3312 Marine Biology

In this course, students explore the ocean environment, the abiotic and biotic factors that influence marine populations, and the physical and behavioral adaptations of organisms to life in a liquid environment.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011). [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l)
