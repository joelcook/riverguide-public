# BSC 3465L Biotechnology II Laboratory

In this course, students examine advanced techniques and applications of biotechnology, recombinant DNA technology, with applications in plant and animal systems, and the impacts of biotechnology on society in a laboratory setting.  This course is the lab component of [BSC 3465](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3465), and is a continuation of [BSC 3464L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3464l).

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 3464](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3464), [BSC 3464L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3464l) with a grade of "C" or higher

### Corequisites

[BSC 3465](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3465)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $100.
