# BSC 3464 Biotechnology I

In this course, students examine genes and genetics, molecular biotechnology, theories and applications of biotechnology research, genome analysis and bioinformatics. Also discussed are biotechnology of microbial systems used in diagnosis, therapeutics, and vaccines.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [PCB 3063](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063), [PCB 3063L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063l), all with grade of "C" or higher
