# BSC 3464L Biotechnology I Laboratory

In this course, students examine techniques and applications of biotechnology, recombinant DNA technology, with applications in plant and animal systems, and the impacts of biotechnology on society in a laboratory setting.  This course is the lab component of [BSC 3464](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3464).

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [PCB 3063](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063), [PCB 3063L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063l), all with grade of "C" or higher

### Corequisites

[BSC 3464](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3464)
