# BSC 1005L Life Science Lab

In this course, students learn the basic principles of biology through virtual experiences reinforcing subject matter present in BSC1005, Life Science. Topics include the metric system, the scientific method, cell structure, function and reproduction, microscopy, gene expression, and climate change. This laboratory does not fulfill the credit requirements for biology majors.

1 credit

### Corequisites

[BSC 1005](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/1000/bsc-1005)
