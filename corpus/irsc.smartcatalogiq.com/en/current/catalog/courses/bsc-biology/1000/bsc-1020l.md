# BSC 1020L Introduction to Human Biology Lab

This course is the laboratory component for [BSC 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/1000/bsc-1020), Human Biology.  Lab experiences include microscope technique, basic chemistry, cell structure, genetics and body systems terminology.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

Must score into college-level English and reading

### Corequisites

[BSC 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/1000/bsc-1020)
