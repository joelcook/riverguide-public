# BSC 2010 General Biology I

In this course students will apply the scientific method to critically examine and explain the natural world. This course will cover molecular biology, cellular biology, genetics, metabolism, and replication.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENC 0017](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/0000/enc-0017) with a min grade of S; OR [ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101) with a min grade of C; OR Score into College-Level Placement in English and Reading

### Corequisites

[BSC 2010L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2010l) with a grade of C or higher
