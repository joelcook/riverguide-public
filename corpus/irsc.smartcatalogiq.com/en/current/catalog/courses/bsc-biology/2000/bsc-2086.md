# BSC 2086 Anatomy & Physiology II

In this course, students study the anatomy and physiology of human systems. Topics covered are the circulatory, digestive, respiratory, excretory, endocrine, and reproductive systems.  This course is a continuation of [[BSC 2085](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085)](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085).

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2085](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085) and [BSC 2085L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085l) with C or better

### Corequisites

[BSC 2086L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086l)
