# BSC 2011L General Biology II Lab

In this course, students investigate the plant and animal kingdoms, comparative physiology of vertebrate systems, plant and animal development, and ecology. This is the lab component for [BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011).

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2010), [BSC 2010L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2010l) with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $75.
