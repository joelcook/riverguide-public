# BSC 2086L Anatomy & Physiology II Lab

In this course, students engage in lab experiences in the circulatory, digestive, respiratory, excretory, endocrine, and reproductive systems. This course is the lab component for [BSC 2086](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086).

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2085](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085) and BSC 2085Ll with C or better

### Corequisites

[BSC 2086](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $75.
