# BSC 2010L General Biology I Lab

In this course, students engage in laboratory procedures that center around topics covered in the corequisite [BSC 2010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2010) lecture. Fundamental concepts discussed include the metric system, the scientific method, the chemical composition, and processes constituting life, as well as genetic processes and the means of trait inheritance.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[ENC 0017](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/0000/enc-0017) with a min grade of S; OR [ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101) with a min grade of C; OR Score into College-Level Placement in English and Reading

### Corequisites

[BSC 2010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2010)
