# BSC 4434 Introduction to Bioinformatics

In this course, students are introduced to computational tools for analyzing molecular data, including database use, sequence alignment, phylogenetics, and genomic analysis.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[STA 2023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sta-mathematics/2000/sta-2023),[BSC 3464](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3464), [BSC 3464L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/3000/bsc-3464l), all with a grade of "C" or higher
