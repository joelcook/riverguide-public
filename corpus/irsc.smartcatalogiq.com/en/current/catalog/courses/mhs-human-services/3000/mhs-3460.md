# MHS 3460 Crisis Intervention

In this course, students identify and examine the stages of crisis response, types of crises, and techniques of crisis intervention. Focus includes an examination of the psychological and physical responses to crisis situations, identification of at-risk individuals, and prevention of crises.

3 credits
