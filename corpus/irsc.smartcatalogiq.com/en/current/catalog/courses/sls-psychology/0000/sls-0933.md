# SLS 0933 Special Topics in Work Skills (N)

This course demonstrates how to develop realistic employment goals as well as identify potential careers available in the community. Interpersonal skills on the job, understanding the workplace culture and comparing the differences in workplace settings through site visits and classroom lectures are emphasized. Course content covers specific skills and the tools, technology and supplies needed to complete specific jobs for occupations such as housekeeping, laundry, retail, culinary, assembly and landscaping/horticulture.

An on-the-job component teaches workplace applications of the theoretical concepts introduced in the classroom components.

15 - 300 hours
