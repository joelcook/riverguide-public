# SLS 0460 Specialized Career Education, Basic 1 (N)

Students learn about personal and career exploration. Students identify and describe personal skills, interests, values, experiences, personality traits, and academic abilities. Students review and discuss career and employment opportunities, determine realistic employment goals, explain individual rights and responsibilities in the workplace, as well as describe the rights, responsibilities, and benefits of employment.

150 hours
