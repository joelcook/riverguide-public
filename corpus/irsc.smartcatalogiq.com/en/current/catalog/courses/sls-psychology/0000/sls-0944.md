# SLS 0944 Specialized Career Education, Internship (N)

This course consists of an on-the-job training component. Students are either placed in a job shadowing opportunity or work one-on-one with an instructor to prepare for employment.

150 hours

### Prerequisites

[SLS 0464](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sls-psychology/0000/sls-0464) with a grade of "S"
