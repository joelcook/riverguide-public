# SLS 0464 Specialized Career Education, Advanced 2 (N)

In this course, students learn about workplace organization; the roles within teams, work units, departments, organizations, and the larger environment. Students work on identifying and demonstrating positive human relations and leadership skills, critical thinking skills, creativity and innovation for problem solving.

150 hours

### Prerequisites

[SLS 0463](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sls-psychology/0000/sls-0463) with a grade of "S"
