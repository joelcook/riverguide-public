# SLS 0462 Specialized Career Education, Basic 3 (N)

In this course, students learn how to be a successful employee and to request work-related reasonable accommodations. The course includes an introduction to appropriate oral and written communication and industry-related math skills.

150 hours

### Prerequisites

[SLS 0461](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sls-psychology/0000/sls-0461) with a grade of "S"
