# COP 2001 Computer Programming II

In this course, students explore how to design and analyze simple algorithms and data structures as a continuation of [COP 2000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cop-computer-science/2000/cop-2000). Students are introduced to object-oriented programming concepts such as, data abstraction and encapsulation, classes and objects, inheritance, polymorphism, and template functions, classes, and libraries.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[COP 2000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cop-computer-science/2000/cop-2000) with grade of C or higher

### Course Fee

Lab fee $20.
