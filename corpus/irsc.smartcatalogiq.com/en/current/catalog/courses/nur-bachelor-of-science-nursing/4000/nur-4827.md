# NUR 4827C Leadership and Management in Professional Nursing

In this course, students explore principles of nursing leadership and management with an emphasis on decision-making, priority setting, delegating, communicating, team building, and managing fiscal and human resources. The focus is on preparation of the professional nurse for leadership responsibilities in collaboration with the interdisciplinary health environment.  Students engage in leadership experiences across a variety of clinical settings.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[NUR 3846](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/nur-bachelor-of-science-nursing/3000/nur-3846), [NUR 3164](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/nur-bachelor-of-science-nursing/3000/nur-3164), [NUR 4837](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/nur-bachelor-of-science-nursing/4000/nur-4837) all with grade of C or higher
