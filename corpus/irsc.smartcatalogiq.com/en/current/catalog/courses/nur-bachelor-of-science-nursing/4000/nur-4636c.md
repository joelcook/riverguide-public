# NUR 4636C Community Health Nursing

In this course, students learn community and public health nursing in relation to disaster preparedness, epidemiology, and environment. The focus of this course is on nursing care designed to prevent and/or reduce risk of disease and injury and promotion of health and wellness in varied populations across the age spectrum.  Students engage in a variety of community-based learning experiences.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/Corequisites: [NUR 3164](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/nur-bachelor-of-science-nursing/3000/nur-3164), [NUR 3846](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/nur-bachelor-of-science-nursing/3000/nur-3846), [NUR 4837](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/nur-bachelor-of-science-nursing/4000/nur-4837) with grade of C or higher
