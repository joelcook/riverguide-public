# NUR 3846 Nursing Theory

In this course, students explore the development of professional nursing and nursing theory. Students apply grand, middle-range, and practice theories to their personal nursing practice. Students review nursing theories and identify the theory whose principles align with and inform their professional practice.

3 credits
