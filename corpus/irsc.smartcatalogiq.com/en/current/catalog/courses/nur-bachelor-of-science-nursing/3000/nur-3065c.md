# NUR 3065C Nursing Assessment

In this course, students explore the holistic assessment of individuals by advancing students' knowledge and skills in history taking, risk appraisal, health promotion, and physical examination techniques. The clinical learning experiences provide an opportunity to expand diagnostic reasoning skills in assessing variations to develop a plan of care.

3 credits
