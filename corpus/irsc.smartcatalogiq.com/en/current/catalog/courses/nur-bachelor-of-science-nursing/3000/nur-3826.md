# NUR 3826 Ethical and Legal Issues in Health Care

In this course, students explore the ethical and legal rights and responsibilities of the professional nurse in a changing health environment. The emphasis of this course is to determine the nurse's individual accountability within the legal scope of practice and to clarify individual accountability for ethical nursing practice.

3 credits
