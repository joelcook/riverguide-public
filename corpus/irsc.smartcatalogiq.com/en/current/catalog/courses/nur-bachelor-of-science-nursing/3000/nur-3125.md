# NUR 3125 Pathophysiology

In this course, students explore advanced study of pathophysiology and symptomatology across the lifespan due to alterations in selected human biological systems. The focus is on alterations in physiological functions as manifestations of disease. Emphasis is on relating signs, symptoms, and laboratory findings of common alterations and understanding nursing interventions to promote adaptation.

3 credits
