# GER 1120 Elementary German I

In this course, students learn the basics of speaking, reading, and writing in German, while also exploring the diverse cultures of German-speaking countries. Designed for beginners or those with minimal German background, the class focuses on building essential language skills and understanding German grammatical structures. Through engaging activities, students improve their ability to communicate in German and gain insights into the traditions and lifestyles of communities where German is spoken.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

student must score into college-level English on placement test.
