# GER 1121 Elementary German II

In this course, students deepen their understanding of German by enhancing their speaking, reading, and writing skills, and further exploring the rich cultures of German-speaking countries.  Building on the foundation laid in [GER 1120](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ger-german/1000/ger-1120), this class expands students' knowledge of German grammatical structures and vocabulary. Through a variety of engaging activities, students not only refine their ability to communicate more effectively in German but also deepen their appreciation for the cultural nuances and historical contexts of German-speaking communities.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[GER 1120](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ger-german/1000/ger-1120) or its equivalent.
