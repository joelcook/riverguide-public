# EEX 2030 Teaching Exceptional Learners in Inclusive Setting

In this course students develop the knowledge and skills necessary for inclusive general and special educators to meet the needs of students with exceptionalities in grades K-12. Focus is on specialized instructional methods and strategies for students with exceptionalities. The adaptation of general education curricula and the use of evidenced-based instructional approaches that correspond to the capabilities and styles of the various learners are emphasized. This course meets Florida recertification credit for Teaching Students with Disabilities (as approved by the school district).

1 credit
