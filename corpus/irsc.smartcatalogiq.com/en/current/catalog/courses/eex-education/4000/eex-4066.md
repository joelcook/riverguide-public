# EEX 4066 Educational Programming in Exceptional Student Education

In this course students explore curriculum, methods, and materials required as part of the K-12 Exceptional Student Education (ESE) continuum of services. Topics include eligibility; assessment; Individual Education Plans (IEP's); Universal Design for Learning (UDL); High Leverage Practices (HLP's); differentiation; varied instructional models; collaborative consultation; transition and functional living skills used in programs for individuals with mild to moderate disabilities.

3 credits
