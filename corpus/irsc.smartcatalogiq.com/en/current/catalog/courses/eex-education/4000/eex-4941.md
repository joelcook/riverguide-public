# EEX 4941 Internship in Exceptional Student Education

In this course pre-service teachers demonstrate pre-professional competencies during a full-time internship in a K-12 Exceptional Student Education (ESE) instructional role in a school approved by the department and the school district. Permission to register required.

9 credits

### Prerequisites

Senior level status, all program requirements must be met, and permission of department [EEX 3103](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/3000/eex-3103), [EEX 4221](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4221), [EEX 4601](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4601), [EEX 4264](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4264), [EEX 4265](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4265)., all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[EEX 4945](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4945)

### Course Fee
