# EEX 4945 Seminar in Exceptional Student Education

In this course pre-service teachers receive support in instructional strategies, planning techniques, evaluation procedures, and classroom management skills during their full-day internship in a K-12 Exceptional Student Education (ESE) school setting to demonstrate professional competencies.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Senior level status, all program requirements must be met, and permission of department; [EEX 3103](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/3000/eex-3103), [EEX 4221](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4221), [EEX 4601](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4601), [EEX 4264](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4264), [EEX 4265](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4265), all with a grade of C or higher
