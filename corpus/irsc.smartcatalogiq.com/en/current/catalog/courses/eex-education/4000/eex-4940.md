# EEX 4940 Student Teaching/Exceptional Student Internship and Seminar

In this course students demonstrate pre-professional competencies during a full-time internship in a public school approved by the department and the school district. Permission to register required.

12 credits

### Prerequisites

Senior level status, all program requirements must be met, and permission of clinical education coordinator. [EEX 3103](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/3000/eex-3103), [EEX 4221](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4221), [EEX 4601](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4601), [EEX 4264](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4264), [EEX 4265](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eex-education/4000/eex-4265), all with grade of C or higher

### Course Fee

Insurance fee $4.75.
