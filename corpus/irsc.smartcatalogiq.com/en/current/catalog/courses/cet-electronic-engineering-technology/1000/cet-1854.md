# CET 1854 Introduction to Wireless Technology

Students explore key wireless networking topics, including wireless technology and architecture, network design, types of wireless networks, and applications in this course. The special focus is on technical matters, wireless communication, wireless networking, and wireless LANs products. It is recommended that all students have at least a basic knowledge of networking, including Protocol, the OSI module, and TCIP/IP.

3 credits
