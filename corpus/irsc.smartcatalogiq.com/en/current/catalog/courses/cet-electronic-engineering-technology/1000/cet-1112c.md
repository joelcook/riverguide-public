# CET 1112C Logic Circuits I

In this course, students learn the logic circuitry used in digital electronic systems. Topics covered are number systems, logic gates, Boolean algebra, Karnaugh maps, DeMorgan's Theorems, IC specifications, interfacing, encoders, decoders, and flip-flops. Students build and analyze simple digital circuits to evaluate the theory behind the logic gates.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/Coreq: [EET 1215C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/1000/eet-1215c) with grade of 'C' or higher
