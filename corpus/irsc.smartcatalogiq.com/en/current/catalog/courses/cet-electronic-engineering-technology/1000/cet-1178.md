# CET 1178 A+ Certification Hardware

In this course, students apply the practical skills needed to install, configure, and troubleshoot computer hardware in preparation for entry-level IT support roles. Aligned with the current objectives of the CompTIA A+ Core 1 (Hardware) certification, the course emphasizes experiential learning and real-world problem-solving as students develop proficiency in hardware setup, networking tools, and mobile device configuration.

3 credits
