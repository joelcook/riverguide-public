# LAE 4416 Children's Literature in Elementary Education

In this course students select, evaluate and use fiction, nonfiction and poetry for instructional, informational, and recreational purposes in Elementary Education. Topics include the development of analytical thinking, writing skills, and oral expression as they are applied to the study of children's literature.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Passing score on both the General Knowledge ELA and General Knowledge Essay
