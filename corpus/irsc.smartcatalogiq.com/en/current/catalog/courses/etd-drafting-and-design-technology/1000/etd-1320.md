# ETD 1320 Introduction to AutoCAD

In this course, students explore AutoCAD software for 2-Dimensional technical drawings. Students focus on elementary procedures, use of menus and ribbons, and demonstration of drawing commands. Students use this knowledge in the construction of numerous technical drawings. Extensive hands-on practice using AutoCAD software is required as students complete a series of tutorials, exercises, and drawings.

3 credits
