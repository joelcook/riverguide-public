# ETD 1842 3D Visualization with AutoCAD

Students explore 3D Studio VIZ, a 3D visualization software. Students delve into the creation of 3D models combined with intuitive multimedia tools for the creation of presentation graphics. Students practice methods of modeling, creation of lighting systems, application of materials and production of rendered images along with special effects.

3 credits
