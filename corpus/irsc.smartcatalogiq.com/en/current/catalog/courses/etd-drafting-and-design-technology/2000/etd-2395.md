# ETD 2395 Computer Aided Drafting - Architectural

In this course, students construct architectural drawings using current Autodesk software with specialized CAD commands applicable to the architectural field. Students explore real world architectural modules using industry standard CAD software.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BCN 1250](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bcn-building-construction-technology/1000/bcn-1250) and [ETD 1320](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/etd-drafting-and-design-technology/1000/etd-1320), both with grade of 'C' or higher
