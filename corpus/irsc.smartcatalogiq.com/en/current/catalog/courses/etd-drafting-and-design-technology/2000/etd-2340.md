# ETD 2340 AutoCAD Level 2

In this course, students create highlighted 3D drawings and renderings using intermediate-level techniques in industry-standard computer aided drafting (CAD) software. Students focus on implementing productivity tools in the design process through various drawing, editing, and display techniques.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ETD 1320](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/etd-drafting-and-design-technology/1000/etd-1320).
