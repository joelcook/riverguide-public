# ETD 2364 3D Modeling with Solidworks

In this course, students investigate mechanical design utilizing advanced computer-aided drafting techniques with the current SolidWorks software. Students focus on three-dimensional coordinate systems, parametric modeling, wire frame development, surface generation, and multiple-part/assembly construction techniques.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ETD 1320](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/etd-drafting-and-design-technology/1000/etd-1320)
