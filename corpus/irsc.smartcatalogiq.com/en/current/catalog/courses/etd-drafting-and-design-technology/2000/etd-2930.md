# ETD 2930 Special Topics in Drafting

In this course students explore computer-aided drafting topics of special and current interest. Students focus on technical updates of CAD software along with exploration of newly developed processes or CAD software pertaining to specialized engineering disciplines.

1 - 3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ETD 1320](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/etd-drafting-and-design-technology/1000/etd-1320) or [ETD 2364](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/etd-drafting-and-design-technology/2000/etd-2364) and permission of instructor.
