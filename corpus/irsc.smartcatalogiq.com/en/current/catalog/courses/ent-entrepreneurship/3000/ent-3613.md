# ENT 3613 Creativity in Entrepreneurship

In this course, students dive into the connection between creativity and business, learning how to think outside the box and solve problems in innovative ways. They discover how to spot opportunities, take smart risks, and develop unique products or services that tackle real-world challenges. Through interactive activities, case studies, and group projects, students brainstorm ideas, test their feasibility, and create practical solutions that bring value to today’s fast-changing markets. This is the first course in a sequence of courses designed for a student to develop a practical business plan.

3 credits
