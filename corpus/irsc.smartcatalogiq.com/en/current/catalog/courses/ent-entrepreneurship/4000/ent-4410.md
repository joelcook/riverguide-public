# ENT 4410 Small Business Accounting and Finance

In this course, students explore financial and economic concepts and the fundamentals of small business finance. Students create financial reports to assist in decision making and apply financial forecasting. Additionally, they explore financing options and build the financial portion of the business plan. This course should be taken in the second or third semester of the program.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENT 3613](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ent-entrepreneurship/3000/ent-3613) with a grade of 'C' or higher
