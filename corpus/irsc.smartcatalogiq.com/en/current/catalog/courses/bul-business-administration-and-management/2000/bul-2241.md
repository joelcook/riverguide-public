# BUL 2241 Business Law I

In this course, students explore American Law, Contract Law, and Article II of the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC), which deals with the sale of goods. Through the study of content and cases, the students learn the fundamentals of preventive law, tort law, criminal law, the court system, and legal terminology.

3 credits
