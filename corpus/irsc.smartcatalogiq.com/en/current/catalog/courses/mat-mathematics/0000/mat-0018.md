# MAT 0018 Developmental Math I (N)

In this course, students prepare to transition from arithmetic to algebra. Algebra concepts (variables, signed numbers, order of operations, and equations) are introduced early and then repeated with traditional arithmetic concepts (fractions, decimals, and percents).

3 credits
