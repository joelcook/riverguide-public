# MAT 0028 Developmental Math II (N)

In this course, students prepare forIntermediate Algebra ([MAT 1033](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mat-mathematics/1000/mat-1033)) or Mathematical Thinking ([MGF 1130](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mgf-mathematics/1000/mgf-1130)).  Topics include properties of integers and rational numbers, integer exponents, simple linear equations and inequalities, operations on polynomials, beginning techniques of factoring, introduction to graphing, and introduction to operations on rational expressions.

Credit not applied toward degree.

3 credits
