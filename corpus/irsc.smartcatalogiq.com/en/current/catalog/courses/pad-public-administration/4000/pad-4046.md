# PAD 4046 Values and Ethics in Public and Non-Profit Organizations

In this course, students examine values and, ethics, in the context of public service.  It emphasizes perspectives on values and ethical systems and the influence of ethics on public administration, organizational and individual behavior, and process.

3 credits
