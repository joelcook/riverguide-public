# BCT 1760 Building Codes and Specifications

In this course, students explore how to locate information in building codes. Students will learn how to apply basic definitions, fire zones, construction types, construction regulations, and selected specialized subjects to local building. Topics include National Electrical Code, Standard for Hurricane.

3 credits
