# BCT 1562 Plumbing and Electrical Systems

In this course, students explore residential, commercial, and industrial wiring and plumbing practice. Students will participate in concentrated instruction and lab activities associated with how to wire and plumb a residential home. Practical application is combined with theory so that students can analyze and compare the function of common plumbing and electrical components and materials.

3 credits
