# PET 2622 Care and Prevention of Athletic Injuries

This course provides the knowledge and ability to make educated decisions regarding the most appropriate course of action when confronted with an injured athlete. The focus is on recognition of serious, life-threatening conditions as well as less serious injuries that must be referred to a medical doctor.

3 credits
