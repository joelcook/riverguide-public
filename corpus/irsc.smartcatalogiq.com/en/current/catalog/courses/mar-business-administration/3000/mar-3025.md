# MAR 3025 Small Business Marketing

In this course, students explore how small businesses innovate and market their products and services to consumers. Through a hands-on, applied approach, students develop the marketing section of a business plan, focusing on strategies to guide small business success. This course should be taken in the second or third semester of the program.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENT 3613](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ent-entrepreneurship/3000/ent-3613) with a grade of 'C' or higher
