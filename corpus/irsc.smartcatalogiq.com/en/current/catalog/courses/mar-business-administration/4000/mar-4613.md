# MAR 4613 Marketing Research

In this course, students construct a marketing research study. Students apply the steps of the marketing research process to current marketing issues and focus on primary (quantitative and qualitative) and secondary information to gain insight and experience in defining problems including methodology design, sampling, data collection, data analysis, and reporting on findings.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MAR 3023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mar-business-administration/3000/mar-3023) with grade of 'C' or higher
