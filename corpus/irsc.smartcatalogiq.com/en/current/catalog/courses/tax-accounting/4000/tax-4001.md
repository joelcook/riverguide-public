# TAX 4001 Individual Taxation

In this course, students examine the Federal Income Tax system, with specific emphasis on tax regulations affecting individual taxpayers and sole proprietorships.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ACG 2071](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acg-accounting/2000/acg-2071) with a grade of C or higher
