# TAX 4101 Corporate Taxation

This course provides a comprehensive study of corporate taxation under the rules and regulations of the Internal Revenue Code, with an introductory overview of partnership taxation.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ACG 3131](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acg-accounting/3000/acg-3131) with grade of "C" or higher
