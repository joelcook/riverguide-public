# AER 0418 Automobile Brake System Technician (N)

In this course students examine the automotive service industry. Students explore career opportunities and requirements of a professional auto mechanic.Students study diagnostics and repair of brake systems, drum brakes, disc brakes, power assist units, electronic brakes, traction, and stability control.

150 hours
