# AER 0453 Automobile Suspension and Steering (N)

In this course students examine the automotive service industry. Students explore career opportunities and requirements of a professional auto mechanic. Students study diagnostics and repair of general suspension, steering systems, front suspensions, rear suspensions, wheel alignment, and tires.

150 hours
