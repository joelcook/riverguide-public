# AER 0503 Automobile Engine Performance Technician (N)

In this course students examine basic and advanced engine performance. Students study basic and advanced engine performance diagnosis, service and repair of engines, ignition, fuel, air induction, exhaust, computer, engine and emission control systems.

300 hours
