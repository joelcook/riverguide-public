# AER 0274 Manual Drivetrain and Axle Technician (N)

In this course students explore the automotive service industry. Students explore career opportunities and requirements of a professional auto mechanic.Students study diagnostics and repair of drive train, clutch, transmission, transaxle, half shaft universal, constant-velocity joint, rear axle, ring and pinion gears, differential case assemble, limited slip differential, drive shaft, and four-wheel drive/all-wheel drive.

150 hours
