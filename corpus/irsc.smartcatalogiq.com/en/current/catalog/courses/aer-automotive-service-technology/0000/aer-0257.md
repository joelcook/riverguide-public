# AER 0257 Automatic Transmission and Transaxle Technician (N)

In this course students examine the automotive service industry. Students explore career opportunities and requirements of a professional auto mechanic. Students study diagnostics, repair, service, and operation of automatic transmission/transaxles.

150 hours
