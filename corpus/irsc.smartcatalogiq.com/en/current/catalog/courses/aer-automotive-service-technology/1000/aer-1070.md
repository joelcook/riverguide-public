# AER 1070 Automotive Service Management (N)

In this course, students explore the operation and management of an automotive service facility. Students visit facilities and meet with service managers and other personnel to create reports.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
