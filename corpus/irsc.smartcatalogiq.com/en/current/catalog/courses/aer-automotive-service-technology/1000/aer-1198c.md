# AER 1198C Engine Overhaul, Repair and Testing (N)

In this course, students study the process of testing and repairing engine mechanical problems. The student measures various engine parts and components and compares those measurements to manufacture specifications to determine the appropriate part replacement.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c)
