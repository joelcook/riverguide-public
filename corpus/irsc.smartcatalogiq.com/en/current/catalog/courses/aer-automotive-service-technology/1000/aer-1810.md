# AER 1810 Automotive Work Experience (N)

In this course, students participate in an internship or on-the-job training. Students are required to be employed in an approved job in the automotive field. Students submit timecards and flat rate sheets weekly.

1 - 3 credits

### Prerequisites

[AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
