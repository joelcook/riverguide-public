# AER 1498C Suspension, Steering, and Alignment (N)

In this course, students investigate tire repair, removal/installation, and balancing. Students also explore tire wear and vehicle alignments, including lane assist and other safety features.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

pre/coreq: [AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
