# AER 1598C Automotive Brake Systems (N)

In this course, students focus on automotive braking systems. Drum, disc, and parking brake systems are covered, along with Anti-Lock (ABS) systems. Students learn to bend and flare brake pipes and machine rotors and drums.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

pre/coreq: [AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
