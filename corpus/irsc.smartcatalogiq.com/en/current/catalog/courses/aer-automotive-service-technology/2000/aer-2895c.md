# AER 2895C Advanced Engine Performance (N)

In this course, students explore emission control devices, while building on skills acquired in the previous engine performance course. The focus is sensor diagnosis and the exhaust system.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)

### Corequisites

[AER 2898C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/2000/aer-2898c)
