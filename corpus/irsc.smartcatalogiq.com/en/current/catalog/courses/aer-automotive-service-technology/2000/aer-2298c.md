# AER 2298C Automatic Transmissions and Transaxles (N)

In this course, students examine how automatic transmissions work, are serviced and repaired, and how hydraulics and planetary gearsets work in combination to achieve various gear ratios and transmit power.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

pre/coreq: [AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
