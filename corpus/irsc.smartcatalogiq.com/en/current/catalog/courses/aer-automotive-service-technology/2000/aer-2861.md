# AER 2861 Electric Vehicle Drive Systems (N)

In this course, students explore the electric vehicle drive systems and motors. Topics covered include electric machine propulsion, regenerative braking and coasting modes, powertrain architectures and powertrain components, and the diagnostics of EV drive system errors.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
