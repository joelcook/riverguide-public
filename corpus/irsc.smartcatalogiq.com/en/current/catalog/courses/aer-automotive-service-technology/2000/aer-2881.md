# AER 2881 Electric Vehicle Power Inverter Systems and Controls (N)

In this course, students explore topics in 3-Phase Power inverter systems and controls, and DC-DC converter systems. Topics covered include system overviews, operations, service, and diagnosis.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
