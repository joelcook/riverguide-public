# AER 2695C Advanced Automotive Electrical Systems (N)

In this course, students delve into basic electricity, introducing electronics used in sensors and computer control circuits, and diagnosis of sensor and control circuits with DVOMs and oscilloscopes.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
