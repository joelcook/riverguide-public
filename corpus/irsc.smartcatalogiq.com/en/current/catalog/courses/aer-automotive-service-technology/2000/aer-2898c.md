# AER 2898C Engine Performance (N)

In this course, students focus on ignition, fuel, and computer control systems. Students use on board diagnostics to view engine operating parameters and analyze how computers control ignition timing and fuel delivery to achieve a balance between fuel economy, emissions, and performance. Students examine how diagnostic tools and service information are used to determine component or circuit operating characteristics and failures.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

pre/coreq: [AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c)
