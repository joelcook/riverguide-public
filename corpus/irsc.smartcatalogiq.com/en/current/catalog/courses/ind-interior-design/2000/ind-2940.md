# IND 2940 Internship

In this course, students apply knowledge gained in the classroom to real-world interior design business situations.  Students develop skills in business principles and practices, promotional techniques, and career development through hands-on practicum experiences.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[IND 1019](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/1000/ind-1019), [IND 1935](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/1000/ind-1935), [IND 2910](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/2000/ind-2910), all with grade of 'C' or higher
