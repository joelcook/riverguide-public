# IND 2523 Interior Design Portfolio

In this course, students refine advanced design skills and prepare for entry into the design profession by developing a professional portfolio.  Students compile and present a cohesive body of work suitable for final packaging and presentation to prospective employers.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[IND 2016](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/2000/ind-2016), [IND 2911](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/2000/ind-2911), all with grade of 'C' or higher
