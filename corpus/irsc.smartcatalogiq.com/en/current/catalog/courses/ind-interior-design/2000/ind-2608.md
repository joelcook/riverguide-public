# IND 2608 Sustainable Design for Interiors

In this course, students are introduced to the basic theories and practices of ecology relating to interior design products and practices. Students become aware of the impact of their material and design selections on the environment. "Green" or "Eco" is an emerging field of importance in both residential and commercial interior design.

3 credits
