# IND 2016 Commercial Interior Design I

In this course, students are presented with the design processes used in commercial interiors. Space planning, human factors, technical issues, furniture and material selection, budgets, code requirements, and presentation techniques are reviewed.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[IND 1935](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/1000/ind-1935), [IND 1019](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/1000/ind-1019), all with grade of 'C' or higher
