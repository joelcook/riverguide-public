# IND 2931 Special Topics in Interior Design

This course focuses on interior design technology topics of current interest. Main areas of study include technical updates of CAD software and exploration of newly developed interior design technology specific to licensed, professional interior designers including kitchen and bath designers.

.5 - 5 credits
