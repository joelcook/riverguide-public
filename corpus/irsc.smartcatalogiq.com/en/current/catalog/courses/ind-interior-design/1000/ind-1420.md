# IND 1420 Materials and Sources of Interior Design

In this course students explore the non-textile construction materials of interior design. Wood, plastic, stone, masonry, glass, paint, wall coverings, and marble are a few of the materials students study. Students also study appliances and bathroom fixtures. Students learn the applications of materials and specifications recommended by professional trade associations.

3 credits
