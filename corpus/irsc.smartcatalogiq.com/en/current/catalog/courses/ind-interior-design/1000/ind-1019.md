# IND 1019 Interior Design Proxemics & Environmental

In this course, students examine the use and psychology of space planning and the effect of environmental factors.  This involves the understanding of the space required for various functions in everyday life, as well as the specific functions of workers and the items used (furniture, machines, appliances, etc.) Students explore the basic environmental requirements to perform those functions.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[IND 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/1000/ind-1020), [IND 1401](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/1000/ind-1401), all with grade of 'C' or higher
