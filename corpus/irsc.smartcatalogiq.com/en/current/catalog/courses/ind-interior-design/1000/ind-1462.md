# IND 1462 Introduction to Architectural CAD

In this course, students explore CAD, focusing on using the computer as a drafting and design tool. Students learn Basic CAD drafting, 3 dimension viewing, detail drawings and creation of templates and symbols using AutoCad software for architectural/interior design applications.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[IND 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/1000/ind-1020), [IND 1401](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ind-interior-design/1000/ind-1401), all with grade of 'C' or higher
