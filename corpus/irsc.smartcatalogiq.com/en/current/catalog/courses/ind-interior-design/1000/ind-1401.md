# IND 1401 Technical Design I

In this course, students explore the basic technical aspects of interior design. Students focus on drafting and creation of drawing types, visualizations and other essential technical systems used in the interior design profession. Work is completed by hand, using a drafting table and a computer. Students develop appropriate uses of tools and materials.

2 credits
