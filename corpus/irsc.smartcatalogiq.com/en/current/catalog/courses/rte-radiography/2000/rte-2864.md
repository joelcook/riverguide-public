# RTE 2864 Radiographic Clinical Education VII

In this supervised clinical practice course conducted in clinical education sites in the four-county district, students achieve competencies in surgical procedures, venipuncture, and sterile procedure preparation. Students continue to practice radiological procedures in which they have achieved competency.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 2533](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533), [RTE 2533L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533l), [RTE 2854](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2854), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites

[RTE 2385](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2385), [RTE 2553](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2553), [RTE 2553L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2553l)
