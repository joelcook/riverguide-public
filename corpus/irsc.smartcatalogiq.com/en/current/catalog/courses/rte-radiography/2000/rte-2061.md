# RTE 2061 Radiographic Seminar

In this course, students prepare for the ARRT certification examination. Mock examinations are administered to practice test-taking skills and to evaluate strengths and weaknesses within specific content areas designated by the ARRT. Students also prepare for job-seeking opportunities by participating in a resume writing and interview skills workshop. Students create their own resumes and participate in a mock interview before the end of the semester.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 2385](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2385), [RTE 2553](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2553), [RTE 2553L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2553l), [RTE 2864](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2864), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)
