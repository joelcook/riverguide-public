# RTE 2385 Radiation Safety and Protection

In this course, students study the concepts of radiobiology and factors that modify the cellular response to ionizing radiation. Radiation effects in humans and animals are presented with particular attention to the Japanese atomic bomb survivors. Students learn radiation dose limits and radiation dose reduction strategies for patients, other personnel, and themselves.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 2533](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533), [RTE 2533L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533l), [RTE 2854](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2854), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)
