# RTE 2844 Radiographic Clinical Education V

In this supervised clinical practice course, students perform radiographic procedures in clinical education sites in the four-county district. During this course, students continue to achieve competencies in spine, bony thorax, skull, and facial bones radiographic procedures and surgical procedures under direct supervision and continue to practice radiological procedures in which competency has been achieved under indirect supervision.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1457](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1457), [RTE 1457L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1457l), [RTE 1523](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1523), [RTE 1523L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1523l), [RTE 1834](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1834), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)
