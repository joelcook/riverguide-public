# RTE 2533 Radiographic Procedures IV

In this course, students study the anatomy and positioning for fluoroscopic examinations including studies of the esophagus, upper GI, lower GI, biliary, and urinary systems. Students learn radiographic positioning, anatomical landmarks, and image evaluation. Students evaluate radiographic images and learn to make corrections to positioning and technique. Students also identify and define various pathologic conditions.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 2844](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2844) with a grade of 'C' or higher

### Corequisites
