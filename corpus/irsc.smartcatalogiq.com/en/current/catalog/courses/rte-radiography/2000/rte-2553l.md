# RTE 2553L Radiographic Procedures V Lab

In this course, students apply the principles from [RTE 2553](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2553) by demonstrating knowledge and skill in performing sterile tray set-up, vital signs, venipuncture, and trauma positioning. Students practice these skills on each other in a controlled lab setting. Trauma scenarios are provided and students correctly position and image phantoms to demonstrate critical thinking and problem-solving skills. Students continue to demonstrate proper identification of anatomical landmarks, radiation safety, proper patient care, and communication skills.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[RTE 2533](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533), [RTE 2533L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533l), [RTE 2854](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2854), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites

[RTE 2385](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2385), [RTE 2553](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2553), [RTE 2864](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2864)
