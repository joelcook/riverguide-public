# RTE 2553 Radiographic Procedures V

In this course, students study trauma and mobile imaging as well as advanced modalities such as mammography, bone densitometry, interventional radiology, and CT scanning. Students explore the uses of pharmacology in diagnostic imaging. Students examine critical thinking scenarios based on trauma examinations and adapt imaging plan to various patient conditions.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 2533](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533), [RTE 2533L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533l), [RTE 2854](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2854), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)
