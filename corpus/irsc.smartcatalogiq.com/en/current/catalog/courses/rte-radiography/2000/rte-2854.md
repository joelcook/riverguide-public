# RTE 2854 Radiographic Clinical Education VI

In this supervised clinical practice course, students perform radiographic procedures in clinical education sites in the four-county district. During this course, students achieve competencies in the digestive, urinary, and biliary systems under direct supervision. Students continue to perform radiographic examinations on the spine, bony thorax, skull, and facial bones under indirect supervision. Students perform surgical procedures under direct supervision.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[RTE 2844](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2844) with a grae of C or higher

### Corequisites

[RTE 2533](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533), [RTE 2533L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2533l)
