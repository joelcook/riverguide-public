# RTE 2874 Radiographic Clinical Education VIII

In this supervised clinical practice course conducted in clinical education sites in the four-county district, students continue to practice radiological procedures in which they have achieved competency. Students focus on completion of the competencies required for the radiography program and national certification.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 2385](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2385), [RTE 2553](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2553), [RTE 2553L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2553l), [RTE 2864](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2864), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites

[RTE 2061](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/2000/rte-2061)
