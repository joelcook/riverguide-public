# RTE 1814 Radiographic Clinical Education II

In this supervised clinical practice course, students perform radiographic procedures in clinical education sites in the four-county district. During this course, students continue to achieve competency in chest and abdominal radiographic procedures.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1000), [RTE 1503](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1503), [RTE 1804](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1804), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites

[RTE 1111](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1111), [RTE 1111L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1111l)
