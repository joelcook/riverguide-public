# RTE 1834 Radiographic Clinical Education IV

In this supervised clinical practice course, students perform radiographic procedures in clinical education sites in the four-county district. During this course, students achieve competencies in spine, bony thorax, skull, and facial bones radiographic procedures under direct supervision and continue to practice radiological procedures in which competency has been achieved under indirect supervision.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1824](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1824), [RTE 1513](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513), [RTE 1513L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513l), [RTE 1418](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1418), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites
