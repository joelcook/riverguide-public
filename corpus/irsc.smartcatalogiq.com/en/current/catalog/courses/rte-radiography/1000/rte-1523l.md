# RTE 1523L Radiographic Procedures III Lab

In this course, students apply the principles from [RTE 1523](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1523) by demonstrating proper identification of anatomical landmarks and radiographic positioning of the spine, bony thorax, skull, and facial bones. These skills are employed in a simulated lab setting while maintaining radiation safety practices and proper patient care. Students have the opportunity to practice communication skills by using identification factors, taking history, and instructing others on procedures. For each unit students are assessed on these skills in a controlled lab setting.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1418](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1418), [RTE 1513](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513), [RTE 1513L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513l), [RTE 1824](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1824), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites
