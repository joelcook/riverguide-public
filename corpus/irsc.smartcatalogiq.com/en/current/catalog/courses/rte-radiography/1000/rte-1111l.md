# RTE 1111L Patient Care Lab for Imaging Sciences

In this course, students apply the concepts and principles learned in [RTE 1111](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1111) by simulating routine and emergency patient care procedures. Students practice and are evaluated on topics such as patient identification, communicating effectively, patient safety, utilizing standard precautions, demonstrating infection control procedures, and executing patient transfers.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1000), [RTE 1503](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1503), [RTE 1804](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1804), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites

[RTE 1111](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1111), [RTE 1814](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1814)
