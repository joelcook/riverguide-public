# RTE 1523 Radiographic Procedures III

In this course, students study the anatomy and positioning of the bony thorax, spine, skull, and facial bones. There is attention to radiographic positioning, anatomical landmarks, and image evaluation. Students evaluate radiographic images and learn to make corrections to positioning and technique.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1418](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1418), [RTE 1513](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513), [RTE 1513L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513l), [RTE 1824](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1824), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)
