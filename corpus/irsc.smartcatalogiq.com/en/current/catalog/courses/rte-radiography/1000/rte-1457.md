# RTE 1457 Radiographic Imaging II

In this course, students analyze the principles of radiologic imaging as they apply to receptor exposure, spatial resolution, contrast, magnification, and distortion. Students study various types of digital image receptors, digital image processing, and digital image management with an emphasis on the tradeoffs between image quality and patient radiation dose reduction.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1418](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1418) with a grade of 'C' or higher
