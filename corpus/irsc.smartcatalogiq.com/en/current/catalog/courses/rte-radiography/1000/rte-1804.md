# RTE 1804 Radiographic Clinical Education I

In this supervised clinical practice course, students perform radiographic procedures in clinical education sites in the four-county district. During this course students orient to the imaging department location and prepare to achieve competencies in chest and abdominal radiographic procedures.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Radiography Program

### Corequisites

[RTE 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1000), [RTE 1503](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1503)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $50.
