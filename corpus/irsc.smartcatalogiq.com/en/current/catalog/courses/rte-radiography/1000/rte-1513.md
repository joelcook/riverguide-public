# RTE 1513 Radiographic Procedures II

In this course, students study the anatomy and positioning of the upper extremity, shoulder girdle, lower extremity, and pelvic girdle. Students evaluate radiographic images and learn to make corrections to position and technique. In addition, students learn about pathologic conditions and their radiographic appearance.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1111](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1111), [RTE 1111L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1111l), [RTE 1814](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1814), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites
