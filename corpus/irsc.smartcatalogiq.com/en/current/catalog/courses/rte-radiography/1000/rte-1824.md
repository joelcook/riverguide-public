# RTE 1824 Radiographic Clinical Education III

In this supervised clinical practice course, students perform radiographic procedures in clinical education sites in the four-county district. During this course, students continue to achieve competency in chest and abdominal radiographic procedures. Additionally, students achieve competencies in the upper and lower extremities, the shoulder girdle, and the pelvic girdle.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1111](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1111), [RTE 1111L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1111l), [RTE 1814](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1814), (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)

### Corequisites

[RTE 1418](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1418), [RTE 1513](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513), [RTE 1513L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513l)
