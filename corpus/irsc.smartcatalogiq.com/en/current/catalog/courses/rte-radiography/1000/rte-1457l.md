# RTE 1457L Radiographic Imaging II Lab

In this course, students apply the concepts and principles learned in RTE1457 by observing and measuring the effects of the x-ray beam on image quality and exposure parameters. Radiographers must analyze image quality and take corrective action when necessary; so, the students practice these skills in the energized x-ray lab. Students adhere to and practice radiation safety while in the laboratory setting. For each lab project the students prepare a written analysis and summary for assessment.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[RTE 1418](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1418), [RTE 1513](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513), [RTE 1513L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1513l), [RTE 1824](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1824) (all with a grade of 'C' or higher)
