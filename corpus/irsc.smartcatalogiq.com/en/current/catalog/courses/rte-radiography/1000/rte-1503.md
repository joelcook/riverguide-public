# RTE 1503 Radiographic Procedures I

In this course, students study radiographic positioning terminology and principles that form the foundation for subsequent learning. Students learn radiologic procedures of the chest and abdomen as well as common pathologies that may be imaged.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Radiography Program

### Corequisites

[RTE 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1000), [RTE 1804](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/rte-radiography/1000/rte-1804)
