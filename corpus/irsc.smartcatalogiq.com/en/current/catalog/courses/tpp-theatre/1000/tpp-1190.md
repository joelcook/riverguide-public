# TPP 1190 Rehearsal and Performance Lab

In this course, students apply their acting and performance skills to rehearsing for a major theatrical production and reflect upon the process in writing.  Students demonstrate their skills and technique in a culminating public performance. Students must audition for this course.

1 credit
