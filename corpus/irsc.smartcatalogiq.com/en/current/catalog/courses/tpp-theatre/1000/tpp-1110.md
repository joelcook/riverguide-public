# TPP 1110 Acting

In this course, students explore and reflect in writing upon fundamental acting principles and techniques. Students demonstrate these principles and techniques through performing short scenes and monologues in front of their peers.  Students also create theatrical resumes/portfolios in order to prepare for professional or college transfer auditions.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Student must score into college-level English and reading on placement test.
