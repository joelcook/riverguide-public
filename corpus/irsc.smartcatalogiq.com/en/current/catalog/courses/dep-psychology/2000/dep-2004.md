# DEP 2004 Human Development

This course is a basic introduction to human development from conception to death. Physical, cognitive, social, and emotional changes are examined during infancy, childhood, adolescence, and adulthood. The stages of development are also examined from the different perspectives of the current major theories in human development.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Student must score into college-level English and reading on placement test.
