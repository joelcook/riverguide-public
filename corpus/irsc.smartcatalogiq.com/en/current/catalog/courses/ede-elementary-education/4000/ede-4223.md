# EDE 4223 Integrated Music, Art and Movement

In this course students develop a critical understanding of art, music, and movement. Emphasis is placed on the effective integrative lesson planning and modeling techniques to art, music, and movement in elementary schools.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214) with grade of C or higher
