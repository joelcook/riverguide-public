# ISC 3940 Community-Based Medical Shadowing

In this course, students participate in democratic service through planned service-learning activities, engaging in supervised career exploration within community health sciences settings. Through seminars and reflection exercises, students assess their experiences and examine how community organizations address local problems, issues, and concerns. Instructor approval is required.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[CHM 2211](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2211), [CHM 2211L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2211l), [PCB 3063](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063) and [PCB 3063L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pcb-biology/3000/pcb-3063l), all with a grade of 'C' or higher
