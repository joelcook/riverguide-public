# ISC 3426 Health Professional Exam Preparation

In this course, students are introduced to concept organization and its application to the sciences including biology, anatomy, chemistry, biochemistry and physics. Students will focus on preparation for standardized exams such as the MCAT, PCAT, and DAT.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), [CHM 2211](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2211), [CHM 2211L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2211l), and either [PHY 1053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1053) and [PHY 1053L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1053l), or PHY2048 and [PHY 2048L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2048l), all with a grade of 'C' or higher

### Corequisites

[BCH 4053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053), [BCH 4053L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bch-biology/4000/bch-4053l), and either [PHY 1054](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1054) and [PHY 1054L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1054l), or [PHY 2049](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2049) and [PHY 2049L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2049l)
