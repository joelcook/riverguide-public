# ISC 4911L Senior Capstone II

In this course, students continue to engage in research projects, write review or analysis papers, or participate in service-learning opportunities under the guidance of an instructor or researcher. The course culminates in students disseminating their work through written or oral presentations. Permission is required to register.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[ISC 4910L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/isc-interdisciplinary-sciences/4000/isc-4910l) with a grade of "C" or higher

### Course Fee

Lab fee $100.
