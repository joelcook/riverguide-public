# ETS 2520 Process Measurement Fundamentals

In this course, students learn typical operations performed in industrial measurement and control loops. Topics students learn include basic physics involved in the measurements and common types of sensors used in industry, emphasizing pressure, temperature, flow, level, and analytical measurement theory. Students learn how to select proper testing devices and perform pressure, temperature, flow, and level measurements. This course is designed for students who will be supporting industrial equipment processes.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EET 1025C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/1000/eet-1025c) with grade of 'C' or higher
