# ETS 2542 Programmable Logic Controllers I

In this course, students learn the basic operational concepts common to PLCs, focusing on PLC principles, programming, numbering systems, data manipulation, math, and sequencer instructions. This programmable logic controllers (PLC) course is designed for students preparing for careers in electronics, manufacturing, electrical or industrial technology.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/Corequisite: [EET 2141C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/2000/eet-2141c) with grade of 'C' or higher
