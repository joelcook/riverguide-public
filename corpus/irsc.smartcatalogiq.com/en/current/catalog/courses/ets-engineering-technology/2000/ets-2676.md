# ETS 2676 Introduction to Robotics

In this course, students learn the basic principles of industrial electronics, computers, automated equipment, sensors, servo systems, stepper motors, relays, and controllers. Students gain practical experience by programming multiple robotic inventory handling systems.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ETS 2542](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ets-engineering-technology/2000/ets-2542) with grade of 'C' or higher

Lab fees $20, Fall 2026 only
