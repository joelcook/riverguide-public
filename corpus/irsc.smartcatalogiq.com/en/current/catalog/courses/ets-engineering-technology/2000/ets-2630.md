# ETS 2630 Manufacturing Processes

In this course, students learn the fundamental principles of the use of computers to integrate and automate a broad range of engineering and manufacturing functions. Topics include manufacturing and process planning, just-in-time production, debugging automation cells, and programming a manufacturing robotic arm.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ETS 2676](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ets-engineering-technology/2000/ets-2676) with grade of 'C' or higher
