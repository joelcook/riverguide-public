# ETS 2530 Process Control Technology

In this course, students learn to analyze systems and devices and perform calculations to determine parameters to accurately predict operation. Students examine the concepts and principles of open and closed loop systems, transducers, transformers, transmission, and distribution systems. This course is designed for students studying systems and associated electronic circuits encountered in the field of electric machinery and industrial controls.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EET 1025C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/1000/eet-1025c) with grade of 'C' or higher
