# ETS 2544 Programmable Logic Controllers II

In this course, students learn the basic operational concepts common to programmable logic controllers (PLCs), focusing on PLC principles, programming, numbering systems, data manipulation, math, and sequencer instructions. This second PLC course is designed for students preparing for careers in electronics, manufacturing, electrical or industrial technology.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/Corequisite: [ETS 2542](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ets-engineering-technology/2000/ets-2542) with grade of 'C' or higher
