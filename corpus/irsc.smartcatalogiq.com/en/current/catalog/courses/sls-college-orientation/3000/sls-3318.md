# SLS 3318 Orientation to Education Programs

In this course, students explore the school of education department processes and overall expectations for disposition for teaching. Pre-service teachers learn how coursework relates to meeting the Florida Department of Education requirements for teacher certification and how to ensure they remain a satisfactory candidate for graduation.

0 credits
