# MAN 3303 Management Concepts

In this course, students explore the foundational principles and practices of management. Students explore key concepts such as planning, organizing, leading, and controlling within various organizational contexts. Topics include decision-making, strategic management, organizational structure, leadership styles, motivation, communication, and team dynamics. Both theory and practical applications are emphasized, encouraging students to understand how managers solve problems, make decisions, and drive organizational success.

3 credits
