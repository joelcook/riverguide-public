# MAN 4504 Operations Management

In this course, students explore operational decision-making management techniques to improve the processes and productivity in organizations. Topics explored and discussed are quality and outcomes, efficiency, forecasting, work-flow processes, inventory control, design of goods and services, waiting lines, and critical path. Students address how to manage a project from beginning to end - including how to identify needs, and define, assign, and track items.

3 credits
