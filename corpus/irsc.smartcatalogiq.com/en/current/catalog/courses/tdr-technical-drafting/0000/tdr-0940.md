# TDR 0940 Mechanical Drafter On-the-Job-Training (N)

In this On-the-Job Training (OJT) course, students apply the skills and concepts learned in the Mechanical Drafter Apprenticeship program. Students practice workplace competencies in a real-world job setting and maintain state-required documentation throughout the course.
