# HSC 1001 Introduction to Healthcare Management

This course provides a basic overview of the healthcare system, roles of the health team members, and the basic skills necessary for success in a health career. This course includes application of the five competencies in healthcare management; leadership, professionalism, communication; knowledge of healthcare environment; and business skills.

4 credits
