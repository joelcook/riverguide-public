# HSC 0930 Health Science Special Topics (N)

In this course, students learninformation and/or skills related to specific areas of individual or public/community health including the management of health care resources, facilities, or programs.  This course provides the necessary knowledge and skills for careers in health- related areas, or to enhance individual knowledge about matters relating to health care.
