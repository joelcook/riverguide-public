# RMI 2500 Individual Life and Health Insurance

In this course, the student explores the role of life and health insurance in meeting economic security needs, types of individual and special life annuity contracts, individual health insurance contracts including disability and medical expense insurance as related to premiums, reserves, non-forfeiture values, surplus, and dividends.

3 credits
