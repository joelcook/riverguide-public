# PGY 1801 Introduction to Digital Photography

In this course, students explore how to create and edit digital images. Techniques that enhance or alter existing images are covered. The use and maintenance of new equipment and technologies are also studied, along with special effects. Additionally, students cover how to import, export, print and publish photographs on the Web.

1 credit
