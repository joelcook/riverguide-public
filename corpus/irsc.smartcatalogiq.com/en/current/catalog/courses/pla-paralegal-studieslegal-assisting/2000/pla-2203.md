# PLA 2203 Civil Litigation I

This course explores the jurisdiction of courts, the process of civil litigation, and alternatives to litigation. Students identify the proper court in which to file pleadings, draft pleadings and motions, and examine the Florida and federal rules of civil procedure. Students are also introduced to the law of evidence.

3 credits
