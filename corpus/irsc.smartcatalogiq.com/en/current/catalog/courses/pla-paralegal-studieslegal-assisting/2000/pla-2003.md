# PLA 2003 Introduction to Paralegal Studies

This course provides an overview of the training and purpose of paralegals and examines the roles of lawyers and paralegals.  The course explores the ethical and professional practice standards applicable to attorneys, paralegals, and other legal professionals.

3 credits
