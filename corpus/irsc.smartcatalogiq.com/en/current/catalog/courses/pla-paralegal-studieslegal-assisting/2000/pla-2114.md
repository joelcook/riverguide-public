# PLA 2114 Legal Research and Writing II

This course reinforces legal research and writing skills obtained in PLA1104, Legal Research and Writing I. Students write advanced case briefs, conduct research, apply legal materials to factual scenarios, and draft legal memoranda.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[PLA 1104](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pla-paralegal-studieslegal-assisting/1000/pla-1104)
