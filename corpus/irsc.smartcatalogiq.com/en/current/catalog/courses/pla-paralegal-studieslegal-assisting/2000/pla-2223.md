# PLA 2223 Civil Litigation II

This course continues to examine the process of civil litigation, as presented in Civil Litigation I. The methods and scope of discovery are outlined, the pretrial process is explained, trials are discussed, and post-trial options are highlighted.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[PLA 2203](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pla-paralegal-studieslegal-assisting/2000/pla-2203)
