# PLA 1763 Law Office Management

This course examines the fundamentals of law office management, including the application of legal ethics to the functions of managing trust accounts, billing, and attorneys’ fees. Other topics include the maintenance of law libraries, technology, and human resources.

3 credits

Lab fee $16.
