# PRN 0374C Practical Nursing Medical Surgical II (N)

In this course, students assimilate theoretical and clinical applications of medical/surgical nursing care utilizing nursing principles throughout the lifespan. Students apply concepts related to basic care and comfort, psychosocial integrity, pharmacological interventions, client safety physiological adaption, health promotion and safety. Students focus on organized client care functions.

350 hours

### Prerequisites

[PRN 0373C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/prn-nursing-practical-nursing/0000/prn-0373c) with grade of "C" or higher
