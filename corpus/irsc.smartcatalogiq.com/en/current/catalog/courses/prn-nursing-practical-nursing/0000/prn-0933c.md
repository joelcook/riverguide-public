# PRN 0933C Transition to Graduate Practical Nurse (N)

In this course, students assimilate the integration of knowledge, attitudes, and skills necessary to implement safe care for clients with multi-contextual problems. Students apply concepts related to basic care and comfort, psychosocial integrity, pharmacological interventions, client safety, physiological adaptation, and health promotion. Students focus on employability skills, professional practice issues, and the role of the LPN in the health care team.

150 hours

### Prerequisites

[PRN 0374C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/prn-nursing-practical-nursing/0000/prn-0374c) with a grade of "C" or higher
