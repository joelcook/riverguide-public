# DES 2051C Pain Control and Anesthesia

In this course, students study the concepts of pain perception, pain reaction, methods of pain control, and the administration of local anesthetic. Students learn the relevance of psychosomatic pain control and topical and local anesthesia to dental hygiene practice.

2 credits

Lab fee $50.
