# CGS 1100 Introduction to Computer Applications for Business

In this course students are introduced to computer applications for business use. Students explore topics such as computer concepts, the Windows® operating system, word processing, electronic spreadsheets, electronic databases, presentation graphics, and the Internet.

3 credits
