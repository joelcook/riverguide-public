# CGS 1700 Introduction to Operating Systems

In this course the students are introduced to hardware, software, and operating system concepts used with computer systems. Students gain exposure to popular operating systems such as Windows, and Linux. Specifically, the student is presented with topics such as how to copy, format, delete, and use subdirectories to customize their computer system. Students focus on utility commands, kernel software, shell programs, and connectivity.

3 credits
