# CAP 4793 Advanced Data Analytics

In this course the students address advanced data analytic methodologies and techniques, such as neural networks, unsupervised learning, and emerging modes of data acquisition and aggregation. Students also develop the skillset to manage complex data analytics applications.

3 credits
