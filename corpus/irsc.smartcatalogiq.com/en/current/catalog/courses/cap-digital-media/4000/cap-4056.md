# CAP 4056 Game Design II

In this course, students explore advanced principles and theories of video game design, including software, content, interaction, and game play design. Students examine the important techniques used by game programmers and designers during a typical production cycle. Student assignments may include implementation of reusable game technology, algorithm analysis, usage of game industry tools and platforms, and game projects.

3 credits
