# HIM 2211 Health Information Systems

In this course, students study information technology related to healthcare and the automated tools and techniques for collecting, storing, and retrieving data. Topics include: system analysis, design, and security, file structure, networking, telecommunications, document imaging, medical informatics, electronic health record, and implementation issues. Upon completion, students assist in the design, implementation, evaluation, and maintenance of automated information systems in healthcare.

3 credits
