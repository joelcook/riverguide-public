# HIM 1012 Legal Aspects of Health Information

In thiscourse, students study principles of legal issues affecting the preparation and use of health information documents and electronic health records. Both the computer-based record and the paper record are examined with regard to local, state, and federal laws. Legal guidelines for release of information by health care providers are explored

3 credits
