# EEX 2010 Introduction to Special Education

This course introduces the major areas of exceptional students' education. Emphasis is on etiology, terminology, classification, prevalence, educational approaches, legal and medical implications, and personal and social growth adjustments of students labeled exceptional.

3 credits
