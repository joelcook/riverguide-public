# DEH 1800 Clinical Dental Hygiene I

In this course, students continue to study clinical dental hygiene skills. Students focus on beginning patient care as it applies to clinical policy, safety practices, assessment procedures, practice management, radiographic interpretation, instrumentation, stain removal and fluoride. Students learn instrument sharpening; root planing and biofilm control procedures are included.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[DES 1800](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/des-dental-science/1000/des-1800), [DES 1800L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/des-dental-science/1000/des-1800l), [DEH 1003](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1003), [DEH 1003L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1003l)
