# DEH 1003 Pre-Clinical Dental Hygiene

In this course, students explorethe dental hygiene profession, professional conduct, and the theory and practice necessary for instrumentation. Students focus on the use of dental hygiene instruments and performance of an extra and intraoral examination. Students learn beginning instrumentation skills.

1 credit
