# DEH 1300 Pharmacology

In this course, students study the usage, administration, indications, contraindications, adverse reactions, and precautions of pharmaceutical preparations. Students identify the therapeutic agents used in the treatment of dental disease.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2085](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085), [BSC 2085L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2085l)

### Corequisites

BSC 2094 , BSC 2094L or [BSC 2086](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086), [BSC 2086L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086l)
