# DEH 1800L Clinical Dental Hygiene I Lab

In this course, students begin patient care on relatively non-complex patients in a closely monitored environment. Students apply clinical policies, safety practices, assessment procedures, practice management, radiographic techniques, instrumentation, stain removal and fluoride. Students practice instrument sharpening, root planing and biofilm control procedures.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[DES 1800](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/des-dental-science/1000/des-1800), [DEH 1003](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1003), [DEH 1003L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1003l)
