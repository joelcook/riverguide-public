# DEH 1003L Pre-Clinical Dental Hygiene Lab

In this course, students learn the theory and practical skills necessary for instrumentation. Clinical hours are included to demonstrate competence in the use of dental hygiene instruments and performing an extra and intraoral examination. Students learn beginning instrumentation skills in a clinic setting.

2 credits

### Corequisites

[DEH 1003](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1003)
