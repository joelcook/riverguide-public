# DEH 2806 Clinical Dental Hygiene IV

In this course, students focus on the concepts of ethics, professional codes, the law and risk management as applied to the dental hygiene practice setting. Students prepare to seek employment as dental hygienists. Students discuss current and future directions of the career field. Students learn advanced techniques and new technologies in patient care. Students prepare for state licensure requirements and examination.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[DEH 2804](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/2000/deh-2804), [DEH 2804L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/2000/deh-2804l)
