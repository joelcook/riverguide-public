# DEH 2400 General and Oral Pathology

In this course, students learn the principles of general pathology with consideration of the more common diseases affecting the human body. Students study pathological conditions of the teeth, soft tissues and supporting structures of the oral cavity.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2086](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086), [BSC 2086L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2086l), along with [MCB 2010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/2000/mcb-2010), [MCB 2010L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mcb-biology/2000/mcb-2010l), [DEH 1130](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1130)
