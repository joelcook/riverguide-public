# DEH 2804 Clinical Dental Hygiene III

In this course, students demonstrate independence and use evidence-based knowledge learned in previous clinical instruction for planning patient services, as well as professional and ethical judgement in a classroom setting. Students exhibit competency in all areas of dental hygiene. Students present case documentation, dental hygiene treatment planning, nutritional counseling, and intraoral photography in a classroom setting.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[DEH 1802](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1802), [DEH 1802L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1802l), DES 1051
