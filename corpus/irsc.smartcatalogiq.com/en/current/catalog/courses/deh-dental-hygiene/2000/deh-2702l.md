# DEH 2702L Community Dental Health Lab

In this course, students learn the application of basic statistical concepts, program development, implementation, research, and evaluation. Students apply the principles of public and community dental health, as they relate to the role of the dental hygienist, through service-learning projects’ implementation and evaluation.  Students participate in 15 hours of academic service learning.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[SPC 1608](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/spc-speech/1000/spc-1608), [DEH 2702](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/2000/deh-2702)
