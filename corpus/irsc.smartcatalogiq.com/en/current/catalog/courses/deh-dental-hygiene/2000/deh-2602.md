# DEH 2602 Periodontology

In this course, students learn the characteristics, etiology, prognosis and treatment of periodontal disease.  Students study periodontal examination, treatment planning and the dental hygienist’s role in periodontal therapy.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[DEH 1130](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/deh-dental-hygiene/1000/deh-1130)
