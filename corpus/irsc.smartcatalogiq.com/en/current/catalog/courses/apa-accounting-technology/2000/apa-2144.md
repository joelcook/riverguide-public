# APA 2144 Intuit QuickBooks Online

In this course, students use QuickBooks Online accounting software to manage income and expenses to track and report on the financial health of a business. Students operate commonly used features of QBO including accounts payable and receivable, payment processing, and how to generate and customize reports.

3 credits
