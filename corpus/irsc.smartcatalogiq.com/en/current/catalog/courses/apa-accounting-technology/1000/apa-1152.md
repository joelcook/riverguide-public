# APA 1152 Orientation to QuickBooks Online

In this course, students navigate the primary modules of QuickBooks Online. Topics include recording basic transactions related to accounts receivable, accounts payable, invoicing, payments, and payroll. Additional topics include generating customized reports and graphs to analyze financial data.

1 credit
