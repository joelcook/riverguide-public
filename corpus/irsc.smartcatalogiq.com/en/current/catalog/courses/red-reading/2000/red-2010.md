# RED 2010 Introduction to Reading Language Arts

In this course students explore how to teach conceptually and developmentally appropriate literacy content for all students at the PK-6 grade level. Major topics include components of reading, writing, grammar, and mechanics.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/corequisites: [EDF 2005](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/2000/edf-2005), [ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101), [ENC 1102](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1102), with grade of C or higher
