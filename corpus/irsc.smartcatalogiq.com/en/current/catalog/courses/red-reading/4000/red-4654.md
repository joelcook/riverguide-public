# RED 4654 Differentiated Instruction Foundations and Applications

In this course students explore issues related to differentiated reading instruction. Knowledge and skills concerning differentiated instructional theory, classroom applications, and evaluation techniques used in differentiated instruction are discussed. This course is aligned with Competency 4 of the Florida Reading Endorsement: Differentiation.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[RED 3342](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/3000/red-3342), [RED 4519](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/red-reading/4000/red-4519), Passing score on GK Reading. All courses require a grade of C or higher.
