# RED 3360 Teaching Reading in Middle/Secondary Schools

This course promotes the effective teaching of literacy skills across the curriculum and includes Reading Competencies 1 and 2 with a focus on secondary classrooms. The major emphasis of this course is placed on current theories, research-based strategies and materials used in content area literacy instruction.

3 credits
