# EEC 1946 Practicum in CDA Assessment

In this course students explore supervised participation in a Child Development Center. Students are introduced to trends and issues in Early Childhood Education in preparation for the Florida Child Care Professional Credential (FCCPC) Assessment. An approved field experience is required.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Pre/Co-requisite: Any one of the following with grade of 'C' or higher: [CHD 1220](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chd-child-development-and-education/1000/chd-1220), [EEC 1202](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eec-child-development-and-education/1000/eec-1202), or [CHD 1332](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chd-child-development-and-education/1000/chd-1332)
