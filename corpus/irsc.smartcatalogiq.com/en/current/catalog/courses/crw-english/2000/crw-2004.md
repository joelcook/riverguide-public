# CRW 2004 Creative Writing IV

This course builds on skills developed in prerequisite course, [CRW 2003](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/crw-english/2000/crw-2003). Students practice creative forms of writing, such as poetry, short fiction, and non-fiction. A significant writing project/presentation is also required.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[CRW 2003](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/crw-english/2000/crw-2003) with grade of C or higher, or permission of instructor.
