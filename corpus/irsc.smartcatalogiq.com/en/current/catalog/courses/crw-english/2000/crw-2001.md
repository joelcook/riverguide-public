# CRW 2001 Creative Writing I

This is an introductory creative writing course. Students analyze major characteristics of various genres, such as poetry, short fiction, and non-fiction, and will also practice basic techniques of writing in major genres.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101) with a grade of "C" or higher, or permission of instructor.
