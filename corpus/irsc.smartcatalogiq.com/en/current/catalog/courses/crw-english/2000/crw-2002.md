# CRW 2002 Creative Writing II

This course builds on skills developed in the prerequisite course, [CRW 2001](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/crw-english/2000/crw-2001). Students practice creative forms of writing, such as poetry, short fiction, and non-fiction. Coursework will focus on improvement in language and style through the use of appropriate literary devices.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[CRW 2001](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/crw-english/2000/crw-2001) with grade of C or higher, or permission of instructor.
