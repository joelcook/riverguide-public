# FSS 2204C Quantity Food Production II

Students learn techniques and methods for vegetable, starch, meat, fish and poultry cookery, including the basic cooking techniques of sautéing, roasting, poaching, braising, sauces, center of the plate concept and deep-frying.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[FSS 1203C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/fss-restaurant-management/1000/fss-1203c) with grade of C or higher
