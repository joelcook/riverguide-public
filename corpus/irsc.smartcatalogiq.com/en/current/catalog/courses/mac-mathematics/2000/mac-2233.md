# MAC 2233 Business Calculus I

This course is primarily for business students. In this course, students explore limits, differentiation and integration of algebraic functions, exponential and logarithmic functions, areas, and applications of the preceding topics to problems in business.

Gordon Rule course - must achieve a grade of "C" or higher for the A.A. and A.S. Degree.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MAC 1105](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1105) with a grade of "C" or higher, or placement scores.
