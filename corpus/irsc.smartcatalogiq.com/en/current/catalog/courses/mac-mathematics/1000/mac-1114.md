# MAC 1114 Plane Trigonometry

In this course, students develop trigonometric skills required in the study of calculus: properties of trigonometric functions and inverse trigonometric functions, trigonometric identities, conditional trigonometric equations, graphing techniques, solutions of triangles, vector algebra, parametric equations, polar coordinates, and applications.

This course requires a graphing calculator.

Gordon Rule course - must achieve a grade of "C" or higher for the A.A. and A.S. Degree.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MAC 1105](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1105) with a grade of "C" or higher, or placement scores.
