# MAC 1140 Precalculus Algebra

In this course, students develop algebraic skills required for the study of calculus: properties of polynomial, rational, exponential and logarithmic functions, polynomial and rational inequalities, conic sections, matrices and determinants, sequences and series, mathematical induction, and the Binomial theorem with applications.

This course requires a graphing calculator.

Gordon Rule course - must achieve a grade of "C" or higher for the A.A. and A.S. Degree.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MAC 1105](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1105) with a grade of "C" or higher, or placement scores.
