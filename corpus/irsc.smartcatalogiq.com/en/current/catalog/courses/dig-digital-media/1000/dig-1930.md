# DIG 1930 Special Topics in Digital Media

In this course, students explore selected topics in digital media and emerging technologies. Students examine technologies shaping current electronic media, such as introduction to digital video animation; pre-production, production, and post production processes including digital imaging, virtual reality, video, animation, simulation, and gaming.

.5 - 3 credits
