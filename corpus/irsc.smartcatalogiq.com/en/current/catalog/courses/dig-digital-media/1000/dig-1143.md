# DIG 1143 Video Making for Social Media

In this course, students design and develop videos for online platforms. Students in this course explore topics such as streaming media, file formats, digital media creation, and publishing media to the internet. While gaining hands-on experience, students develop techniques and strategies for creating quality-rich content for social media and related applications.This course is for students who desire to design and develop videos for online platforms.
