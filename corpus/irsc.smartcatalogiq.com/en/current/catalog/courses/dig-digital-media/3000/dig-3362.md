# DIG 3362 3D Character Animation

In this course, students delve into the processes and techniques associated with the design of 3D artificial characters, and the manipulation of those characters to convey a story in a visual medium. Study topics include theory of artificial character acting, techniques to make artificial movement believable, and techniques to bring character to life.

3 credits
