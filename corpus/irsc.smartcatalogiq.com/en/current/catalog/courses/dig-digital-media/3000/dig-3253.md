# DIG 3253 Digital Audio Production

In this course, students explore advanced concepts in mixing and editing digital audio content using a variety of technologies within a virtual studio environment. Practical assignments are tailored toward developing audio editing and assembly skills. Students experience the stages of pre-production, production, and post-production in the process of audio creation.

3 credits
