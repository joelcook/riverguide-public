# DIG 3375 Modeling Vehicles and Structures

In this course, students explore advanced tools, concepts and techniques for the development of 3D vehicles and structures. Topics include hard surface modeling, procedural modeling, polygonal topology, UV layout, and texture painting.

3 credits
