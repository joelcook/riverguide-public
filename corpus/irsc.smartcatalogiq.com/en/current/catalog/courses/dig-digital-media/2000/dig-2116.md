# DIG 2116 Digital Imaging 2

In this course, students explore advanced techniques using digital image manipulation software. In addition, tools to create, edit, and enhance digital images for print and digital content are covered.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[DIG 1115](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/dig-digital-media/1000/dig-1115) with grade of 'C' or higher

### Course Fee

Lab fee $30.
