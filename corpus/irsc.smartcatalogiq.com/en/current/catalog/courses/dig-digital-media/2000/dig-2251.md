# DIG 2251 Digital Audio Fundamentals

In this course, students explore how to create and edit audio with current Adobe, Apple, and other standard software. Students focus on recording and editing, removing unwanted noise, customizing sound effects, adding aural emphasis to a scene, and creating royalty-free music to match the mood of a production. Students learn techniques for recording new dialogue tracks and sound effects while learning how to modify existing audio files.

3 credits
