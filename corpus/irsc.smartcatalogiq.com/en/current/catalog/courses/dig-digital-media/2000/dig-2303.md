# DIG 2303 3D Digital Animation 2

In this course, students explore fundamentals in complex 3D modeling, 3D painting, texture map creation, and UV layout. This course is an intermediate introduction to creating digital 3D assets for film and games.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[DIG 2302](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/dig-digital-media/2000/dig-2302) with grade of 'C' or higher

### Course Fee

Lab fee $30.
