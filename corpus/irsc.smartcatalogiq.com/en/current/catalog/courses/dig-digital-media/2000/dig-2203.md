# DIG 2203 Digital Video 2 Production

In this course, students delve into intermediate to advanced techniques for digital video creation and production. Students blend traditional approaches with different stages of production and with non-linear DV technologies.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[DIG 2030](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/dig-digital-media/2000/dig-2030) or [DIG 1143](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/dig-digital-media/1000/dig-1143) with grade of 'C' or higher

### Course Fee

Lab fee $25.
