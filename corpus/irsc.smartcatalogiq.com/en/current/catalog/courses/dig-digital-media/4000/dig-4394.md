# DIG 4394 Motion Graphics: Advanced Compositing

In this course, Students examine digital post-production techniques used for film, animation, video, video games, and the web. Students learn fundamental concepts of video compositing, tracking, and chroma key to create content for use in digital media video projects.

3 credits
