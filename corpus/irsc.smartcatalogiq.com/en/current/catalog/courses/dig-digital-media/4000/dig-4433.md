# DIG 4433 Advanced Animation Techniques

In this course, students delve into advanced processes in animation for use in film and video games. Topics emphasized include character acting, facial animation, video referencing, rotoscoping, motion capture, and cinematography.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[DIG 3362](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/dig-digital-media/3000/dig-3362) with grade of C or higher
