# MNA 2216 Inventory Management

In this course, students analyze inventory control problems and methods. Topics include demand forecasting, independent demand inventory systems, inventory models and aggregate planning. Inventory management is presented within the context of sustainable, efficient, and effective supply chain management.

3 credits
