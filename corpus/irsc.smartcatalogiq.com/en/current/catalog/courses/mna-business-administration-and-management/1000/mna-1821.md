# MNA 1821 Electronic Commerce

In this course, students explore the concepts, tools, and strategies for understanding and exploiting opportunities associated with electronic commerce. Topics cover online ordering, interacting with firms and governments, and how businesses are organized and compete in the global marketplace. The students develop a strategic understanding of the electronic marketplace based on fundamental economics of the digital economy.

3 credits
