# STS 0853 Surgical Technology Apprenticeship: Seminar (N)

The course prepares surgical technology apprenticeship students to sit for the national examination. Course participants receive a comprehensive review based on the certification exam content identified by The National Board of Surgical Technology and Surgical Assisting. The student utilizes resources offered through a variety of practice activities as well as their apprenticeship resources.

100 hours
