# STS 0850 Surgical Technology Apprenticeship: Fundamentals (N)

This course provides instruction on the role of the surgical technologist and the surgical team. An introduction to the overall healthcare system provides a foundation for cognitive development through the program. The topics include professional behavior and growth, the surgical team, legal concepts, and asepsis/infection control.

180 hours
