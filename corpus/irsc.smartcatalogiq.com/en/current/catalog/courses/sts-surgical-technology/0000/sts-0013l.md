# STS 0013L Central Sterile Processing Clinical (N)

In this course, students focus on applying the principles and practice of central sterile processing and materials management through clinical observation and participation under professional supervision in the hospital environment.

410 hours

### Prerequisites

[HSC 0003](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/hsc-health-science/0000/hsc-0003), [STS 0019C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sts-surgical-technology/0000/sts-0019c), all with grade of "C" or higher

### Corequisites

pre/coreq: HSC 0003
