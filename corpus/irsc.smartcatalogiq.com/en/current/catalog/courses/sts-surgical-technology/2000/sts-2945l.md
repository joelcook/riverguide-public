# STS 2945L Surgical Specialties Clinical II

In this course, students explore the more complex perioperative case management of the surgical patient through clinical observation and participation under professional supervision in the hospital, ambulatory surgery center, and freestanding specialty surgery center settings.

5 credits

### Prerequisites

[STS 2324C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sts-surgical-technology/2000/sts-2324c), [STS 2944L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sts-surgical-technology/2000/sts-2944l) with grade of "C" or higher

### Corequisites

[STS 2930](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sts-surgical-technology/2000/sts-2930)
