# STS 2944L Surgical Specialties Clinical I

In this course, students participate in clinical training in perioperative case management of the surgical patient through observation and participation in surgical procedures under professional supervision in the hospital, ambulatory surgery center, and freestanding specialty surgery center settings.

5 credits

### Prerequisites

[STS 2323C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sts-surgical-technology/2000/sts-2323c), [STS 2365C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sts-surgical-technology/2000/sts-2365c), and [STS 2324C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sts-surgical-technology/2000/sts-2324c)
