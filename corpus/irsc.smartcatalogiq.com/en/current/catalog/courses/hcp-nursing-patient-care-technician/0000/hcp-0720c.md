# HCP 0720C Electrocardiograph Technical Skills (N)

In this course, students review the anatomy and physiology of the cardiovascular system and cardiac medications. Students learn the care and maintenance of equipment. Students perform basic electrocardiograph patient care techniques. Students learn to recognize artifacts and identify cardiac rhythms.

75 hours
