# HCP 0120 Nursing Assistant - Clinical (N)

In this course, students apply nursing assistant theory and laboratory skills in a clinical setting, aligned with the Florida Department of Education (FDOE) academic standards, to prepare for the nursing assistant state certification examination.

40 hours

### Prerequisites

HCP 0122 with a grade of C or higher

### Course Fee

Lab fee $30. Insurance $13.39.
