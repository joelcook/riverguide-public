# HCP 0750C Basic Concepts of Phlebotomy (N)

In this course, students explore techniques, procedures, and issues pertaining to the proper collection of blood specimens for routine clinical laboratory testing in order to become well trained, proficient, and professional phlebotomists.

75 hours
