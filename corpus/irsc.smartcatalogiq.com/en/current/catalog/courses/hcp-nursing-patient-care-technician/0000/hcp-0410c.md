# HCP 0410C Nursing Assistant (N)

In this course, students develop practical knowledge of the responsibilities and work practices of a Nursing Assistant (NA), aligned with the Florida Department of Education (FDOE) academic standards. Students also gain the technical knowledge and skills needed to demonstrate competence, successfully complete the Florida Nursing Assistant (NA) certification examination, and pursue employment as a Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA).

165 hours

Lab fee $30. Insurance fee $13.39.
