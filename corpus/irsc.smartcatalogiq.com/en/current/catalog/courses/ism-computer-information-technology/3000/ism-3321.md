# ISM 3321 Cyber Security Essentials

In this course, students evaluate the management of information security problems covered including attack methods; detection and prevention techniques; cryptography, firewalls and intrusion detection systems; security policies and risk management; and incident response.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[CTS 1650](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cts-computer-science/1000/cts-1650)
