# ETI 2315C Fluid and Pneumatic Instrumentation

In this course, students learn principles of pneumatic instrumentation and control loops encountered in the industry. Transmitter, indicator, positioner, controller, and actuator theories are covered in depth. Students also learn about operation, arrangement, and calibration methodologies. Students learn how to select and properly calibrate, maintain, and repair pneumatic testing devices.

3 credits
