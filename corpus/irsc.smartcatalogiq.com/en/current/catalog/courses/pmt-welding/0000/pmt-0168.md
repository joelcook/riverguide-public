# PMT 0168 Welding Certification Prep (N)

In this course, students work toward certifications in plate, pipe, and tubing for multiple combinations of filler materials, base materials, and positions in accordance with American Welding Society (AWS) standard D1.1, American Petroleum Institute (API) code 1104, and American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) welding codes. Students create a resume and prepare for job interviews.

30 hours

### Prerequisites

[PMT 0164](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pmt-welding/0000/pmt-0164) with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

pre/corequisite: [PMT 0164](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pmt-welding/0000/pmt-0164)
