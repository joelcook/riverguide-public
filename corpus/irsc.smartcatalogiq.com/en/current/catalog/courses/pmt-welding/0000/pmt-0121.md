# PMT 0121 Shielded Metal Arc Welding Principles (N)

In this course, students explore principles of Shielded Metal Arc Welding including power supplies, equipment set-up, joint configuration, electrode selection, electrode manipulation, arc control, and finished bead characteristics. Students complete fillet welds with different electrodes, and joint designs in multiple positions, to industry standards.

120 hours

### Prerequisites

[PMT 0108](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pmt-welding/0000/pmt-0108) with a grade of "C" or higher
