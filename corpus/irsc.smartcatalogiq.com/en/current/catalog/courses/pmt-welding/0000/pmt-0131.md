# PMT 0131 Gas Tungsten Arc Welding Principles (N)

In this course, students explore the fundamentals of gas tungsten arc welding (TIG). Emphasis is placed on safety, equipment setup, controls, and troubleshooting. Students learn torch manipulation and troubleshooting, and how to weld multiple joint designs on carbon steel in all positions.

120 hours

### Prerequisites

[PMT 0101C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pmt-welding/0000/pmt-0101c) with a grade of "C" or higher
