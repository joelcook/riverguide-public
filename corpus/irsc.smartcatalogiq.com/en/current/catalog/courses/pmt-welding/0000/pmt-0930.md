# PMT 0930 Special Topics in Welding (N)

In this course, students study current or special-interest topics in welding. The course may be repeated up to three times. Topics may include: specialty welding operations, robotic welding programming and operation, CAD/CAM plasma arc operations for the welding industry, and review for the welding certification exam.

30-150 hours
