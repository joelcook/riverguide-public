# PMT 0870 Apprenticeship - Welding OJT (N)

In this course students participate in on-the-job training to develop their skills and apply theoretical concepts introduced in the classroom components of the Welding Apprenticeship. On-the-Job Training (OJT) provides relevant learning experiences not always available in the classroom setting, along with career-related experience, and workplace competencies expected by employers in the welding industry.

667 hours
