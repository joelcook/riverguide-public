# MLT 1362L Hematology Lab

In this course, students engage in laboratory training in MLT skills related to hematology and coagulation. The emphasis is on appropriate collection, handling, processing, and testing of specimens in the area of hematology and coagulation.

1 credit

### Corequisites

[MLT 1362](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/1000/mlt-1362), [MLT 1040C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/1000/mlt-1040c)
