# MLT 1362 Hematology

In this course, students explore the basics of hematology concepts including blood cell maturation, blood cell disorders, and recognition of normal and abnormal blood cells.

4 credits

### Corequisites

[MLT 1040C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/1000/mlt-1040c), [MLT 1362L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/1000/mlt-1362l)
