# MLT 1330 Hemostasis

In this course, students examine the basics of laboratory testing in hemostasis including specimen processing, plasma coagulation components, and associated disorders.

3 credits

### Corequisites

[MLT 1362](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/1000/mlt-1362), [MLT 1362L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/1000/mlt-1362l)
