# MLT 1230 Urinalysis and Body Fluids

In this course, students review the principles of kidney function, chemical and microscopic examination of urine, and special urinalysis screening tests. Students study content pertaining to cerebrospinal fluid, gastric body fluids, gastric analysis, fecal analysis, and miscellaneous body fluids.

2 credits
