# MLT 1230L Urinalysis and Body Fluids Laboratory

In this course, students examine the laboratory concepts of urinalysis and body fluids which include the chemical, physical and microscopic characteristic of urine. Students also review the studies of cerebrospinal fluid, gastric fluid, fecal analysis and other miscellaneous body fluids.

1 credit
