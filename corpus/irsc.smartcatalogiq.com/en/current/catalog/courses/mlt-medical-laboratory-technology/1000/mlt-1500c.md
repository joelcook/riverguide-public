# MLT 1500C Immunology and Serology

In this course, students examine the formation, structure, and action of antigen-antibody reactions with performance of sero-diagnostic tests procedure.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[MLT 1525](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/1000/mlt-1525), [MLT 1525L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/1000/mlt-1525l)
