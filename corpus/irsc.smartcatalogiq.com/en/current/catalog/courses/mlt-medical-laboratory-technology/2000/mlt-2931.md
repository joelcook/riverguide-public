# MLT 2931 Medical Lab Technology Review II

In this course, students participate in a comprehensive review of knowledge and competencies required for career entry at the technician level of certification. Using information from the list of objectives for each previous course and clinical, components of competencies used for the certification examination are emphasized. Students are required to achieve a 76% or better on examinations in Clinical Chemistry, Urinalysis, and Body Fluids, Immunology, Microbiology and Virology. This course is meant to provide a transition from the student role to a graduate role.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[MLT 2930](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/2000/mlt-2930) with a grade of "C" or higher

### Corequisites

MLT students can select any 2 of the following MLT courses: [MLT 2807L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/2000/mlt-2807l), [MLT 2809L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/2000/mlt-2809l), [MLT 2810L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/2000/mlt-2810l), [MLT 2811L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mlt-medical-laboratory-technology/2000/mlt-2811l)
