# MLT 2400 Medical Microbiology

In this course, students review the general characteristics and classification of clinically important bacteria. Students examine the theory, practical application, and pathogenesis of clinical microbiology, including collection, setup, identification, susceptibility testing and reporting procedures.

4 credits
