# ASC 1572L sUAS Indoor Flight Training

In this course, students gain hands-on experience in unmanned aircraft systems in a controlled indoor environment. Through a sequence of carefully planned exercises, the students build their muscle memory and get used to navigating a small recreational drone through various types of obstacles. Other skills developed in this course are depth perception, roll, pitch, yaw, and attitude controls, and similar flight controls.

3 credits
