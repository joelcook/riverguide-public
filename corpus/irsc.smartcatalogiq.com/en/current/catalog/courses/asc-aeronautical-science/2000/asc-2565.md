# ASC 2565 FAA-107 Commercial Remote Pilot Ground School

Students learn a variety of subjects in aviation in this course with the intent of preparing the student for the FAA Part 107 commercial remote pilot test. Topics students learn include airspace classification, airport operations, weather effects, sUAS loading, emergency procedures, radio communication, physiological factors, test sites, UTM and sectional charts.

3 credits
