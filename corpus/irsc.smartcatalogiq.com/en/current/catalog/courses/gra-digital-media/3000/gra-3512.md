# GRA 3512 Branding and Corporate Identity

In this course, students explore the application of visual identity systems for large, medium, and small companies. Comprehensive research on color symbolism, graphic forms, typography, and design is required. Students design identity systems stressing creative solutions and originality.

3 credits
