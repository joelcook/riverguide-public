# GRA 3735 Multimedia Video Production

In this course, students explore narrative and experimental forms of time-based graphics software and their uses in digital media projects. Time-based content is integrated into multimedia video projects for a variety purposes. The emphasis is on creating cohesive portfolio-quality work.

3 credits
