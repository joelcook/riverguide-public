# GRA 3102 Visual Communication I

In this course, students increase their awareness and understanding of the impact that images have in a visual message. Explored are design principles and image making techniques including the idea, concept development, information hierarchy, visual metaphors, and composition. Through lectures, examples, and independent research, students create visual work demonstrating a variety of major design influences.

3 credits
