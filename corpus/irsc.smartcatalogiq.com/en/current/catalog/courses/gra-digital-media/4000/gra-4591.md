# GRA 4591 Art Direction and Creative Process

In this course, students explore the role of the art director in the production of multi-faceted design projects. Students work collaboratively on leadership, communication, and negotiation skills. Emphasis is placed on coordinating creative effort from concept to finished product.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[GRA 3512](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/gra-digital-media/3000/gra-3512) with grade of 'C' or higher
