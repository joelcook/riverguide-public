# GRA 4950 Portfolio-Graphic Design and Vis Com

In this course, students develop an advanced digital media portfolio with emphasis on printed and digital materials, including an online format. Topics include the creation of a personal business packet and self-promotional pieces. Interview and job search skills are discussed and developed. Individual assignments are given to strengthen and round out each portfolio. Students organize a public exhibition of work. Industry professionals review student work and advise on career opportunities.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[GRA 3512](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/gra-digital-media/3000/gra-3512) with grade of 'C' or higher, or permission of instructor
