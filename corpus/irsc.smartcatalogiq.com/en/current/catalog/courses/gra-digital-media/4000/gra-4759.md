# GRA 4759 Scripting for Websites 2

In this course, students explore advanced concepts in website design, including interactivity, animation, user interface (UI), user experience (UX), and trends in social media. It is for students who have mastered the skills of building a basic website and want to advance to more sophisticated interface design and techniques. Students are challenged to solve advanced communication problems while addressing technical issues related to website design. Students work with industry standard tools to produce aesthetically complex websites.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[GRA 3758](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/gra-digital-media/3000/gra-3758) with grade 'C' or higher
