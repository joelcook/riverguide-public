# GRA 1206 Typography

In this course, students explore typography as a primary tool of graphic designers by creating expressive, technical, and visual aspects of type. These creations require the knowledgeable use of typeface, size, leading, line length, headlines, hierarchy, and overall development of typographic aesthetics. Students explore the importance of experimentation with typographic design in visual expression.

3 credits
