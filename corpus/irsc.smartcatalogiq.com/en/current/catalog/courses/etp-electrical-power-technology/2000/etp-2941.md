# ETP 2941 Professional Internship for Maintenance Technicians

In this course, students work in a utility or corporation that has an on-the-job training program, if they meet the employment criteria of these companies. Students work on assignments pertinent to their career track of choice under the guidance and supervision of experienced professionals. Permission from the instructor is required.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[EET 1025C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/1000/eet-1025c) with grade of 'C' or higher
