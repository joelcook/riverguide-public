# ECO 2023 Principles of Economics Micro

In this course, students learn economic theory and the fundamentals of economic analysis. Students examine theories and applications related to the following core set of topics in microeconomics: supply and demand, elasticity, market structures, price determination, factors of production, distribution of income and effects of monopoly and oligopoly on markets.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Student must score into college-level English and reading on the placement test.
