# PHY 2049 Physics with Calculus II

This course is a continuation of [PHY-2048](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2048) including Coulomb's Law, electric fields and potentials, capacitance, currents and circuit, Ampere's Law, Faraday's Law, inductance, Maxwell's equations, electromagnetic waves, ray optics, interference, and diffraction.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHY 2048](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2048), [PHY 2048L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2048l), [MAC 2312](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/2000/mac-2312) or higher, all with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PHY 2049L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2049l)
