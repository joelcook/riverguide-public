# PHY 2048L Physics with Calculus I Lab

This is the lab component for [PHY-2048](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2048). Lab experiences include the areas of Newtonian mechanics, including motion, vectors, Newton's laws, work and conservation of energy, systems of particles, collisions, equilibrium oscillations, and waves.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[MAC 2311](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/2000/mac-2311) with a grade of C or higher; student must score into college-level reading on placement test.

### Corequisites

[PHY 2048](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/2000/phy-2048)
