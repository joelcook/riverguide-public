# PHY 1054 College Physics II

In this course, the second in a two-part series intended for non-physics majors, students learn about topics including wave motion; Coulomb's Law; electric fields and potentials; capacitance, currents and circuits; magnetism and Faraday's Law; and ray optics.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHY 1053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1053), [PHY 1053L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1053l), all with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites
