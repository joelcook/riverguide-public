# PHY 1053L College Physics I Lab

In this course, students conduct lab experiments in areas of physics which include kinematics, dynamics and statics, momentum and energy, and rotation. This course is the lab component for [PHY 1053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1053).

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[MAC 1114](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1114) or [MAC 1147](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1147) or [MAC 2311](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/2000/mac-2311), with a grade of C or higher.

### Corequisites

[PHY 1053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1053)

Lab fee $60.
