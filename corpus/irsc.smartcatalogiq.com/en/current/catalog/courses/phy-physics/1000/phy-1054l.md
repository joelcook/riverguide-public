# PHY 1054L College Physics II Lab

In this course, students conduct lab experiments in areas of physics which include electric charge, fields and circuits; electromagnetism, applied electricity; geometrical, wave, and applied optics; electrons and photons; atoms, and nuclei.  This course is the lab component for [PHY 1054](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1054).

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[PHY 1053](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1053), [PHY 1053L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1053l), all with a grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PHY 1054](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/phy-physics/1000/phy-1054)
