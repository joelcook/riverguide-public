# PHY 1053 College Physics I

This course is the first in a two-part series intended for non-physics majors, offering an algebra and trigonometry approach to topics such as kinematics, dynamics, energy, momentum, rotational motion, fluid dynamics, oscillatory motion, and waves. The course fosters analytical and critical thinking skills to promote a scientific understanding of the real world.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MAC 1114](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1114) or [MAC 1147](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1147) or [MAC 2311](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/2000/mac-2311), with a grade of C or higher.
