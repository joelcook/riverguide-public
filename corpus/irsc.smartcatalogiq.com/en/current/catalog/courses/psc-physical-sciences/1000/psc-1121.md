# PSC 1121 Survey of Physical Science

This course provides non-science majors with an introduction to the various scientific disciplines that collectively seek to understand the physical universe. Students learn an appreciation for the scientific method.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Student must test into college-level reading on placement test.
