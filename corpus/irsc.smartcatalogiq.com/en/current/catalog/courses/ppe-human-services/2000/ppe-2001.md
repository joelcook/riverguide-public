# PPE 2001 Person and Personality Development

In this course, students examine the major theories of personality, personality development, and personality assessment. The major theorists include Freud, Erikson, Jung, Adler, Laing, Fromm, Maslow, Skinner, Rogers, and Ellis. Objectives include exploration of the ways phenomena such as anxiety, creativity, happiness, and love relate to personality.

3 credits
