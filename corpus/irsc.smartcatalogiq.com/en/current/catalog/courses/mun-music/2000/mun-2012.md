# MUN 2012 Instrumental Ensemble

Students rehearse and perform various genres of String, Wind, Percussion and chamber music literature in a small chamberwinds setting. All participants are required to have previous knowledge and proficiency on a specific wind, percussion, or string instrument.

1 credit
