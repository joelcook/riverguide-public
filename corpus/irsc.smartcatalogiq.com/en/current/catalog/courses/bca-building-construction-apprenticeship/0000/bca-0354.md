# BCA 0354 Apprenticeship - Electrical 5 (N)

In this course, students continue to build upon the knowledge and skill develop in[BCA 0353](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0353) and work in conjunction with the activities of [BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349). Students develops competencies in the installation of residential wiring.

Instructor permission required.

90 hours
