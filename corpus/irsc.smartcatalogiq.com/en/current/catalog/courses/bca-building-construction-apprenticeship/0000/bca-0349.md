# BCA 0349 Apprenticeship - Electrical On-the-Job Training (N)

In this course, students apply skills and theoretical concepts introduced in the classroom components of the Electrical Apprenticeship in the field. On-the-Job Training (OJT) provides relevant learning experiences not always available in the classroom setting, along with career-related experience, and workplace competencies expected by employers of the electrical industry.

640 hours

### Prerequisites

Permission of instructor
