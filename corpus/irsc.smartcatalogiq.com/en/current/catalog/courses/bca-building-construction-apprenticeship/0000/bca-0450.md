# BCA 0450 Apprenticeship - Plumbing 1 (N)

In this course studentsdevelop the competencies essential to pipe trades. These competencies relate to career and training opportunities, the use and care of tools, safety precautions, basic math applications, standards and codes, and human relations.

90 hours
