# BCA 0351 Apprenticeship - Electrical 2 (N)

In this course, students continue to build upon the knowledge and skill develop in [BCA 0350](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0350) and work in conjunction with the activities of [BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349). The course enhances competencies related to safety practices, direct current electrical circuits, communication, math applications, electrical codes, and employability skills.

Instructor permission required.

90 hours

### Corequisites

[BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349)
