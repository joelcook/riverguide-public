# BCA 0355 Apprenticeship - Electrical 6 (N)

In this course, students continue to build upon the knowledge and skill develop in [BCA 0354](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0354) and work in conjunction with the activities of [BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349). Students learned more advanced concepts applicable to installation of residential wiring.

Instructor permission required.

90 hours

### Corequisites

[BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349)
