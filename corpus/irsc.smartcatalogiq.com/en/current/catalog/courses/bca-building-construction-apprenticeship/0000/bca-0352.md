# BCA 0352 Apprenticeship - Electrical 3 (N)

In this course, students continue to build upon the knowledge and skill develop in [BCA 0351](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0351) and work in conjunction with the activities of [BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349). The course provides instruction in electrical math and alternating current circuit skills.

Instructor permission required.

90 hours

### Corequisites

[BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349)
