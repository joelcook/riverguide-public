# BCA 0350 Apprenticeship - Electrical 1 (N)

In this course, students develop the essential competencies for working in the construction electrical industry. These competencies include safety practices, direct current-electrical-circuit skills, appropriate communication and math skills, basic electricity and electrical codes, and employability skills.

90 hours
