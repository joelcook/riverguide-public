# BCA 0353 Apprenticeship - Electrical 4 (N)

In this course, students continue to build upon the knowledge and skill develop in [BCA 0352](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0352) and work in conjunction with the activities of [BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349). Students develops the competencies needed for employment in the residential electrical industry, including electrical math, alternating current circuits, and troubleshooting residential electrical systems.

Instructor permission required.

90 hours

### Corequisites

[BCA 0349](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bca-building-construction-apprenticeship/0000/bca-0349)
