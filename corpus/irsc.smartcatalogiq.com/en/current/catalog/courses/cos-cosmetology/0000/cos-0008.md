# COS 0008 Cosmetology III: Skillset Review (N)

In this course, studentsreview and apply all cosmetology theory and practical skills acquired throughout the program. Students continue to refine and master their technical abilities in a supervised clinic environment while returning to the classroom for theory review and state board exam preparation. Instruction emphasizes speed, accuracy, sanitation, and professional standards. In addition, students receive guidance in career readiness, including job placement assistance, résumé building, interviewing techniques, and salon business practices.

300 hours

### Prerequisites

[COS 0010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cos-cosmetology/0000/cos-0010), [COS 0088](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cos-cosmetology/0000/cos-0088)
