# COS 0088 Cosmetology II: Applied Skills and Tactics (N)

In this course, studentsbuild foundational knowledge and skills introduced in Cosmetology I. Students will refine technical abilities and apply advanced cosmetology procedures, including haircutting and hairstyling, advanced chemical services, creative hair coloring, skin care, nail enhancements, and salon business management. Emphasis is placed on developing speed, accuracy, creativity, and professional client relations through both classroom instruction and hands-on clinic practice.

450 hours
