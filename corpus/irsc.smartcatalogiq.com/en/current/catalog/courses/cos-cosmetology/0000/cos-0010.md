# COS 0010 Cosmetology I: Theory of Cosmetology (N)

In this course, students acquire foundational, theoretical, and practical procedures of cosmetology. Core topics include infection control, nail diseases and disorders, principles of hair design, chemical texture services, and hair coloring. Students apply their learning in both classroom and clinic settings to develop essential cosmetology skills and techniques. Upon completion, students are prepared to advance their training and perform basic services in a supervised clinic environment.

450 hours
