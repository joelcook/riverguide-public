# ENC 0017 Integrated Reading and Writing (N)

This co-requisite course is intended to be taken concurrently with a linked section of English Composition I ([ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101)) and provides foundational and individualized support for [ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101). This course supplements skills in reading, grammar, and composition. In addition to introducing students to institutional resources, the course aids in developing the comprehension, organization, and editing skills needed for reading and writing at the college level. Simultaneous successful completion of both linked courses is required.

Credit does not apply toward degree.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

Non-exempt students must submit placement scores.
