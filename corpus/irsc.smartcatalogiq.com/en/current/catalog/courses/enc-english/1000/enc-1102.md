# ENC 1102 English Composition II

As an extension of [ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101), student work in this composition course emphasizes textual interpretation and research methods. Students read a variety of texts and demonstrate analytical and critical thinking skills through multiple writing assignments.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101) with a grade of "C" or higher
