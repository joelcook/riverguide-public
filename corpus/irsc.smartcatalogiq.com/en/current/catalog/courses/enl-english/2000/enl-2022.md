# ENL 2022 English Literature: Romantic Era to Present

In this course, students survey English literature from the Romantic era to the present. The readings will include selections from the Western canon. Students demonstrate college-level skills in multiple assignments.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101) with a grade of "C" or higher
