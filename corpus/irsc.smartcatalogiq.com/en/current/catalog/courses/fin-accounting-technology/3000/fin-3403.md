# FIN 3403 Corporate Finance

In this course, students explore an overview of the acquisition, financing, and management of business assets. An integrated perspective on financial markets, financial institutions, and financial management is included along with how businesses meet their financial goals. Topics include securities markets, evaluation of securities, financial statement analysis, financial planning and forecasting, the time value of money, risk and return, valuation, weighted average cost of capital, debt and equity capital structures, capital budgeting, ratio analysis, cash flow, and miscellaneous financial decision-making techniques.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ACG 2071](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/acg-accounting/2000/acg-2071), [ECO 2013](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eco-economics/2000/eco-2013), [ECO 2023](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eco-economics/2000/eco-2023), all with grade of 'C' or higher
