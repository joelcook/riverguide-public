# FIN 2001 Introduction to Finance

In this course, students explore the  theory and practice of financial management that focuses on the practitioner, with particular emphasis for the small to medium size business owner, as well as one who performs in a middle management capacity in a large firm. Students gain preparation for employment in areas such as business development, installment, credit, commercial loans, or public relations.

3 credits
