# AML 2010 American Literature through Reconstruction

In this course, students survey American literature from its origins to Civil War Reconstruction. The readings will include selections from the Western canon. Students demonstrate college-level writing skills in multiple assignments.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101) with a grade of "C" or higher
