# AML 2020 American Literature from Reconstruction to Present

In this course, students survey American literature from Civil War Reconstruction to the present. The readings will include selections from the Western canon. Students demonstrate college-level writing skills in multiple assignments.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[ENC 1101](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/enc-english/1000/enc-1101) with a grade of "C" or higher
