# ASL 1150 American Sign Language II

In this course, students advance their proficiency in American Sign Language through intermediate grammatical structures and an expanded vocabulary, enabling more complex conversations. They deepen their understanding of Deaf culture, enhancing their communicative skills, including both receptive and expressive capabilities. Through interactive exercises and immersive activities, students refine their ASL fluency and cultural insights, preparing themselves for further linguistic and cultural exploration.

4 credits

### Prerequisites

[ASL 1140](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/asl-sign-language/1000/asl-1140)
