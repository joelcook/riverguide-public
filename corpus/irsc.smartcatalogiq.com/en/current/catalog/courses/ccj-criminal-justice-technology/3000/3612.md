# CCJ 3612 Criminal and Delinquent Behavior

Within this course, the student examines patterns of criminal and delinquent behaviors through theoretical concepts and their influence on public policies, including the development of evidence-based approaches for preventing and mitigating crime and delinquency.

3 credits
