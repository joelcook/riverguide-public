# CCJ 3641 Organized Crime

Within this course, the student examines the world of organized crime, by analyzing it from a social perspective. International and domestic criminal groups are examined including relevant legislation, multi-agency task forces, international cooperation, and threat analysis. The student analyzes the history, current role, and future of organized crime; and develops a framework for understanding the importance of how the world economy expands and is shadowed by a criminal economy.

3 credits
