# CCJ 4678 Race, Gender, Ethnicity and Crime

Within this course the students examines issues of race, gender and ethnicity and their
relationship to crime throughout the history of the United States. Topics
include the effect of changes in the legal and social climate including the
passage of the 13th and 14th Amendments, the civil rights movement, the women's
movement, gender orientation and immigration.

3 credits
