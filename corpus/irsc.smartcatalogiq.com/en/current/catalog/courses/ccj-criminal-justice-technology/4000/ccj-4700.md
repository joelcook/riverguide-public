# CCJ 4700 Methods of Research in Criminal Justice

Within this course, the student examines how research is conducted in criminology and criminal justice. The student explores the research process, ethical issues, different types of quantitative and qualitative research designs, data analysis, and the reporting of research results.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Permission required from the Criminal Justice Department Chair to enroll
