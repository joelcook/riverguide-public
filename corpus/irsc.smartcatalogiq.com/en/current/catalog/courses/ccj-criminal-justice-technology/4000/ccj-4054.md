# CCJ 4054 Criminal Justice Ethics and Liability

Criminal Justice Ethics and Liability allows the student a critical examination of conduct and ethics of criminal justice supervisors, managers, and leaders that may give rise to civil liability.  The student additionally conducts a critical examination of decision-making and human relations within criminal justice agencies.

3 credits
