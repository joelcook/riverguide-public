# DES 1020 Head, Neck & Dental Anatomy

In this course, students learn tooth morphology and the relationship of the teeth to one another and to their surrounding structures. Students study the occlusion of the teeth, bones of the skull, muscles, nerves, and blood vessels.

3 credits
