# DES 1800 Introduction to Clinical Procedures

In this course, students learndental office professionalism, patient reception and positioning for the delivery of care. Students study instrument exchange and oral evacuation, disease transmission and infection control, principles and techniques of disinfection, principles and techniques of instrument procession and sterilization, occupational health and safety, chemical and waste management. Students discuss topics such as patient information and assessment, vital signs, oral diagnosis and treatment planning, coronal polishing, history of dentistry, and the dental health team.

2 credits
