# DES 1051C Nitrous Oxide Inhalation Analgesia

In this course, students will assist the dentist in the initiation of nitrous-oxide inhalation analgesia as required by Chapter 64B5 of the Florida State Board of Dentistry.

1 credit

### Course Fee

Lab fee $50.
