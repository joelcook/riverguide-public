# BOT 3015 Plant Biology

In this course, students learn the history and impact of plants on people and the environment, emphasizing structural and functional relationships and the ecological adaptations of plants. Students survey the evolutionary relationships among plants, fungi, selected Protista, and prokaryotes.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), all with C or higher
