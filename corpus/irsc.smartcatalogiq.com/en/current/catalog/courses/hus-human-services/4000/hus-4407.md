# HUS 4407 Substance Abuse and Aging

In this course, students examine the problem of substance abuse in the aging population, including the abuse of prescription medicines, alcohol, and illegal drugs. Students discuss issues related to lack of independence, mobility, depression, and bereavement.

3 credits
