# HUS 4319 Introduction to Play Therapy

In this course, students examine the theoretical bases for play therapy. Students also describe the basic elements of play therapy including the role of the play therapist and indications for using play therapy. Practical exercises include in-class application of play therapy techniques in simulated scenarios.

3 credits
