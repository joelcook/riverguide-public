# HUS 4410 Internet Addictions

In this course, students examine the dynamics of Internet addiction. Focus includes identifying the issues related to diagnosis and treatment of this emerging problem. Various types of Internet addiction are examined including online gambling, online affairs, and pornography.

3 credits
