# HUS 4574 Issues of Aging and Family Dynamics

In this course, students examine issues such as health, finances, and social roles that affect the elderly and ways in which this influence familial role changes and independence. A holistic approach to the overall well-being of the elderly in particular and the family unit in general is presented.

3 credits
