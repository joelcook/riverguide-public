# HUS 3351 Family Systems and Dynamics

In this course, students examine family systems theories and the roles of family members in “traditional” and “non-traditional” families. Students describe the theoretical bases for family therapy modalities. Role playing and skill-building exercises allow for practical application of techniques.

3 credits
