# HUS 3360 Sexual Abuse of Children and Adolescents

In this course, students prepare for the interviewing, evaluation, assessment, and networking skills necessary for working effectively with child abuse victims. Videos and movies are used to illustrate techniques and strategies for interviewing abused children. Individual projects involving creation of comprehensive prevention of sexual abuse programs are included.

3 credits
