# HUS 3350 Issues in Intimate Partner Abuse and Family Violence

In this course, students examine the theories explaining familial abuse and learn evaluation and outreach skills. The dynamics of partner violence, child abuse, elder abuse, and sibling violence are examined. Legal issues related to family violence are also addressed.

3 credits
