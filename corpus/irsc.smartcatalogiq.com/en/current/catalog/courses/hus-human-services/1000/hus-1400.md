# HUS 1400 Introduction to Drugs of Abuse

In this course, students examine the dynamics of drug addiction and dependence, classification and origins of drugs, short and long-term effects, risk of dependence, and medical uses. Students also examine drug education, laws, treatment, and rehabilitation.

3 credits
