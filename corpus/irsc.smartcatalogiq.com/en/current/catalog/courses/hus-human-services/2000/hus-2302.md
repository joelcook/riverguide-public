# HUS 2302 Techniques of Interviewing and Intervention

In this course, students examine the basic techniques and theories of interviewing, evaluation, and intervention. Students engage in classroom skill-building exercises and role plays. Special attention is given to crisis theory and applied interventions.

3 credits
