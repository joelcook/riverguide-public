# SCE 3905 Science through Tutoring

In this course students explore the general science skills needed for successfully tutoring in an academic setting, general methods of tutoring, and the tutoring techniques needed in specific courses. Teacher-tutor seminars, teacher-tutor conferences, and formal instruction supplement the extensive tutoring experiences.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214), nine (9) hours of science content courses, or permission of instructor, all with grade of C or higher
