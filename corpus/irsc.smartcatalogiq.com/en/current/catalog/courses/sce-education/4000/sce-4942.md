# SCE 4942 Teaching Secondary Science Practicum

In this course students present interactive curriculum projects to secondary school students in local area school districts. Students spend a minimum of 30 school-based hours in the secondary school classroom. Project presentations are coordinated with in-service secondary school teachers and their curriculum schedules and needs. This course addresses specific Florida State Standards, subject matter competencies, and pedagogy pertinent to the discipline and required for certification.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[EDF 2005](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/2000/edf-2005), [EDF 3214](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edf-education/3000/edf-3214); Secondary Biology [EDG 3343](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/3000/edg-3343), nine (9) hours of science content courses, all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

Middle Grades [EDG 3343](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/edg-education/3000/edg-3343) or Secondary Biology [SCE 3360](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sce-education/3000/sce-3360).
