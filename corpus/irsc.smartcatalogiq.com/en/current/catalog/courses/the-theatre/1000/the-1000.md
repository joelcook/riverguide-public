# THE 1000 Introduction to Theatre

In this course, students will explore dramatic structure, techniques, and various organizational elements. The course provides an introduction to theatre as a collaborative art form through the critical analysis of its historical context, production, theory, and connections to theatrical literature, including the Western canon.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Student must score into college-level English and reading on placement test.
