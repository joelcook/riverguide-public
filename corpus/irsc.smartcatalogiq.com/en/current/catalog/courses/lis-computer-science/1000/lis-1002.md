# LIS 1002 Information Literacy and Research Skills

In this course, students learn college-level information literacy skills. These skills include the ability to find and access resources of different types, to analyze those sources, and to document using different types of citation styles. Students also apply information literacy and critical thinking skills to course content.

1 credit
