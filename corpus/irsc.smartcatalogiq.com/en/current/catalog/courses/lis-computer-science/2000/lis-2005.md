# LIS 2005 Advanced Information Literacy and Research Skills

In this course, students learn advanced research skills which allow students to distinguish among different research types and access points according to the information cycle. Students apply critical thinking skills, information literacy techniques, and effective search strategies to develop an insightful and defendable thesis statement and an essay that uses ethical and documented source citations.

3 credits
