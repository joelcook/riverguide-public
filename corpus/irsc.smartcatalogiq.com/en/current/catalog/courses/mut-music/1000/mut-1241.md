# MUT 1241 Sightsinging and Ear Training I

In this course, students further develop advanced aural and vocal skills based on melodic, rhythmical, and vocal music principals, with additional focus on advanced ear training.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[MUT 1221](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mut-music/1000/mut-1221), [MUT 1222](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mut-music/1000/mut-1222)
