# MUT 2117 Theory of Music IV

In this course, students develop mastery of even more complex elements of musical form and analysis (melody, harmony, rhythm, and structure) and develop conservatory-ready skills in the science of music theory.  The musical concepts presented in this course are primarily based in the Western tradition and students analyze highly complex musical literature. Students also prepare for continued study of music theory at the University level.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MUT 1111](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mut-music/1000/mut-1111), [MUT 1112](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mut-music/1000/mut-1112), [MUT 2116](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mut-music/2000/mut-2116)
