# CJE 3065 Police and Society

During this course the student is provided a examination of the
function of police in a democracy. It focuses on the changing role of law
enforcement throughout history and the impetus for these changes including key
legislation, court rulings, and shifts in the societal fabric. The need for law
enforcement to respond and adapt to changes as well as current and emerging
issues are addressed.

3 credits
