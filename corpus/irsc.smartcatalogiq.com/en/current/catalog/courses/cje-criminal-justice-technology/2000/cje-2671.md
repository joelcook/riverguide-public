# CJE 2671 Latent Fingerprint 1

This course provides a foundation in basic fingerprinting. Students learn topics which include classification, identification, filing and rolling of fingerprints, problems and practices associated with post mortem fingerprinting and proper presentation of fingerprint evidence.

3 credits
