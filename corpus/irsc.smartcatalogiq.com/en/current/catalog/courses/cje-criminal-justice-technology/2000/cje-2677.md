# CJE 2677 Latent Fingerprint Development 2

This course provides a continuation of [CJE 2671](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cje-criminal-justice-technology/2000/cje-2671) Basic Fingerprinting. Students learn different methods involved in detection, enhancement, and recovery of latent fingerprints. Techniques involve chemical and mechanical methods on substrates and evaluation for proper application in both theory and practices.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[CJE 2671](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cje-criminal-justice-technology/2000/cje-2671) with a grade of C or higher
