# CJE 1002 Police Procedures

In this course, students develop knowledge on key issues facing law enforcement in performing their policing duties. It emphasizes what the police responsibility is, the constitutional and statutory constraints under which police function and how the tasks to be performed can be accomplished responsibly and humanely within these constraints.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Permission of Criminal Justice Department Chair
