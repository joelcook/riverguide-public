# CJE 1641 Intro to Crime Scene Technology

This is an introductory course in Crime Scene Technology. Students learn the techniques, materials, and instrumentation used in securing, searching, recording, collecting, and examining physical evidence. There is special emphasis on the tools, instruments, and techniques used in the studies of crime scene reconstruction, fingerprints, firearms, tool marks, and blood stain pattern analysis.

3 credits
