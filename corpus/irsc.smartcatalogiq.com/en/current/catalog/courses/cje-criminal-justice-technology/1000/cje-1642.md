# CJE 1642 Crime Scene Technology 2

This is the second course in Crime Scene Technology. Students continue to examine and develop the techniques, materials and instrumentation used in securing, searching, recording, collecting, and examining physical evidence. There is special emphasis on the tools, instruments, and techniques used in the studies of crime scene reconstruction, fingerprints, firearms, tool marks, and blood stain pattern analysis.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[CJE 1641](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cje-criminal-justice-technology/1000/cje-1641) with a grade of C or higher
