# CJE 1772 Crime Scene Photography II

This course expands upon concepts, knowledge and skills taught in Crime Scene Photography 1. Students learn to include specialty light sources, lighting techniques, shutter speed, depth of field, filters and utilizing of specialized equipment.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[CJE 1673](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/cje-criminal-justice-technology/1000/cje-1673) with a grade of C or higher
