# HFT 2210 Hospitality Mgmt & Leadership

Students examine managerial functions in the hospitality industry and training techniques to become effective managers by applying management theories. Much emphasis is placed on human relations and how to work efficiently with peers, superiors, and subordinates.

3 credits
