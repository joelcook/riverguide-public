# EDP 2002 Introduction to Educational Psychology

This course introduces psychology as it is applies to teaching and learning with a focus on the characteristics of physical, social, cultural, emotional, and intellectual differences among learners. Major topics include the study of the teaching/learning process, problem-solving, motivation and assessments.

3 credits
