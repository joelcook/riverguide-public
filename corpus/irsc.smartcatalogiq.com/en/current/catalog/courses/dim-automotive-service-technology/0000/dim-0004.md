# DIM 0004 Introduction to Diesel Mechanics (N)

In this course students explore shop organization, management, safety, and workplace communication skills. Students recognize, identify, and demonstrate the safe use of tools and equipment, and demonstrate knowledge in diesel engine construction, operation, and servicing.

150 hours
