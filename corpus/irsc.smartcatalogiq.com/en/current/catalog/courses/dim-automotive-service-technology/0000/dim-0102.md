# DIM 0102 Diesel Electrical and Electronics Technician (N)

In this course, students learn foundational skills and concepts related to electrical systems and components in preparation for entry into the Diesel Engine Service industry. Students study general electrical systems, batteries, starting, charging, lighting, gauges, warning devices, and related electrical system diagnostics, service, and repair.

300 hours
