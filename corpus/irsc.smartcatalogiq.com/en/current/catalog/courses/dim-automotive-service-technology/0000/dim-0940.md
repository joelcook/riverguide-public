# DIM 0940 Diesel Technician On-the-Job-Training (N)

In this On-the-Job Training (OJT) course, students apply the skills and concepts learned in the Diesel Technician Apprenticeship program. Students practice workplace competencies in a real-world job setting and maintain state-required documentation throughout the course.
