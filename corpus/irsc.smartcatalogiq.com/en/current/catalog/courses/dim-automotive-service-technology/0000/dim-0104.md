# DIM 0104 Diesel Engine Technician (N)

In this course, students learn foundational skills and concepts related to diesel engine components and other systems in preparation for entry into the Diesel Engine Service industry. Students study engine, cylinder head, valve train, engine block, lubrication, cooling, air induction, exhaust, fuel, and engine brakes diagnostics, service, and repair.

300 hours
