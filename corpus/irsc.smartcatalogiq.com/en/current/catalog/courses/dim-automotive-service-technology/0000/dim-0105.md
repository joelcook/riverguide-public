# DIM 0105 Diesel Brakes Technician (N)

In this course, students learn foundational skills and concepts related to diesel braking systems in preparation for entry into the Diesel Engine Service industry. Students study the diagnosis, service, and repair of air and hydraulic brakes. Students study diagnostic, service, and repair of air, and hydraulic brakes. Students prepare for ASE T4 brake certification.

300 hours
