# DIM 2021C Diesel Service (N)

In this course, students develop the skills to identify and compare diesel fuel systems and differentiate between gasoline and diesel exhaust systems. They gain hands-on experience replacing tires and diagnosing and repairing steering and suspension components. Students are introduced to air brake systems and learn how to service wheel ends, including bearings and hubs, as well as perform drum and disc brake repairs.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[AER 1081C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1081c), [AER 1198C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1198c), [AER 1694C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/1000/aer-1694c), [AER 2898C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/aer-automotive-service-technology/2000/aer-2898c)
