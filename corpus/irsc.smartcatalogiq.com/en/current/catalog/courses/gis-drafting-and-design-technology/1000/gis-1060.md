# GIS 1060 Introduction to GIS with ArcGIS

In this course students explore how to implement Geographic Information Systems (GIS) by utilizing ESRI's (Environmental Systems Research Institute) current ArcGIS software. Special focus in on GIS software and procedures by viewing, analyzing, and producing maps based on various geographic/spatial data including local area information.

3 credits
