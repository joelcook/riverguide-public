# AST 1931 Introduction to Planetarium Operations

In this course, students who are interested in planetarium operations and are invited by the instructor learn about planetarium maintenance, creating educational programming, and multimedia production techniques. They also explore K–12 astronomy education curricula, the logistics of field trip experiences, public programming, and recorded and interactive live presentations.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[AST 1002](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ast-physical-sciences/1000/ast-1002) with a grade of C or higher
