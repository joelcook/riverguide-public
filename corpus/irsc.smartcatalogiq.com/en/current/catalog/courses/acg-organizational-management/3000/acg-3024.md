# ACG 3024 Accounting for Non-Financial Majors

In this course, students explore the use of accounting and financial information in a managerial setting to support decision making. Students study the interpretation of accounting information and the language of financial accounting to effectively participate in activities such as planning, investment, control, and managerial decision-making.

3 credits
