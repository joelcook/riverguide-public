# CJK 0109 Review for Basic Corrections (N)

In this course, students participate in the review of course content in the Corrections Basic Recruit Training Program and participate in a State Officer Review Exam to prepare for the State Officer Certification Exam for Correctional Officers.

24 hours

### Prerequisites

Basic Corrections Recruit Training
