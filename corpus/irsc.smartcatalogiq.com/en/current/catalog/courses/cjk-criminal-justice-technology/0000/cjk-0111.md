# CJK 0111 Interviewing and Report Writing in Corrections (N)

In this course students learn interviewing techniques and report writing skills for correctional officers. Emphasis is placed on information gathering, documentation, and clear, accurate written communication. Topics include interview methods, statement collection, note-taking, and the preparation of reports in accordance with agency standards.

16 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Correctional Basic Recruit program
