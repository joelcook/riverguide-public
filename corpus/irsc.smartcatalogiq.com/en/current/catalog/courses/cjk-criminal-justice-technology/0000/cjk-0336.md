# CJK 0336 Incidents and Emergencies in Correctional Facilities (N)

In this course students learn the identification and management of incidents and emergencies within correctional facilities. Emphasis is placed on maintaining control, ensuring safety, and following established response procedures. Topics include disturbances, medical emergencies, escapes, communication, and reporting requirements.

20 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Correctional Basic Recruit Academy program
