# CJK 0327 Shift Management and Safety (N)

In this course students learn shift operations and safety procedures in correctional facilities. Emphasis is placed on inmate supervision, counts, movement control, contraband control, and facility inspections. Topics include maintaining security, identifying hazards, and applying appropriate safety and emergency response procedures to ensure a safe and secure environment.

20 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Correctional Basic Recruit program
