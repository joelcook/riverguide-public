# CJK 0393 Crossover Program Updates (N)

This course provides expanded or updated instruction required by changes made by the Criminal Justice Standards & Training Commission during an ongoing course. This course covers multiple changes to CJSTC programs.

8 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance to Crossover for C.O. to L.E. or L.E. to C.O. programs
