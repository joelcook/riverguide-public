# CJK 0461 Basic Recruit Wellness Program (N)

In this course, students develop the physical fitness necessary to fulfill the role of a Police Officer through enhancing the cardiovascular conditioning of the Basic Recruit.

40 hours

### Prerequisites

Must be enrolled in the Law Enforcement Academy Track program or receive the permission of Criminal Justice Department Chair.
