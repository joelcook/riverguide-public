# CJK 0306 Communications for Correctional Officers (N)

In this course students learn the effective communication skills required in correctional settings. Emphasis is placed on verbal and nonverbal communication, report writing, interpersonal interactions, and conflict resolution. Topics include interviewing techniques, documentation, radio procedures, and communication strategies to maintain safety, security, and professionalism.

32 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Correctional Basic Recruit program
