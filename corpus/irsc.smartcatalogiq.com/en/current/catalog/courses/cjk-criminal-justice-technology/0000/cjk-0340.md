# CJK 0340 Officer Wellness and Physical Fitness (N)

This course prepares students physically to perform the duties of a correctional officer and mentally with wellness and nutritional coursework structured to improve overall health condition.

30 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Corrections Basic Recruit Academy program
