# CJK 0421 Conducted Electrical Weapon/Dart Firing Stun Gun (N)

At the end of this course, students know the legal and use of force aspects of using a stun gun or CEW, how using a stun gun or CEW affects the human body, and how to operate a stun gun or CEW safely.

4 hours
