# CJK 0093 Critical Incidents (N)

This course enables students to identify the crowd control procedures in order to safely and effectively disperse or control a large group of people, understand local emergency response plans, respond to a bomb threat, assess the scene, search and evacuate a building or suspected bomb sight, identify weapons of mass destruction (WMD), and properly respond to a WMD incident.

44 hours
