# CJK 0400 Traffic Incidents (N)

This course teaches students the basic of traffic incidents to include legal implications, licensing, insurance requirements, ticketing/citations, and unoccupied vehicles. It also covers directing pedestrian and vehicular traffic. This course is part of the FDLE mandated basic Law Enforcement Academy training curriculum.

12 hours
