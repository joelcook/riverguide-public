# CJK 0040C Criminal Justice Firearms (N)

This course provides law enforcement, correctional, and correctional probation basic recruit students with instruction on the basic knowledge and proficiency skills needed to safely handle and shoot a handgun (semiautomatic pistol or revolver) and long gun (shotgun or rifle/carbine). Students are instructed on firearm safety, ammunition use, handgun and long gun use, use of cover and concealment, and survival shooting. Firearms instructors have some flexibility in teaching basic principles but may not alter proficiency skill requirements.

80 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Law Enforcement Basic Recruit Training program

### Course Fee

Lab fee $760. Insurance fee $1000.00.
