# CJK 0324 Supervising in a Correctional Facility (N)

In this course students learn the principles and practices of supervising inmates within a correctional facility. Emphasis is placed on maintaining security, enforcing rules, and ensuring accountability. Topics include inmate supervision, counts, movement control, communication, and the application of policies and procedures to maintain order and safety.

32 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Correctional Basic Recruit Academy program
