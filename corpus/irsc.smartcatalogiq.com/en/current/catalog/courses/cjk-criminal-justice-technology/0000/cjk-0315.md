# CJK 0315 Facility and Equipment (N)

This course familiarizes correctional officers with equipment, hazardous materials, sanitation, and health issues in a correctional facility. This course is part of the Correctional Academy.

8 hours

### Prerequisites

Acceptance into the Correctional Basic Recruit program
