# CJK 0402 Traffic Crash Investigations (N)

This course teaches traffic crash investigations, laws pertaining to traffic crashes, and procedures for responding to a traffic crash. This course is part of the FDLE mandated curriculum for basic Law Enforcement Academy training.

30 hours
