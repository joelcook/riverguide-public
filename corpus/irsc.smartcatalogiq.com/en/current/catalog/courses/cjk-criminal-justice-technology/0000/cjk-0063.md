# CJK 0063 Fundamentals of Patrol (N)

This course provides an overview of the law enforcement techniques and tactics that officers use while on patrol. The course focuses on the use of communications equipment, community oriented policing, officer safety, and basic instruction on responding to calls and making arrest. This course is part of the FDLE Mandated curriculum for the basic Law Enforcement Academy.

40 hours
