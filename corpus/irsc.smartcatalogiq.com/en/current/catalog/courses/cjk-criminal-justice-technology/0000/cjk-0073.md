# CJK 0073 Crimes Involving Property and Society (N)

This course teaches students to conduct an initial investigation of crimes against property, economic crimes, animal cruelty and crimes against society. This course is included in the FDLE mandated curriculum for basic Law Enforcement Academy training.

12 hours
