# ZOO 3733L Human Anatomy Laboratory

In this course, students participate in the laboratory component of Human Anatomy lecture [ZOO 3733](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/zoo-biology/3000/zoo-3733), which focuses on visual anatomical identification through dissections, models, and a reverse dissection technique using printed anatomical images placed on an adult-sized skeleton. Using a regional approach, students examine the anatomical details of the head and neck, back and spinal cord, thorax, abdomen, pelvis and perineum, as well as the upper and lower limbs.

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[CHM 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210), [CHM 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/chm-chemistry/2000/chm-2210l), [BSC 2011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011), [BSC 2011L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/bsc-biology/2000/bsc-2011l), all with "C" or higher
