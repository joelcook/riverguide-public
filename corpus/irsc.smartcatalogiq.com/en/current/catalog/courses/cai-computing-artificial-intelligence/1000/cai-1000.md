# CAI 1000 Overview of Artificial Intelligence

In this course, students explore a conceptual survey of Artificial Intelligence. This includes an overview of Artificial Intelligence (AI), approaching the concept from its origins to expectations for the future.  Data techniques, AI applications, ethics, and philosophical issues are covered. No high-level math or programming is required. The students will apply AI tools to solve real-world problems.

1 credits
