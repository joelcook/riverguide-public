# CAI 2001 Artificial Intelligence for All

In this course, students explore Artificial Intelligence (AI). Concepts include current and future applications and ethical implications. Students will apply AI tools to solve real-world problems through hands-on projects. Students will develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills while learning how AI is shaping industries such as healthcare, finance, entertainment, and others.

3 credits
