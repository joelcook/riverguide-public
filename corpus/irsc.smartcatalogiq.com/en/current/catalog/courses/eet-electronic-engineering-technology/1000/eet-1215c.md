# EET 1215C Introduction to Electronics

In this course, students learn the basic principles of proper troubleshooting techniques and the repair of electronic equipment. Students review direct current (DC) and alternating current (AC) theory, diodes, transistors, op-amps, and logic circuits. Students review the function and operation of electronic test equipment as they apply to the troubleshooting process. Students create or repair a variety of circuits and prepare a report detailing their troubleshooting experience. The report may include schematic capture, LTspice simulation software (LTSPICE), breadboard photos, breadboard performance verification, solder board floor planning, soldering the complete circuit, and final performance verification.

3 credits
