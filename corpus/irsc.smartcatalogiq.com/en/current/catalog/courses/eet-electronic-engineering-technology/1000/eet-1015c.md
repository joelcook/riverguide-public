# EET 1015C DC Circuits

In this course, students learn the fundamental theories of DC circuits, with emphasis on circuit operation and troubleshooting. Topics covered are electronic components, Ohm's law, Power law, series DC circuits, parallel DC circuits, electromagnetic devices, and DC test instruments. Students learn how to measure resistance, voltage, and current in a DC circuit using a digital multimeter.

3 credits
