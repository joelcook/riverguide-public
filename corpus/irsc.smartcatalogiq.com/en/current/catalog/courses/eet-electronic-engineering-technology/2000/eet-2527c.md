# EET 2527C Motor Starters, Controllers, and Breakers

In this course, students learn operating principles, troubleshooting, repair, and maintenance of switch gear, motor control centers, breaker panel power, control, instrument cable, raceways, protective devices, and grounding as related to the generating station. Hands-on laboratory exercises reinforce each major concept studied.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EET 1025C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/1000/eet-1025c) with grade of 'C' or higher
