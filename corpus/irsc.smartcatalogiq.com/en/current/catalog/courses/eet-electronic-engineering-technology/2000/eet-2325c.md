# EET 2325C Telecommunication Circuits I

In this course, students learn principles of electronic communication, modulation and demodulation methods, radio transmitter and receiver theory and circuits, and multiplexing and de-multiplexing schemes in this course. Students learn about AM and FM modulation as well as various types of digital modulation techniques. The practical portion consists of building a simple radio receiver.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EET 2141C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/2000/eet-2141c) with grade of 'C' or higher
