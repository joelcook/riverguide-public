# EET 2141C Electronic Devices I

In this course, students learn how to apply electronic principles to analog circuits and systems, including semiconductor diodes, fundamental theory of transistors and other solid-state devices, analysis of amplifiers, and other applications using sinusoidal waves. Students also practice safety procedures required in an electronics laboratory and with electronic circuits and systems.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EET 1025C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/1000/eet-1025c) with grade of 'C' or higher
