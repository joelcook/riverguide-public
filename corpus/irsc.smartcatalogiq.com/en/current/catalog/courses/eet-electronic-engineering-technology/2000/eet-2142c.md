# EET 2142C Electronic Devices II

In this course, students learn how to apply electronic principles to analog circuits and systems, including operational amplifiers, differential amplifiers, oscillators, control devices, and other integrated circuits using sinusoidal waves. Students learn the fundamental principles of modulation and wireless data techniques on the basic level.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[EET 2141C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/eet-electronic-engineering-technology/2000/eet-2141c) with grade of 'C' or higher
