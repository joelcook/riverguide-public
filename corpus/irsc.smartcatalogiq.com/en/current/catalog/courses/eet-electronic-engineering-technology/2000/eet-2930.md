# EET 2930 Special Topics in Electronics Engineering

In this course, students learn a variety of technical and organizational skills of the electrical power industry that are required as different seasonal needs emerge. Learning outcomes are customized to meet electronic engineering professionals' special technical training needs. The specific competencies may come from but are not limited to circuit analysis, analog or digital electronics, RF circuits, lasers and photonics, biomedical instrumentation, solar technology, computer architecture, and networking.

.5 - 3 credits
