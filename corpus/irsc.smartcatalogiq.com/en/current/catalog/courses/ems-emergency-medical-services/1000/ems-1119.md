# EMS 1119 Emergency Medical Technician

In this course, students acquire basic life support theory and skills required to recognize, assess, plan, and provide basic management of acutely ill and injured patients. Students participate in a combination of classroom discussions, and scenario-based applications.

9 credits

### Corequisites

[EMS 1119L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/1000/ems-1119l), [EMS 1431](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/1000/ems-1431)
