# EMS 1119L Emergency Medical Technician Lab

In this course, students acquirebasic life support theory and skills required to recognize, assess, plan, and provide basic management of acutely ill and injured patients. Students participate in a combination of classroom discussions, and scenario-based applications.

Requires acceptance to the EMT program. Students must maintain EMT program eligibility throughout the course.

2 credits

### Corequisites

[EMS 1119](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/1000/ems-1119), [EMS 1431](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/1000/ems-1431)
