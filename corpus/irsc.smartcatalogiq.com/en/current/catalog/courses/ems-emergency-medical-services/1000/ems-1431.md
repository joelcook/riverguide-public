# EMS 1431 EMT Clinical/Field Experience

This course reinforces EMT theory and skills as students participate as members of the emergency medical services (EMS) team in both the hospital and prehospital settings during clinical rotations in emergency departments and onboard advanced life-support (ALS) ambulances.

Requires admission to the EMT program. Student must maintain EMT program eligibility throughout the course.

1 credit

### Corequisites

[EMS 1119](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/1000/ems-1119), [EMS 1119L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/1000/ems-1119l)
