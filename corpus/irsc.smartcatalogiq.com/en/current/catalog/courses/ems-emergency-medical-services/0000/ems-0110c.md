# EMS 0110C Emergency Medical Technician

This course provides instruction in basic life support emphasizing the theory and skills required to recognize, assess, plan, and provide basic management of acutely ill and injured patients. Students participate in a combination of classroom discussions, laboratory skills, and scenario-based applications. In addition, students participate as members of the emergency medical services (EMS) team in both the hospital and prehospital settings during clinical rotations in emergency departments and onboard advanced life support (ALS) ambulances.

300 hours
