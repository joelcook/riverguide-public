# EMS 2659 Paramedic III

In this capstone course, students participate as team leaders onboard an advanced life support (ALS) ambulance under the direct supervision of a single preceptor. Additionally, this course utilizes classroom lectures, lab exercises and scenario-based practice to review and reinforce the application of all prior knowledge and skills obtained through the program.

6 credits

### Prerequisites

[EMS 2602C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/2000/ems-2602c), [EMS 2665](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/2000/ems-2665) with grade of C or higher
