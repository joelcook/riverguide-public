# EMS 2602C Paramedic II

In this course, students explore introductory-level instruction in advanced life support emphasizing the theory and skills required to recognize, assess, plan, and comprehensively manage acutely ill or injured patients. Students participate in a combination of classroom discussions and scenario-based skills lab practice and application as well as physical conditioning. Areas of focus include: Cardiology, Neurology, Medical Emergencies, Obstetrics, Pediatrics, and Electrophysiology.

12 credits

### Prerequisites

[EMS 2601C](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/2000/ems-2601c), [EMS 2664](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/ems-emergency-medical-services/2000/ems-2664) with grade of C or higher
