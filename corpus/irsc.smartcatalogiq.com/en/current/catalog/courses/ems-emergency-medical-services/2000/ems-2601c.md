# EMS 2601C Paramedic I

In this course, students explore introductory-level instruction in advanced life support emphasizing the theory and skills required to recognize, assess, plan, and comprehensively manage acutely ill or injured patients. Students participate in a combination of classroom discussions and scenario-based skills lab practice and application as well as physical conditioning.

12 credits

### Corequisites
