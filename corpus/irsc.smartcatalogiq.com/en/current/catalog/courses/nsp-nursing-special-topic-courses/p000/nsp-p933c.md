# NSP P933C Intravenous Therapy (N)

In this course, students safely initiate, manage, and discontinue intravenous therapy across the lifespan. Emphasizing evidence-based practice, patient-centered care, and professional accountability, the student develops competency in IV techniques, complication management, and safe clinical decision-making. The student demonstrates effective communication, collaborate within interdisciplinary teams, and adhere to legal, ethical, cultural, and regulatory standards in diverse healthcare settings.
