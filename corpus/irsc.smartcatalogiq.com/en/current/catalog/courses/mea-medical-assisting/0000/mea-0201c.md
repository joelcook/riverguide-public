# MEA 0201C Medical Assisting Clinical Procedures II (N)

In this course, students learn first aid, proper bandaging techniques, and exam procedures and treatments. Students learn to provide assistance and set up for minor surgery including identification, recognition, and care of needed instrumentation; autoclaving and sterilization, sterile fielding and removal of sutures/staples/cast. Students learn disease processes, infectious diseases, disorders, neoplasms, and congenital diseases.  Students demonstrate specialty procedures used in pulmonology, neurology, urology, pediatrics, orthopedics, ophthalmology, and otolaryngology with simulated patients. Students also explore different types of radiological imaging.

130 hours
