# MEA 0334C Medical Office Procedures (N)

In this course, students explore the basic skills necessary for the operations of a medical clinic. Students examine medical insurance, diagnostic and procedural coding, banking procedures, billing and collection procedures, payroll procedures, as well as medical transcription. Students analyze third-party guidelines for reimbursement and comprehend the legal concepts affecting billing and medical records.

100 hours
