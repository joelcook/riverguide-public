# DSC 3215 Emergency Planning

In this course students learn the core principles of emergency planning using FEMA’s Comprehensive Preparedness Guide (CPG) 101 as the primary text. Students have opportunities to discuss, compare, and evaluate components of a comprehensive emergency operations plan.

3 credits
