# SYG 2010 Social Problems

In this course, studentsexamine the causes and proposed solutions to contemporary social problems such as poverty, the economy, alienation, delinquency and crime, family changes, minority groups, war, health, aging, education, and population growth.  Students will engage in a critical analysis of the nature and social consequences of these problems.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

Student must score into college-level English and reading on placement test.
