# CJL 1000 Fundamentals of Law

In this course, students develop a broad understanding of Civil, Criminal, and Constitution Laws and their application to factual situations. The students are exposed to an overview of how the judicial system works, the principles of precedent, statutory interpretation, and appellate procedure.This course provides a basic introduction to law and the legal system designed to provide a broad understanding of Civil, Criminal, and Constitution Laws and their application to factual situations. The students are exposed to an overview of how the judicial system works, the principle of precedent, statutory interpretation, and appellate procedure.

3 credits
