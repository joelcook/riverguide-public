# CJL 3010 Legal Aspects of Policing

Students taking this course are provided a critical examination of conduct and ethics of criminal justice supervisors, managers, and leaders that may give rise to civil liability. The course also provides a critical examination of decision-making and human relations within criminal justice agencies.

3 credits
