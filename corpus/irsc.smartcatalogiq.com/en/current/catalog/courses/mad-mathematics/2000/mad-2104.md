# MAD 2104 Discrete Mathematics

Students learn sets, functions, relations, combinatory, prepositional logic, graphs and trees, and applications in this course.

Gordon Rule course - must achieve a grade of "C" or higher for the A.A. and A.S. Degree.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[MAC 1105](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/mac-mathematics/1000/mac-1105) with a grade of "C" or higher.
