# PHT 2210L Principles of Practice III Lab

In this course student practice interventions including the application of thermal, mechanical, electromagnetic, and other energies in physical therapy as presented in [PHT 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2210).

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHT 1080](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1080), [PHT 1080L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1080l), [PHT 1132](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1132), [PHT 1132L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1132l) with "C" or higher

### Corequisites

[PHT 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2210)

Lab fee $80.
