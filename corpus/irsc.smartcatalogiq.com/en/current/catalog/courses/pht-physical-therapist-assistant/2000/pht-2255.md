# PHT 2255 Neuromuscular PT

In this course, students review the foundations and interventions for patients with neuromuscular disorders. Students discuss therapeutic exercise as it applies to the neurological patient.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHT 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2210), [PHT 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2210l) all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PHT 2810](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2810), [PHT 2255L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255l)
