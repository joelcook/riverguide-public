# PHT 2820 PTA Clinical II

In this course, students participate in the second and final clinical education experience. Each student performs advanced skills in critical thinking through the application, adaptation, and modification of interventions in direct patient care under the supervision of a clinical instructor. Students master documentation and communication skills needed to demonstrate entry level skills of the physical therapist assistant.

5 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHT 2255](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255), [PHT 2255L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255l), [PHT 2810](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2810)
