# PHT 2810 PTA Clinic I

In this course, students participate in their first assignment in clinical education. Students perform intermediate level physical therapy treatment techniques under the direction and supervision of a clinical instructor. Students learn to apply, adapt, modify, and adjust interventions in the clinical setting. Further, students develop documentation and communication skills through interactions with patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals.

5 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHT 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2210), [PHT 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2210l), all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PHT 2255](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255), [PHT 2255L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255l)
