# PHT 2255L Neuromuscular PT Lab

In this course, students practice interventions for patients with neuromuscular disorders pertaining to activities of daily living, functional activities and therapeutic exercise as presented in [PHT 2255](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255).

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHT 2210](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2210), [PHT 2210L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2210l), all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PHT 2255](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255), [PHT 2810](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2810)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $80.
