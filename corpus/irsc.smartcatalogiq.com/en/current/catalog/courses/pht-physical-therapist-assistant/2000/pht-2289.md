# PHT 2289 Cardiopulmonary & Systemic Physical Therapy

In this course, students acquaint themselves with pathologies and interventions for the cardiopulmonary, lymphatic and circulatory systems. Students discuss prosthetics and limb management, and exercise as it relates to these pathologies.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHT 2255](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255), [PHT 2255L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2255l) [PHT 2810](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2810), all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PHT 2289L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2289l), [PHT 2820](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/2000/pht-2820)
