# PHT 1213L Foundations of Therapeutic Exercise Lab

In this course, students practice the application and techniques of the principles of therapeutic exercise including principles of strength and endurance training, flexibility, balance and aquatic as presented in [PHT 1213](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1213).

2 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHT 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1000), [PHT 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020), [PHT 1020L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020l), [PHT 1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121), [PHT 1121L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121l) all with grade of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PHT 1080](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1080), [PHT 1080L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1080l), [PHT 1132](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1132), [PHT 1132L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1132l), [PHT 1213](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1213)
