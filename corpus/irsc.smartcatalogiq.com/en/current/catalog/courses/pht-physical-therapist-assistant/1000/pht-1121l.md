# PHT 1121L Functional Anatomy and Kinesiology Lab

In this course, students practice patient skills including palpation, goniometry, muscle testing, muscle length testing, gait analysis, and posture assessment as presented in [PHT1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121).

2 credits

### Prerequisites

Admission to PTA program

### Corequisites

[PHT 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1000), [PHT 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020), [PHT 1020L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020l), [PHT 1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $80.
