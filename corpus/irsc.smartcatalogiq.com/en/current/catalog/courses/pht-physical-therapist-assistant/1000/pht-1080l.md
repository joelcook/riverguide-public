# PHT 1080L Principles of Practice II Lab

In this course, students practice the acute care setting interventions, integumentary assessment, wound care management, and pediatric population as presented in [PHT 1080](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1080).

1 credit

### Prerequisites

[PHT 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1000), [PHT 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020), [PHT 1020L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020l), [PHT 1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121), [PHT 1121L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121l) all with grde of C or higher

### Corequisites

[PHT 1080](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1080), [PHT 1132](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1132), [PHT 1132L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1132l), [PHT 1213](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1213), [PHT 1213L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1213l)
