# PHT 1020L Principles of Practice I Lab

In this course, students practice patient skills including transfers, assistive devices, gait training, and vital signs as presented in [PHT1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020).

2 credits

### Prerequisites

Admission to PTA program

### Corequisites

[PHT 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1000), [PHT 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020), [PHT 1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121), [PHT 1121L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121l)

### Course Fee

Lab fee $80. Insurance fee $26.78.
