# PHT 1213 Foundations of Therapeutic Exercise

In this course, students review basic concepts of mobility and exercise for prevention of injury and restoration of function. Students explore principles of strength and endurance training, flexibility, balance and aquatics. Students establish a foundation in therapeutic exercises.

3 credits

### Prerequisites

[PHT 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1000), [PHT 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020), [PHT 1020L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020l), [PHT 1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121), [PHT 1121L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121l) all with grade of C or higher
