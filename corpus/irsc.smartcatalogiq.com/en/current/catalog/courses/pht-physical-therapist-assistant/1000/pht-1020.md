# PHT 1020 Principles of Practice I

In this course, students establish a foundation in patient interaction skills including written and oral communication skills as it applies to patient care. Students develop an understanding of concepts such as transfers, assistive devices, gait training, vital signs, basic patient handling techniques, documentation, and the role of the PTA in healthcare.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

Admission to PTA program

### Corequisites

[PHT 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1000), [PHT 1020L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020l), [PHT 1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121), [PHT 1121L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121l)
