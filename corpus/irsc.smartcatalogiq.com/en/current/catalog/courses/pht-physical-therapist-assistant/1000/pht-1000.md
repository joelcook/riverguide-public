# PHT 1000 Introduction to Physical Therapy

In this course, students explore the physical therapy profession with emphasis on the role and responsibilities of the physical therapist assistant in patient/client management. Students familiarize themselves with the principles of professionalism, ethical conduct, medical terminology, cultural diversity and communication skills specific to the physical therapy profession.

2 credits

### Prerequisites

Admission to the PTA program

### Corequisites

[PHT 1020](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020), [PHT 1020L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1020l), [PHT 1121](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121), [PHT 1121L](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/pht-physical-therapist-assistant/1000/pht-1121l)
