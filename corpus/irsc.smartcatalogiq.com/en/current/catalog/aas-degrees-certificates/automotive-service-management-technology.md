# Automotive Service Management Technology

******68 Credits******

In the Automotive Service Technology Program students receive a thorough foundation in all aspects of automotive services and maintenance. IRSC’s program is certified by both the National Automotive Technicians Education Foundation (NATEF) and the National Institute of Automotive Service Excellence (ASE) in all eight categories, and provides essential training in both the theory and service of today’s automotive systems and components.
