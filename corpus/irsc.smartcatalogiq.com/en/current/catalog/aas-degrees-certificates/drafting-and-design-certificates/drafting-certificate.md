# Drafting Certificate

**24 Credits**

The Drafting Certificate offers outstanding preparation for a career as a superior CAD technician. Students are trained in the advanced operations in AutoCAD’s three major disciplines as well as technical drawing, dimensioning, sketching, and advanced 3D modeling techniques.
