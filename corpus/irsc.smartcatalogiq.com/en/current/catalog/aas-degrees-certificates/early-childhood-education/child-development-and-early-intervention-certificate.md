# Child Development and Early Intervention Certificate

**36 Credits**

Students may enhance their career success by first attaining a Certificate in Child Development Early Intervention by completing 36 credits from the Major Field Required and Major Field Electives section of the Early Childhood Education degree. Attaining the A.S. Degree is then simply a matter of completing the General Education required courses and the remaining 12 credits of major field and elective courses.
