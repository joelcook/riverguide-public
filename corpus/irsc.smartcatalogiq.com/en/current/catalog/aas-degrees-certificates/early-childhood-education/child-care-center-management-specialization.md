# Child Care Center Management Specialization

**12 credits**

The purpose of this program is to prepare students as child care center administrators with the knowledge and skills to effectively manage a quality childcare program or to provide supplementary training for persons previously or currently employed in these occupations.

Students must have completed the Preschool Specialization program to enroll in the Child Care Center Management Specialization program.
