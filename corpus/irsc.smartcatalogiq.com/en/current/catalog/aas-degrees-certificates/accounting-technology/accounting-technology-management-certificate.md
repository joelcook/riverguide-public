# Accounting Technology Management Certificate

**27 Credits**

Students may enhance their career success by first attaining a certificate in Accounting Applications. This certificate prepares the student for an entry level position in accounting. This may include performing accounting tasks in a variety of areas such as bookkeeping, payroll, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and tax preparation. Students are also encouraged to complete [APA 1152](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/apa-accounting-technology/1000/apa-1152) QuickBooks to be prepared for these positions. Students then have the option to complete the required General Education courses to earn the two-year [A.S. Degree](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/aas-degrees-certificates/accounting-technology), and possibly continue in the program to earn the four-year [B.S. Degree](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/bachelor-degree-programs/bachelors-degree-program-in-accounting).
