# Entrepreneurship Certificate

**12 credits**

The purpose of this program is to teach students the fundamentals of starting and operating a business venture while presenting entrepreneurship as a viable career option. Coursework covers opportunity recognition, business planning, cash flow and financial management, market research, e-commerce, entrepreneurship, and the overall structure of business.
