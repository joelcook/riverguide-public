# Digital Media Technology

**60 Credits**

This program prepares students for careers in the rapidly emerging digital media, modeling, simulation, and gaming industries. Specializations within this degree include computer graphic design, animation, modeling, and entry-level game development. This degree requires the integration of art principles, design concepts, communication skills, and computer fluency to enter the field of digital media technology as a technical specialist. Students obtain a solid foundation in digital media through a core of required courses and continue their education and training in their preferred area of specialization. This degree can also be a basis for the B.A.S. in Digital Media at Indian River State College.
