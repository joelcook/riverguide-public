# Landscape and Horticulture Technician Certificate - 60240

**30 Credits**

Students completing the Landscape and Horticulture Technicians Certificate will have gained an advanced knowledge of common horticultural practices as well as the opportunity to specialize in retail nursery operations, landscape maintenance practices or landscape design principles.

## Degree Requirements

### Major Field Required Courses - 15 credits

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [HOS 1010](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/hos-agriculture-production-technology/1000/hos-1010) | Fundamentals of Horticulture | 3 credits |
| [LDE 1000](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/lde-landscape-technology/1000/gco-1300) | Principles of Landscape Design | 3 credits |
| [ORH 1510](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/orh-landscape-and-horticulture/1000/orh-1510) | Plant Identification | 3 credits |
| [SWS 2104](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/sws-agriculture-production-technology/2000/sws-2104) | Soils and Fertilizers | 3 credits |
| [GEB 1011](https://irsc.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/courses/geb-business-administration-and-management/1000/geb-1011) | Introduction to Business | 3 credits |

Any IPM, HOS, LDE, ORH, PMA, SWS courses
