# Marketing Operations Certificate

**30 credits**

Students may enhance their career success by first attaining a Certificate in Marketing Operations by completing 30 credits from the Major Field Required courses and Major Field Electives within the degree. Attaining the A.S. Degree is then simply a matter of completing the General Education required courses and the remaining Major Field Required and Elective courses.
