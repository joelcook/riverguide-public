# Social and Human Services

**60 Credits**

This program prepares beginning Human Services Workers for careers in a wide variety of human services settings including children’s service agencies, state attorneys’ offices, substance abuse facilities, domestic abuse shelters, juvenile intervention programs, social service agencies, and mental health facilities. Students learn techniques and strategies to assist clients in identifying problematic behavior and promoting positive personal growth. Students will acquire both theoretical as well as practical expertise in the Human Services field.
