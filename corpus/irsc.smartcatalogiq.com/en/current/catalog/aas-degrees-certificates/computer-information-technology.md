# Computer Information Technology

**60 Credits**

This degree provides excellent preparation for a career in computer information technology including but not limited to: applications specialist, programmer, network specialist, and Internet developer. The curriculum provides the conceptual and technological skills required to analyze business situations and to design and develop computer systems and applications.
