# Digital Media Support Certificate

**15 Credits**

The Digital Media Support Certificate offers basic preparation for a career as a Digital Media Support Technician or developer. Students are trained in video creation and 2D/3D animation production techniques as well as basic graphic design, photo enhancement, illustrations, layout, and non-linear video editing techniques.
