# Accounting Technology

**60 Credits**

This degree program prepares graduates for intermediate-level accounting positions within the wide range of industries prominent on the Research Coast. While providing a strong theoretical foundation, this program emphasizes the development of marketable skills required to succeed in today’s highly competitive business world. Traditional classroom instruction is supplemented by “hands-on” micro-computer programming experience to mirror today’s accounting services climate.
