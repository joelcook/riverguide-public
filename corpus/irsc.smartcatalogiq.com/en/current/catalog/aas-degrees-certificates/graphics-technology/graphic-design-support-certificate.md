# Graphic Design Support Certificate

**15 Credits**

This Graphic Design Support Certificate offers basic preparation for a career as an Assistant Graphic Designer. Students are trained in image editing and graphic layout, basic graphic design, photo enhancement, illustrations and publishing techniques.
