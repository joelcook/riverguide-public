# Graphic Design Production Certificate

**24 Credits**

This Graphic Design Production Certificate offers advanced preparation for a career as a Graphic Production Artist or assistant. Students are trained in graphic design techniques, image editing and enhancement, layout, Web design, typography, illustrations and publishing techniques.
