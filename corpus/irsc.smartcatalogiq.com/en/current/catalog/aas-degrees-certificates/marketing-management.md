# Marketing Management

**60 Credits**

The Associate in Science (A.S.), Marketing Management degree program prepares you to pursue a career in a variety of marketing related fields.

No matter who you are or what you do, at some point you may need to market yourself or your ideas to others, whether it is to impress a potential employer, attract consumers to your business or work with companies to develop or enhance a marketing strategy.

Learners in this program will study the concepts used by businesses to create, communicate, and deliver value with the power of branding. Additionally, learners will develop the business skills necessary to compete in the global marketplace.
