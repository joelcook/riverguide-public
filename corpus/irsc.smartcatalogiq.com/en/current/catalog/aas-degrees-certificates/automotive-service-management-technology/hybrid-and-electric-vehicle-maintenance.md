# Hybrid and Electric Vehicle Maintenance

**24 credits**

The Automotive Hybrid and Electric Vehicles Technician certificate equips students with the skills to become proficient troubleshooters for drivability issues in hybrid and electric vehicles. Through the program, students gain expertise in identifying a range of issues, from battery management to power-train malfunctions to ignition failures, employing cutting-edge computer diagnostic equipment. Additionally, students acquire the ability to diagnose systems in vehicles equipped with electric drive systems.
