# Pruitt Campus Maps

The IRSC Pruitt Campus is located just east of I-95 at exit 121.  Turn left (heading east) on St. Lucie West Blvd. and then turn left (heading north) on California Blvd.  Proceed past the roundabout, continue on California Blvd. and the Pruitt Campus will be on the left.
