# Dixon Hendry Campus Okeechobee

Located in the heart of Florida, the Dixon Hendry Campus offers a welcoming, close-knit environment providing flexible scheduling options - including daytime and evening - for students pursuing Associate in Arts and Associate in Science/Applied Science degrees. Career and technical programs are designed to meet the local community's needs, offering customized training for local businesses.

The campus is home to an academic-rich Learning Commons, a one-stop hub where students can access the library, computer resources, Bloomberg Terminal where students can access global financial information, quiet study areas, the testing center, and comprehensive tutoring support - all designed to promote academic success in a comfortable and collaborative environment. Our Student Services Office, located in building B, is a full-service department where students can access support in advising, financial aid, cashiering, and career services - all in one convenient location.

The Williamson Conference and Education Center serves both students and Okeechobee businesses for conferences, courses, seminars, and various community events.

More than just a place to learn, the campus serves as a vibrant hub for collaboration, personal growth, and lifelong learning. With supportive faculty and staff, and programs designed with the community in mind, Dixon Hendry continues to empower students and strengthen ihe region it proudly serves.
