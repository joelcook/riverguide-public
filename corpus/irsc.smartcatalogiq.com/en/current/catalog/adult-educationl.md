# Adult Education

Indian River State College offers GED® Preparation to students interested in studying for the GED® exam through individualized and classroom support. The GED® exam is administered on the computer, and students must have strong academic skills and knowledge in the areas of writing, math, reading, social studies, science and computer technology in order to pass the exam.

Part of the nationally recognized Adult Education Career Pathway model, the program's purpose is to assist all students with transition services to place them into a postsecondary program, achieving a college credential and the knowledge and skill sets necessary to succeed in the 21st century.
