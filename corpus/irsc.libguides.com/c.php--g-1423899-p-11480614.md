# Student Help Desk: How to Clear Cache & Cookies

Get help with password resets and other troubleshooting technical issues with your College account.

## How to Clear Cache & Cookies

### **Use this guide to Clear Cache and Cookies based on your personal device**

### **Google Chrome (Desktop)**

1. Click the **three dots** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **History** > **History**.
3. Click **Clear browsing data** on the left.
4. Choose **All time**, check **Cookies** and **Cached images**, then click **Delete data**.

**Safari (macOS)**

1. Click **Safari** in the top menu bar and choose **Settings** (or Preferences).
2. Click the **Privacy** tab.
3. Click **Manage Website Data** and then **Remove All**.

**Microsoft Edge (Desktop)**

1. Click the **three dots** in the top-right corner.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Privacy, search, and services**.
3. Under **Clear browsing data**, click **Choose what to clear**.
4. Select **All time** and check **Cookies** and **Cached images**, then click **Clear now**.

**Mobile Browsers**

- **Android (Chrome):** Tap the three dots > **History** > **Clear browsing data** > Select **All time** > Check "Cookies" and "Cached images" > **Delete data**.
- **iOS (Safari):** Open **Settings** app > **Safari** > **Clear History and Website Data**.
